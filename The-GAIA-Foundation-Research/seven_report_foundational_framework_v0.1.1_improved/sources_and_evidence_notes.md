## Source-use constraints

- Religious and philosophical sources document teachings or arguments; they do not alone establish empirical truth.
- Secondary educational sources provide orientation; primary research and institutional sources should be preferred for high-consequence claims.
- The consciousness-study record was reviewed at publication-record/abstract/summary level from the 2025 Nature paper; not as a completed audit of full methods, supplements, data, and code.
- All claims remain preliminary; independent verification is required.
