## Core distinction
Embodiment is not a single claim. Bodily systems can enable cognition, modulate it, constitute parts of cognitive activity, or support organismic autonomy. These claims carry different evidential burdens. The first two have broad empirical support; stronger constitutive and autopoietic claims remain theoretical positions rather than automatic consequences of showing bodily influence.

## Integrative Embodied Learning Framework
Treat IELF as a proposed architecture with interacting layers: (1) sensorimotor/parietal-premotor perception–action integration; (2) cerebellar prediction and error-driven adaptation; (3) basal-ganglia/dopaminergic reward-sensitive updating; and (4) hippocampal, interoceptive, and affective context. Predictive processing, reinforcement learning, and Hebbian plasticity can describe complementary processes but are not interchangeable explanations.

A 2026 computational study distinguishes likelihood-precision manipulation from preference reweighting in a foraging gridworld. Precision sharpened interoceptive posteriors and improved survival; preference reweighting altered the distribution of bodily states encountered during learning. Further layouts did not establish the predicted preference advantage on difficult layouts, although both interventions improved average survival and interacted positively. This is a bounded model result, not direct proof of human mechanisms. https://stjohngrimbly.com/iwai2026/

## Whole-cell model constraint
Thornburg et al. model the approximately 100-minute cell cycle of JCVI-syn3A, integrating genetic information processes, metabolism, growth, and division in space and time. The model recovers multiple experimental measures and predicts cell-to-cell heterogeneity. It is a strong example of bottom-up biological integration, but it is not a model of a human body, mind, or consciousness. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.02.009

## Autopoietic enactivism
Mendes argues that autopoietic/autonomist enactivism is the strand of 4E cognition that most directly challenges mainstream cognitivist assumptions. This is a philosophical analysis, not a consensus verdict on embodiment research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11097-025-10131-1

## Validation gates
Any implementation should preregister separate measures for interoceptive discrimination, preference, action selection, regulation/survival, and learned-model coverage; use ablations and out-of-distribution tests; justify parameters empirically; and distinguish simulation fit from biological validity and explanatory adequacy.

## Boundaries
A whole-cell model does not explain human cognition by itself. Bodily influence does not settle the ontology of cognition. A computational model is not evidence of subjective experience.
