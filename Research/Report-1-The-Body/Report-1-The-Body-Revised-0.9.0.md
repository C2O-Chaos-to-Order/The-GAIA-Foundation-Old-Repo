## Core distinction
Embodiment is not a single claim. Bodily systems can enable cognition, modulate it, constitute parts of cognitive activity, or support organismic autonomy. These claims carry different evidential burdens. The first two have broad empirical support; stronger constitutive and autopoietic claims remain theoretical positions rather than automatic consequences of showing bodily influence.

## Integrative Embodied Learning Framework
Treat IELF as a proposed architecture with interacting layers: (1) sensorimotor/parietal-premotor perception–action integration; (2) cerebellar prediction and error-driven adaptation; (3) basal-ganglia/dopaminergic reward-sensitive updating; and (4) hippocampal, interoceptive, and affective context. Predictive processing, reinforcement learning, and Hebbian plasticity can describe complementary processes but are not interchangeable explanations.

A 2026 computational study distinguishes likelihood-precision manipulation from preference reweighting in a foraging gridworld. Precision sharpened interoceptive posteriors and improved survival; preference reweighting altered the distribution of bodily states encountered during learning. Further layouts did not establish the predicted preference advantage on difficult layouts, although both interventions improved average survival and interacted positively. This is a bounded model result, not direct proof of human mechanisms. https://stjohngrimbly.com/iwai2026/

## Whole-cell model constraint
Thornburg et al. model the approximately 100-minute cell cycle of JCVI-syn3A, integrating genetic information processes, metabolism, growth, and division in space and time. The model recovers multiple experimental measures and predicts cell-to-cell heterogeneity. It is a strong example of bottom-up biological integration, but it is not a model of a human body, mind, or consciousness. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.02.009

## Autopoietic enactivism
Mendes argues that autopoietic/autonomist enactivism is the strand of 4E cognition that most directly challenges mainstream cognitivist assumptions. This is a philosophical analysis, not a consensus verdict on embodiment research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11097-025-10131-1

## Validation gates
Any implementation should preregister separate measures for interoceptive discrimination, preference, action selection, regulation/survival, and learned-model coverage; use ablations and out-of-distribution tests; justify parameters empirically; and distinguish simulation fit from biological validity and explanatory adequacy.

## Boundaries
A whole-cell model does not explain human cognition by itself. Bodily influence does not settle the ontology of cognition. A computational model is not evidence of subjective experience.

## Purpose

This report contributes **Embodiment, physiology, interoception, and biological constraints** to the Transformative Intelligence governance architecture. The five phases now function as governance operations:

1. **Divergence — generation:** preserve relevant perspectives and record claim type, evidence, provenance, and uncertainty.
2. **Insurgence — contestation:** challenge core commitments using predeclared criteria; retain rival positions and document the outcome.
3. **Allegiance — stabilization:** adopt provisional working standards with review dates, anomaly triggers, and revision conditions.
4. **Convergence — integration:** connect compatible findings without silently erasing meaningful differences; record information lost or left unresolved.
5. **Ascendence — meta-evaluation:** evaluate the process and specify how conclusions reconnect to first-order evidence or accountable review.

## Report-specific governance rule

For body-related claims, distinguish measured physiological observations from interpretations about cognition, agency, or lived experience. Record method validity, context, uncertainty, and alternative explanations; embodiment does not by itself establish one theory of mind.

## Minimum claim record

For consequential claims, record the exact claim; claim type; source and provenance; method and scope; uncertainty; counterevidence and rival explanations; decision relevance; and review or revision trigger. A source supporting one claim type does not automatically support a stronger causal, normative, or metaphysical conclusion.

## Controls

| Control | Requirement | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| Generation record | Each included perspective declares its evidential basis and limits | Inclusion or exclusion lacks a traceable rationale |
| Challenge protocol | Define success and potential disconfirmation before evaluation | Criteria shift after results to protect a preferred position |
| Commitment review | Record owner, scope, review date, and anomaly triggers | A commitment persists without review or is retired arbitrarily |
| Loss ledger | Document distinctions merged, omitted, or judged incomparable | Synthesis implies equivalence while concealing material differences |
| Re-entry condition | Meta-level recommendations connect to a checkable observation or review | Meta-level conclusions cannot be affected by first-order evidence |
| Dissent and appeal | Preserve minority reports and reasoned challenges | Governance suppresses disagreement instead of adjudicating it |

## Addendum — Hypergraph Architecture Integration (v4.0.0)

**Purpose:** connect this report to the topologically self-referential architecture without replacing its body-level scope.

- **Vertices and relations:** represent anatomical/physiological claims, observations, instruments, environmental conditions, and interpretive assumptions as typed vertices. Use higher-order relations when a claim depends jointly on multiple systems, measures, and contexts; do not force every relation into isolated pairs.
- **Divergence and insurgency:** retain competing mechanistic explanations and predeclare the observation or intervention that could discriminate among them. Record measurement limitations and opacity as explicit nodes.
- **Allegiance and governance:** any commitment about body-level evidence needs provenance, scope, review conditions, and a reversible revision path. Meta-level policy cannot substitute for physiological evidence.
- **Convergence:** when connecting embodiment, interoception, emotion, cognition, and environmental exposures, record cross-domain dependencies and the information lost in synthesis. Correlated measures are not independent confirmation.
- **Meta-wisdom invariant:** preserve source lineage, instrument assumptions, uncertainty, monitoring blind spots, and a non-destructive change history.
- **Testable failure cases:** provenance lost during fusion; an instrument treated as ground truth; context omitted from a multi-system claim; a new framework silently redefining an earlier outcome.
