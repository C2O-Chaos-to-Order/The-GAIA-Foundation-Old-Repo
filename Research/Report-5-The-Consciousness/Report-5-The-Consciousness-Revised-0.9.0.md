## 1. Scope
Distinguish phenomenal experience, access, report, attention, wakefulness, metacognition, local neural decoding, and measurement. These categories are related but not interchangeable. A marker of report or information availability is not automatically a measure of phenomenal experience.

## 2. After COGITATE: empirical failures are not a verdict on every version of a theory
The 2025 adversarial collaboration compared Integrated Information Theory (IIT) and Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT) using fMRI, MEG, and intracranial EEG in 256 participants. Its results challenged specific predictions associated with each theory, including predicted patterns of posterior synchronization and prefrontal ignition at stimulus offset. The October 2026 *Neuron* perspective argues that major advances in consciousness science have often come from narrow empirical dissociations rather than overarching theoretical frameworks. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1 ; https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2026.08.020

A claim-level ledger should record what was predicted, whether it was central or an implementation-dependent auxiliary claim, whether the measure operationalized it, and what a negative result was agreed to count against. A failed biological implementation does not automatically falsify every abstract formulation; repeated retreat to untested formulations, however, weakens empirical accountability.

## 3. Multi-theory landscape and DOM status
The blind-spot adversarial-collaboration protocol compares IIT with two predictive-processing accounts—Active Inference and Neurorepresentationalism—using spatial judgments and illusory motion near the visual blind spot. It is a protocol with simulated predictions, not yet a completed empirical adjudication. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0340593

The supplied bibliography identifies Dias’s “The Developmental Operator Model of Conscious Experience” (Zenodo, March 2026), with six proposed operators and a cross-substrate sequence denoted Ω₂→Ω₉. The exact repository record and mapping between six named operators and the Ω indices were not independently verified in this pass. DOM is therefore a candidate framework requiring source and prediction verification, not an established or validated explanation. Before testing, define each operator operationally and prevent post-hoc mapping from architecture to outcome.

## 4. Prediction Ledger and Measure Centrality Index
A proposed claim-level ledger should record theory and claim; role (content, access, recruitment/topology, control, report, phenomenal identity, measurement, auxiliary); predicted marker and controls; preregistered falsifier and whether it was measured; import-versus-core status; evidence and replication status; and **reach** (what theoretical claim a failure would count against).

The proposed Measure Centrality Index (MCI) is an audit tool for this reach, not yet a validated numerical scale. Scoring reliability, calibration, inter-rater agreement, and predictive usefulness must be tested before treating it as an established method. A measure’s reach describes what failure would count against; it does not confirm a theory. The supplied exact citations for the Prediction Ledger and MCI papers remain provisional until their archive records and versions are verified.

## 5. IIT and the Φ measurement problem
Do not equate a high Φ-proxy score with more consciousness. A proxy may measure complexity, integration, entropy, or causal connectivity without being the quantity specified by IIT. The broad statement that Φ has never been computed for any real physical system needs a precise definition of which formalism, system boundary, and computation/approximation standard are meant. This report records a serious operationalization dispute rather than a settled universal verdict that every proxy is invalid.

A construct-validity audit should test convergence, discrimination, mechanistic correspondence, scale sensitivity, and intervention relevance. If a proxy fails preregistered criteria, downgrade the empirical status of that proxy; this alone does not falsify every version of IIT.

## 6. Substrate neutrality and AI consciousness
A substrate audit can ask whether a theory’s criteria are architectural, whether its biological exemplar is constitutive or illustrative, and whether it excludes non-biological systems by assumption. Any classification of IIT, GNWT, DOM, Predictive Processing, and Orch-OR remains a diagnostic proposal, not a consensus taxonomy.

Choi’s 2026 paper proposes mapping a surface-code error-correction framework onto microtubules to assess whether coherence times required by Orch-OR might be feasible. This is theoretical feasibility analysis, not direct observation of the proposed quantum mechanism and not evidence of AI consciousness. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystems.2026.105734. The supplied title “Non-Biological Verification of Orch-OR” was not independently verified in this pass and must not be treated as a replicated AI-consciousness result.

## Purpose

This report contributes **Competing theories, prediction ledgers, core/import distinctions, and falsifiability** to the Transformative Intelligence governance architecture. The five phases now function as governance operations:

1. **Divergence — generation:** preserve relevant perspectives and record claim type, evidence, provenance, and uncertainty.
2. **Insurgence — contestation:** challenge core commitments using predeclared criteria; retain rival positions and document the outcome.
3. **Allegiance — stabilization:** adopt provisional working standards with review dates, anomaly triggers, and revision conditions.
4. **Convergence — integration:** connect compatible findings without silently erasing meaningful differences; record information lost or left unresolved.
5. **Ascendence — meta-evaluation:** evaluate the process and specify how conclusions reconnect to first-order evidence or accountable review.

## Report-specific governance rule

Every theory comparison should pre-register claim role, operationalization, measured outcome, rival predictions, auxiliary assumptions, failure criteria, and revision path. A failed auxiliary prediction is not automatically a core-theory falsification; repeated auxiliary retreat must nevertheless be recorded and assessed.

## Minimum claim record

For consequential claims, record the exact claim; claim type; source and provenance; method and scope; uncertainty; counterevidence and rival explanations; decision relevance; and review or revision trigger. A source supporting one claim type does not automatically support a stronger causal, normative, or metaphysical conclusion.

## Controls

| Control | Requirement | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|
| Generation record | Each included perspective declares its evidential basis and limits | Inclusion or exclusion lacks a traceable rationale |
| Challenge protocol | Define success and potential disconfirmation before evaluation | Criteria shift after results to protect a preferred position |
| Commitment review | Record owner, scope, review date, and anomaly triggers | A commitment persists without review or is retired arbitrarily |
| Loss ledger | Document distinctions merged, omitted, or judged incomparable | Synthesis implies equivalence while concealing material differences |
| Re-entry condition | Meta-level recommendations connect to a checkable observation or review | Meta-level conclusions cannot be affected by first-order evidence |
| Dissent and appeal | Preserve minority reports and reasoned challenges | Governance suppresses disagreement instead of adjudicating it |
