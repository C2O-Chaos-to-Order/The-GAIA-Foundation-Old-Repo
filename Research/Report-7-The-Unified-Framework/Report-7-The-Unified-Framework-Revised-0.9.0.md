## Twelve simulation specifications — design only
1. **DOM cross-substrate test:** operationalize each operator before mapping; preregister failure conditions; verify the source record first.
2. **IIT proxy construct-validity audit:** test convergence, discrimination, mechanism, scale sensitivity, and intervention relevance.
3. **Prediction Ledger / Measure Centrality Index:** record claim role, core/import status, falsifier, measure, reach, and evidence status; test scoring reliability.
4. **CMG mode / length-of-day spectrum:** compare coupled angular-momentum model with observations and electromagnetic-only alternatives.
5. **Planetary active inference:** reproduce the published model before modifying it; ablate coupling and biosphere activity.
6. **Practice-specific neuroplasticity:** compare practice effects with generic attention, breathing, movement, expectancy, and social-context models; include the review correction.
7. **Soul-evidence discrimination:** formalize rival likelihoods and independently corroborated outcomes; hypothetical likelihoods are not observations.
8. **IELF arbitration:** factorially manipulate precision and preference; measure discrimination, choice, regulation, and learned-model coverage separately.
9. **Substrate audit:** apply architectural, exemplar, and exclusion criteria consistently; neutrality does not establish AI consciousness.
10. **Whole-cell constraint:** reproduce cell-cycle validation targets where data/code permit; do not generalize a minimal bacterium to human body–mind.
11. **Orch-OR versus network alternatives:** define symmetric predictions across quantum, cellular, circuit, and behavioural levels; feasibility is not direct observation.
12. **Earth-system cascades:** compare coupled and uncoupled models under plausible forcing and uncertainty; boundary transgressions do not alone validate active-inference interpretations.

Each simulation requires model specification, data/parameter provenance, code version, random seeds, preregistration, baseline, ablations, sensitivity analysis, outputs, failure logs, and independent reproduction instructions. Without these, label the work design-only or partial, never executed-and-verified.

## Regression safeguards
Reject these category errors: high Φ proxy score equals more consciousness; one adversarial verdict decides every version of a theory; a Markov blanket equals a subject; a 40–100-year oscillation equals a heartbeat; practice-specific plasticity equals spiritual truth; substrate neutrality equals evidence of AI consciousness; planetary-boundary transgression equals planetary failure of inference; high measure reach equals theory confirmation; minimal-bacterium simulation equals a human body–mind model; microtubule coherence equals consciousness; structural isomorphism equals subjective experience; and a substrate audit equals a theory of AI consciousness.
