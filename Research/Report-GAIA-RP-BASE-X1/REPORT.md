## 2. Purpose, Research Questions, and Scope

### 2.1 Primary question

Does Research Plan v1.4 specify a traceable, fail-closed baseline procedure whose key rules can be represented in an executable procedural model, and what do one research pass and one simulation pass establish about that procedure?

### 2.2 Questions

1. Are the baseline pass requirements, operating cycle, interlays, report contract, and stage-gating rules explicit enough to translate into a bounded test?
2. Does the procedural model behave as expected when required evidence is present, missing, failed, or incorrectly represented?
3. Are the Zodiac × Alchemy and Alchemy × Transformative-phase matrices kept distinct as the plan requires?
4. Which parts of the plan are established as documented requirements, which are only proposed conceptual architecture, and which remain too underdefined for a meaningful test?
5. What must be clarified or governed before the next multiplier-labelled stage can be responsibly started?

### 2.3 In scope

This report examines Research Plan v1.4's structure and selected process controls: exact baseline work accounting, the Research → Pre-Simulation Alchemy & Transformative Interlay → Simulation → Post-Simulation Alchemy & Transformative Interlay → Reporting & Verification cycle, evidence labels, matrix cardinalities, predefined acceptance conditions, and failure/gate routing. It uses public methodological sources on reproducibility, preregistration, requirements traceability, and simulation verification/validation.

### 2.4 Out of scope

No research pass in this report attempts to establish the existence or nature of a soul, spirit, consciousness as a metaphysical entity, a literal planetary or universal mind, quantum entanglement among concepts or systems, a universal transformation mechanism, or GAIA's real-world performance. No live system, physical environment, personal data, human subject, or deployed AI system was tested. No prior GAIA report is used as evidence for the truth of its own conclusions.

### 2.5 Intended decision

Determine whether this bounded baseline deliverable's process-control criteria are satisfied and record material gaps. This report does not authorize system implementation, deployment, or irreversible changes. It also does not silently replace or amend a pre-existing research plan in another repository.

## 5. Research Record and Source Review

### Research pass RP-BASE-R1

**Pass count:** 1 of 1 required.  
**Method:** One bounded, targeted methodological review conducted before the procedural simulation. Search terms covered research reproducibility and replicability, preregistration, simulation-model verification and validation, NASA requirements verification matrices, and NIST evaluation / measurement controls. Sources were selected for direct relevance to the report's process question and included official institutional guidance, a National Academies consensus report, and specialist simulation-method literature. This was not a systematic review of all research-method literature.

**Source inclusion rule:** A source was included where it directly addresses at least one of: documenting how results were obtained, making criteria explicit before interpretation, requirements traceability, simulation-model verification/validation, measurement uncertainty, or independent review. Sources are not being used to prove metaphysical propositions or the conceptual truth of GAIA's architecture.

### Sources and findings

**R1 — National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (2019), _Reproducibility and Replicability in Science_.**  
[Publication and free online chapter](https://doi.org/10.17226/25303)  
This consensus report emphasizes that the path from exploratory findings to accepted knowledge requires additional confirmation and recommends clear, specific descriptions of methods, materials, analytical choices, constraints on generalization, and uncertainty. This supports the plan's emphasis on recorded methods, limits, and reproducibility status; it does not mean that a single pass confirms a broad claim.

**R2 — Center for Open Science, “Preregistration.”**  
[Preregistration overview](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/prereg)  
This guidance explains that specifying a plan before a study helps distinguish planned from unplanned work and supports transparent interpretation. That principle supports defining success/failure criteria before interpreting the simulated outputs. The present run is a procedural software test, not a preregistered scientific experiment in a registry.

**R3 — NASA, Systems Engineering Handbook Appendix: Requirements Verification and Validation Matrices.**  
[Official NASA appendix](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/system-engineering-handbook-appendix/)  
NASA's examples map specified requirements to definitive source documents and verification approaches, and separately structure validation against stakeholder needs. This supports unique acceptance-criterion identifiers, source linkage, and criterion-by-criterion results in the GAIA report contract.

**R4 — Sargent, R. G. (2013), “Verification and validation of simulation models,” _Journal of Simulation_, 7, 12–24.**  
[DOI: 10.1057/jos.2012.20](https://doi.org/10.1057/jos.2012.20)  
The paper distinguishes conceptual-model validity, model verification, operational validity, and data validity and discusses methods for documenting and assessing simulation models. The relevance here is a key limitation: a correct execution of a small procedural model demonstrates only behavior in its defined conditions; it does not validate the conceptual framework the model is about.

**R5 — National Institute of Standards and Technology (2023), _Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)_.**  
[DOI: 10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1)  
NIST calls for documented testing and evaluation, measures of uncertainty, comparison with suitable benchmarks, and consideration of independent review and the conditions under which results generalize. This is a relevant control principle for future GAIA technical evaluations, but the present report does not evaluate a deployed AI system.

### Source-review synthesis

The sources converge on process controls rather than any particular metaphysical worldview: make the method inspectable, connect criteria to evidence, disclose uncertainty, distinguish planned work from executed work, and keep model-level results bounded by model assumptions. The plan v1.4 is directionally aligned with these practices. The source review does not establish that the five Alchemies, six phases, Heart/Core, or Zodiac structure are complete, measurable, or universally applicable.

## 6. Assumptions and Method

### 6.1 Assumptions

- The live plan file identified by the Version 1.4 commit is the plan under test for this report.
- The baseline multiplier is interpreted as one distinct research pass and one actually executed simulation pass.
- A simulated test case is a fixture inside a single run; eight fixtures do not count as eight simulation passes.
- A run can have executed successfully as a process while its modeled acceptance criteria fail. Execution status and criterion status are separate.
- The two matrices are treated as categorical analytical instruments; their counts are not evidence of model accuracy or semantic validity.
- An unexecuted simulation cannot satisfy the baseline simulation-pass requirement.

### 6.2 Method

1. Read the live Research Plan v1.4 and extracted its baseline pass requirements, mandatory workflow, matrix definitions, evidence classifications, report contract, and acceptance/gating rules.
2. Reviewed five external methodology sources using the bounded inclusion approach described in Section 5.
3. Defined an executable deterministic model for selected plan controls and prepared synthetic fixtures for pass, block, failure, and interlay cases.
4. Executed the model once, preserving its run identifier, fixture-level expected and observed results, criterion results, model version, hashes, and limitations.
5. Compared the actual output to the declared expected behavior, recorded limitations, and kept procedural findings separate from claims about real-world validity.

The executed script is provided as a companion artifact. Its SHA-256 digest is recorded in Section 8. The execution JSON is also retained. These artifacts document what ran; they do not constitute independent reproduction.

## 10. Results and Synthesis

### Result R-01 — The bounded procedural model passed its declared tests

**Status: PASS within scope.** All eight synthetic fixtures returned their expected outcomes and all 11 declared acceptance checks passed. This supports the limited conclusion that the script's encoded branches behave consistently with its expected outputs for these fixtures.

**It does not establish** that the model contains every rule in Research Plan v1.4, that the plan is optimal, or that the proposed GAIA architecture is true or safe.

### Result R-02 — The plan contains strong process safeguards

**Status: DOCUMENTED; external method alignment observed.** The plan distinguishes research from simulation design and execution, requires unique pass and run records, mandates both interlays, preserves failures and inconclusive outcomes, requires criterion-level evidence, and separates documentation completion from scientific verification. These controls align with the methodological concerns in R1–R5.

**Limit:** process controls improve inspectability; their presence alone does not prove research quality, causal validity, or success of the GAIA programme.

### Result R-03 — The conceptual architecture remains underoperationalized for empirical testing

**Status: INDETERMINATE / REQUIRES FURTHER DEFINITION.** The research plan correctly says that proposed categories are not automatically scientific facts. However, the twelve Zodiac identities and meanings are not enumerated in the plan segment tested here, and operational measures for Soul, Spirit, Heart/Core, and the phases depend on domain-specific future definitions. No matrix cells can be given empirical acceptance results until each applied dimension has an operational question, evidence requirement, applicability rule, and challenge/falsification condition.

**Recommendation:** preserve all missing definitions as unresolved; do not invent names, meanings, numerical values, or mechanisms. A categorical count is not evidence of substantive matrix coverage.

### Result R-04 — Distinct pass quality needs a sharper rule

**Status: PARTIALLY SPECIFIED.** The plan says passes must be distinct and may not be copies or relabelled activities. It should also state a practical distinctness test: each research pass must have a separately identified question or scope, method/source-selection decision, analysis record, and a clear account of what new evidence or challenge it contributes. Closely repeated work should be recorded honestly rather than given a new ID only to satisfy a multiplier.

The model's two-pass fixture uses a conservative exact-count interpretation. How genuinely supplemental exploratory work is recorded alongside the exact required multiplier bundle should be clarified before later stages so that reporting cannot either inflate pass counts or suppress legitimate extra work.

### Result R-05 — A repository-level authority relationship is unresolved

**Status: UNKNOWN / OWNER DECISION REQUIRED.** The authoritative v1.4 Research Plan is stored in [Research-for-The-GAIA-Foundation-Research](https://github.com/KyleAlexanderSteen/Research-for-The-GAIA-Foundation-Research/blob/main/The%20GAIA%20Research%20Plan/README.md). The existing [Foundation Research Plan Extension](https://github.com/KyleAlexanderSteen/The-GAIA-Foundation/blob/main/Research/RESEARCH_PLAN_EXTENSION.md) identifies itself as the governing seven-report framework, and the current Foundation Research index continues to describe that pre-existing work. The relationship between these documents is not explicitly resolved in the source material examined for this report.

This is a governance/traceability issue, not evidence that either substantive framework is true or false. No prior document was overwritten, deleted, or silently declared superseded. Before the next stage, an explicit owner-level decision should state whether the v1.4 plan supersedes the older extension, runs alongside it for a distinct scope, or requires a versioned reconciliation. Preserve both histories and state which plan governs each deliverable.

### Result R-06 — One run is not independent validation

**Status: NOT PERFORMED.** No independent reviewer or external reproduction of GAIA-RP-BASE-SIM-001 occurred. This report records an actual execution and a local artifact digest; neither is equivalent to independent verification, replication, or validation. Broader scientific hypotheses remain untested.
