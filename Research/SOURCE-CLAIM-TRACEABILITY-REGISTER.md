## Purpose and rules

This register records selected high-impact source-to-claim links discovered during the first research-wide audit. It is a starting ledger, not a certification of any report. The complete register must eventually cover every material empirical, historical, quantitative, model-based, and interpretive claim across all canonical reports and the Transformative Intelligence architecture.

**Verification labels**
- **Verified identity / abstract-level check:** source record and high-level claim located; full methods and all downstream claims may remain unaudited.
- **Targeted claim checked:** source supports the narrow claim described; this does not support stronger claims.
- **Provisional / unresolved:** exact record or claim match is not established.
- **Model or perspective:** source is theoretical, conceptual, or model-based; do not represent as empirical proof beyond its actual design.
- **Open:** further source-to-claim checking required.
