## Scope and metaphor boundary
“Life as the soul of the Earth” and “the core as its heart” are interpretive metaphors. They can express ecological interdependence and stewardship, but they are not literal findings of geophysics. This report separates physical mechanisms, Earth-system dynamics, functional descriptions such as regulation, and metaphysical interpretation.

## Millennium-scale geodynamo simulation
Aubert’s 2026 study reports a geodynamo simulation integrated over about 1,700 years of physical time, with parameters intended to match core density, rotation, convective power, and electrical conductivity. The authors report a quasi-geostrophic magneto-Archimedes-Coriolis balance and agreement between simulated dynamics and geomagnetic/geodetic observations. The study links interdecadal magneto-Coriolis waves to variations near a 60-year period, discusses convective power/Ohmic dissipation near 3 TW, and estimates lower-mantle conductance around 10^9 S for core-originated length-of-day variations. https://doi.org/10.46298/jsedi.17790

These are results from a numerical model, not direct observation of the entire core and not proof that the core is biologically or phenomenally heart-like. Reproducibility depends on equations, parameters, boundary conditions, and comparison datasets.

## Proposed core–mantle gravitational mode
Dumberry describes a proposed core–mantle gravitational (CMG) mode involving angular-momentum exchange among the fluid core, inner core, and mantle. The predicted period is about 40–100 years; the mode may have low quality factor below 1 if inner-core viscous relaxation is under ten years. The paper suggests potentially modest amplification of multidecadal length-of-day changes. https://doi.org/10.46298/jsedi.17576

This is a theoretical proposal, not a confirmed observed oscillation. It must be tested against electromagnetic, hydrodynamic, and other explanations. A spectral feature in this period range alone would not uniquely identify the mode.

## Biosphere–climate active inference
Rubin et al. present a proof-of-concept model in which a biosphere–climate system behaves as if it infers external forcing through sensory/boundary states and adjusts greenhouse forcing and albedo to maintain climate trajectories within geophysiological bounds. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-61878-9

The bounded interpretation is that some coupled regulation dynamics can be described using active-inference language. The model does not establish phenomenal experience, human-like intention, or a planetary soul. A Markov blanket is a statistical/organizational boundary in a model; it is not by itself a subject.

## Planetary boundaries
The Planetary Health Check 2026 reports seven of nine planetary boundaries transgressed, with ocean acidification newly assessed as transgressed in 2025. The seven listed are climate change, biosphere integrity, land-system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flows, novel entities, and ocean acidification. https://www.planetaryhealthcheck.org/

This supports the seriousness of coupled Earth-system pressures. It does not mean every subsystem is collapsing or that the planet has failed to “infer” correctly.

## Simulation specifications — design only
1. **CMG mode and length-of-day spectrum:** model coupled mantle/fluid-core/inner-core angular momentum with magnetic and gravitational torques; sweep physically justified parameters and compare spectra with observations and alternatives.
2. **Planetary active inference:** reproduce the published model before modifying it; define internal, sensory, active, and external states; ablate biosphere activity and coupling.
3. **Earth-system cascades:** model coupled boundary indicators with uncertainty ranges, alternative network topologies, and nonlinear thresholds; compare with historical observations and independent ensembles.
4. **Robustness:** preregister parameter ranges, uncertainty propagation, and model-selection criteria. Do not tune parameters solely to reproduce seven transgressions.

A model that produces a cascade only after arbitrary tuning is not validated by resemblance to observed patterns.

## Interpretive safeguards
- A 40–100-year mode is not a heartbeat.
- A Markov blanket is not a subject.
- Homeorhesis is not automatically phenomenal agency.
- Earth-system coupling is empirical; “Earth’s soul” remains philosophical or spiritual interpretation.

## Source-Audit Addendum — Core Dynamics, Magnetic Field, and Habitability (2026-10-23)

### Findings and corrections

1. **The geodynamo is a physical mechanism; “heart” is a metaphor.** Motion of electrically conducting liquid iron alloy in the outer core sustains Earth's main magnetic field. The field is dynamic, changes over time, and has undergone reversals. These facts support a functional analogy about persistence and planetary organization, not evidence that Earth is a living organism or has subjective experience.

2. **The ancient-field timeline carries uncertainty.** Tarduno et al. reported evidence consistent with a geodynamo at about 3.4–3.45 billion years ago, but paleomagnetic interpretation is vulnerable to later alteration and resetting. Treat a specific age as evidence with methodological uncertainty, not a timeless settled point. See https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1183445 and the review https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-earth-032320-102418.

3. **Magnetic fields do not unconditionally protect atmospheres.** Atmospheric escape depends on solar-wind interaction, ionospheric conditions, atmospheric composition, gravity, stellar activity, and multiple escape channels. A global dipole can redirect energy and plasma but is not a guarantee of atmospheric retention. See Gronoff et al. (2020), https://doi.org/10.1029/2019JA027639, and Ramstad & Barabash (2021), https://doi.org/10.1007/s11214-021-00791-1.

4. **Separate the energy systems.** Solar radiation drives much of the climate and surface biosphere; internal heat, compositional buoyancy, secular cooling, latent heat, and inner-core processes contribute to core dynamics. The Sun is not the direct energy source of the geodynamo. Core–mantle coupling is a research topic and should be distinguished from established mechanism, numerical-model output, and speculation.

5. **Do not translate “planetary regulation” into “planetary consciousness.”** Earth-system feedback and geophysical self-organization can be described without claiming a literal planetary mind, soul, or intention.

### Evidence classification

- **Established mechanism:** geodynamo and time-varying geomagnetic field.
- **Active scientific questions:** detailed energy budget, ancient-field chronology, core–mantle coupling, and atmosphere–magnetosphere interaction.
- **Interpretive analogy:** soul/heart language and Earth consciousness.
- **Not performed:** no new geodynamo model, atmospheric-escape simulation, or spectral analysis.
