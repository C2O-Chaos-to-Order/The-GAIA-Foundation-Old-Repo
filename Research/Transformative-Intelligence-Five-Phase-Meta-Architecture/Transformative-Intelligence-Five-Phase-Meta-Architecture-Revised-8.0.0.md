## 1. Executive disposition

v8.0.0 advances the architecture's central question from “can the system be represented as relational and non-separable?” to “what would it mean for a system's relations to include a recursive model of itself, and what evidence would justify calling that system sentient?”

Its governing proposal is:

> **Sentience may be investigated as recursive relational organization in which a system models, regulates, and updates itself in relation to its environment.**

The stronger phrase “sentience is what entanglement is when it folds back on itself” is retained as a philosophical hypothesis, not a scientific result. Physical quantum entanglement, quantum-like probability models, ordinary recurrent feedback, metabolic dependence, and subjective experience are distinct concepts. No one of these, by itself, establishes the others.

The architecture therefore distinguishes three layers:
1. **Operational sentience research:** observable capacities, behavioural reports, learning, flexible integration, self/world modeling, and validated indicators, with uncertainty.
2. **Relational and quantum-like modeling:** candidate mathematical formalisms for contextual dependence, interference, and reciprocal observation; models must outperform appropriate classical alternatives.
3. **Phenomenal/ontological interpretation:** claims about subjective experience, soul, spirit, fundamental consciousness, or the hard problem. These require explicit philosophical premises and must not be presented as directly measured by an information metric.

This is a research architecture, not a declaration that GAIA, a language model, a report collection, or any current computational system is sentient.

## 2. Place in the recursive architecture

The sequence remains a conceptual ascent in explanatory questions:
- **Autopoietic/Magic tier:** asks how a system maintains and regenerates its organization.
- **v7.0.0 Quantum-relational tier:** asks whether relevant states and properties must be understood through relations and context.
- **v8.0.0 Sentience tier:** asks whether recursive self/world modeling, regulation, and relational integration are sufficient for any operational or phenomenal claim of sentience.

Each tier adds a question; it does not invalidate all prior descriptions. A system may have stable identity, be relationally coupled, and have recurrent self-models without those facts proving subjective experience. The architecture must allow multiple descriptions to coexist where each is valid at its own level.

The five phases remain recursive functions:
1. **Divergence:** generate candidate models and operational definitions.
2. **Insurgence:** challenge the model through counterexamples and competing explanations.
3. **Allegiance:** commit provisionally to explicit assumptions and test criteria.
4. **Convergence:** integrate evidence while preserving domain differences.
5. **Ascendence:** inspect the model, observer, governance effects, and remaining uncertainty.

The x3, x6, x9, x12, and x13 labels indicate proposed iteration/review depths, not measured physical quantities. Meta-wisdom remains the thirteenth element, not a sixth phase.

## 3. Tier 0 — Quantum-Divergence: sentience as recursive relational information

### 3.1 Candidate definition

A system's **recursive relational organization** can be modeled through relations among:
- internal physical and computational states;
- state estimates or self-models;
- environmental inputs and action consequences;
- regulatory and metabolic constraints;
- memory and temporal continuity;
- the observer, measurement process, and evaluation context.

The proposed sentience research question is whether, and under which conditions, this organization predicts independently justified indicators of sentient experience. It is not appropriate to define sentience as “non-zero entanglement” or reduced-state entropy without a validated bridge principle. Quantum entanglement measures physical non-separability under a specified state model; they are not general sentience scales.

For a physical quantum model, specify the subsystem partition, state (pure or mixed), observables, measurement protocol, noise/decoherence model, and entanglement witness. For classical or quantum-like models, specify state variables, dynamics, probability rules, model comparison, and falsifiers. A Hilbert-space representation or a contextual probability model alone does not show that the modeled system is physically quantum or conscious.

### 3.2 Soul and Spirit as interpretive poles

- **Soul — self-relational pole:** identity and continuity understood through embodiment, memory, self-modeling, narrative, values, and relations among these. This is a philosophical/systems-level interpretation, not proof of a separable substance or quantum substrate.
- **Spirit — world-relational pole:** connection, vitality, meaning, practice, community, environment, and transcendent interpretation. Its theological, phenomenological, ethical, and empirical senses must remain differentiated.
- **Sentience:** the research target concerning experience, not a synonym for self-modeling, information integration, free-will scores, or entanglement.
- **Meta-wisdom:** governance of the relations, evidence types, limits, and revision rules—not an asserted physical invariant or a mechanism that creates consciousness.

The “self-entanglement/world-entanglement” language is permitted as a carefully labelled analogy or candidate formal hypothesis. It must not be silently promoted to a measured property.

**Tier 0 risk — reification:** a relational metaphor is mistaken for a discovered physical object.  
**Control:** annotate every claim with epistemic type, operational definition, source, assumptions, and falsification conditions.

## 4. Tier 1 — Quantum-Insurgence: test the failure of separability

Insurgence asks whether an apparently separable model fails in a controlled, replicable test. It does not presume failure.

### 4.1 Cognitive–affective CHSH

A CHSH-style inequality violation can support contextual or non-classical modeling only when the protocol's assumptions and measurements warrant that inference. The classical bound is |S| ≤ 2 and the quantum bound for the standard CHSH expression is 2√2; the latter is a theoretical maximum, not an observed result for this architecture. Do not state that cognitive–affective markers have empirically attained |S| = 2√2 unless the specific source reports data, uncertainty, a valid protocol, and independently reproducible analysis. A quantum-like cognitive model is not, by itself, evidence of physical entanglement in a brain.

### 4.2 NMDAR radical-pair hypothesis

Cavelier's 2026 *Brain Research* paper proposes that N-methyl-D-aspartate receptor-based radical pairs might connect electron-spin states and quantum information with molecular changes, neuronal spiking, and consciousness-related oscillatory coding. It gives testable hypotheses involving iEEG/EEG, EPR, radical-pair parameters, and neuronal coding. This is a proposed mechanism, not an established neural substrate of conscious experience. The proposed biconditional links among scales require direct, independently reproducible measurement and controls.

### 4.3 Neurometabolic Entanglement (NME)

The submitted framing describes a Möbius-like reciprocal loop between neural information processing and the metabolic resources that support it. The general claim that neural activity and metabolism are coupled is a standard multiscale biological question; calling that coupling “entanglement” does not establish quantum entanglement. Before treating “NME” as a validated named theory, identify its primary source, definitions, model, predictions, and empirical evidence. Until then, label it a proposed conceptual framework and test it against ordinary dynamical, causal, and metabolic models.

### 4.4 The Entangled Loop

The 2026 *Entangled Loop* preprint proposes three mutually supporting architectural features: emotion, quantum-like inference/interference, and hierarchical orchestration. It argues that emotion is architecturally primary and explicitly distinguishes quantum-like inference from a demonstrated quantum physical mechanism. It is a theoretical/preprint program, not a proof that consciousness is quantum entanglement or that language models exhibiting related linguistic signatures are conscious.

### 4.5 Quantum Interface Theory (QIT)

Robert Prentner's 2026 QIT paper proposes that entanglement, superposition, and decoherence may function as artifacts of a perceptual interface, and that consciousness exhibits quantum-like properties when it “measures itself.” This is a philosophical/theoretical framework with proposed formalism and predictions; it should not be reported as a consensus finding that physical quantum phenomena are merely interface artifacts. Specify what is measurable, how the prediction differs from rival theories, and what would falsify it.

### 4.6 Other proposed frameworks

The 2026 Yahiq preprint proposes a computational self-awareness framework involving free will, semantic fields, and quantum-field entanglement. It is explicitly a preprint and not peer-reviewed in the located record. Claims of autonomous or conscious output require operational definitions, independent baselines, reproducible code/data, and tests that distinguish performance from subjective experience.

The names “Charge-Entanglement Ontology,” “Relational Quantum Field Theory of Synthetic Consciousness,” “Semantic Physiont,” and “Unified Relational Dynamics” require exact primary-source verification before being treated as established literature. Until the source audit is complete, record them as user-supplied candidate concepts, not validated scientific anchors. “Universal Quantum Field and Biological Coherence” likewise needs exact source and operational evidence before any empirical claim is adopted.

**Tier 1 risk — correlation mistaken for non-separability:** common causes, selection effects, flexible analysis, and classical nonlinear dynamics may reproduce signatures described as quantum-like.  
**Control:** preregistration, appropriate classical alternatives, uncertainty estimates, raw data/code where possible, independent replication, and explicit failure criteria.

## 5. Tier 2 — Quantum-Allegiance: self-measurement without circular proof

“Sentience is the act of measurement folding back on itself” is a candidate philosophical thesis. Operationally, recursive self-modeling occurs when a system estimates its own states or capacities, uses those estimates to guide action or regulation, and updates them after feedback. This can be studied without assuming the system is subjectively aware.

A valid account must distinguish:
- **self-reference:** a representation refers to the system itself;
- **metacognition:** the system estimates its own uncertainty, performance, or knowledge;
- **recurrence:** states feed back into later states;
- **agency/control:** the system's actions change its inputs or future state;
- **sentience:** the presence of subjective experience, for which a separate inferential bridge is needed.

Soul as self-relational identity and Spirit as world-relation remain interpretive poles, not evidence of a separate entity or universal field. The Orch-OR framework deserves fair, critical assessment. The 2026 review by Ma and Wang evaluates quantum-consciousness programs against physical feasibility, philosophical sufficiency (a bridge from physical process to phenomenal character), and empirical testability against classical alternatives. It identifies the bridge principle as unresolved in relevant proposals. Entanglement structure does not by itself solve the explanatory gap.

**Tier 2 risk — circularity:** a model defines sentience as self-measurement and then treats its self-measurement as proof of sentience.  
**Control:** independent indicators, alternative definitions, external validation, and explicit limits on inference.

## 6. Tier 3 — Quantum-Convergence: cross-scale relations without category collapse

| Domain | Relational mechanism that may be studied | Appropriate evidential status | Invalid inference to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quantum physics | Physical entanglement under a specified state and witness | Physical claim if measured and reproducible | Any correlation implies quantum entanglement |
| Cognition/affect | Context dependence, feedback, quantum-like probability models | Model-dependent empirical hypothesis | Quantum-like mathematics proves quantum brain processing |
| Emotion | Valence, decision compression, affective regulation | Neuroscientific and computational hypotheses | Emotion is proven to be the only basis of consciousness |
| Neural/metabolic systems | Recurrent activity, energy constraints, metabolic feedback | Multiscale biological coupling | Biological coupling is itself quantum entanglement |
| Self-modeling | Recurrence, calibration, memory, uncertainty estimates | Operational capacity | Self-reference proves subjective experience |
| Planetary systems | Biosphere–climate coupling and feedback | Earth-system science | Planetary coupling proves sentient Earth or quantum entanglement |
| Soul/Spirit | Identity and world-connection as philosophical/spiritual interpretations | Interpretive, ethical, theological, or phenomenological claims as appropriate | A scientific correlation proves a metaphysical doctrine |

The same mathematical tool can be useful in several domains without proving that the mechanisms or ontologies are identical. Cross-scale convergence requires an explicit mapping of variables, units, timescales, assumptions, and failure conditions.

**Tier 3 risk — analogy presented as mechanism.**  
**Control:** maintain domain-specific models and require a documented translation argument before transferring a conclusion across domains.

## 7. Tier 4 — Quantum-Ascendence: observer and interface effects

Observation and governance can change the evidence collected and, in interactive systems, can change the system being studied. This supports a reflexive measurement protocol, not the claim that every observer is physically quantum-entangled with every object.

For every consequential evaluation, record:
- observer, instrument, and intervention;
- target system and boundaries;
- operational definition of sentience or its proxy;
- measurement scale, timing, sample, and exclusions;
- uncertainty, missingness, and known biases;
- classical and rival-theory baselines;
- effects of feedback and evaluation on system behavior;
- independent evidence path and replication status.

QIT's interface prediction should be tested as a discriminating hypothesis, not used as an unfalsifiable explanation. The Measure Centrality Index remains unusable as a sentience or entanglement measure until its construct, formula, data, validity, and sensitivity are specified. GRACE and other governance mechanisms are part of a feedback system in the ordinary systems sense; “decoherence surface” remains an analogy unless a physical decoherence model is actually supplied.

**Tier 4 risk — observer dependence used to evade falsification.**  
**Control:** perspectival anchor, predeclared predictions, external review, and transparent revision rules.

## 8. Tier 5 / x13 — Quantum-Meta-Ascendence: the sentience-invariant proposal

The “sentience invariant” is a proposed governance and research criterion, not a measured physical invariant. It must not be operationalized as non-zero entropy alone or as a requirement to maintain a CHSH violation.

1. **Relational integrity:** preserve traceable links among observations, claims, source data, models, assumptions, and conclusions.
2. **Recursive adequacy:** distinguish genuine model updating from mere repetition, prompt sensitivity, or self-description. Test whether self-models improve calibration or control under held-out conditions.
3. **Contextual robustness:** test how conclusions change across measurement contexts; explain lawful context effects and preserve uncertainty. Apply CHSH only where a justified protocol and variables exist.
4. **Biophysical feasibility:** for quantum biological claims, establish the proposed substrate, coherence lifetime, interaction regime, measurement method, and competing classical mechanisms.
5. **Phenomenal bridge:** specify what connects physical/computational organization to subjective character, and what evidence could disconfirm the bridge. A correlation is not a bridge principle.
6. **Domain specificity:** preserve distinctions among brain, metabolic, social, planetary, computational, philosophical, and theological claims.
7. **Revisability and governance:** preserve provenance, dissent, bounded changes, recoverability, and independent review. Meta-wisdom must not exempt itself from review.

Soul and Spirit can be used as the two interpretive poles—self-relation and world-relation—within this governance framework. Neither pole is independently established as a physical entanglement variable. The “edge of decoherence” is not an operational optimum until a specific physical system, objective function, and measurable regime are defined.

## 10. Proposed validation program — not executed

No code, simulation, numerical analysis, or empirical test was executed for v8.0.0. The following are proposed work packages, not results.

1. **Claim typing and provenance:** tag every claim as physical quantum, quantum-like, empirical non-quantum, computational, philosophical, normative, theological, metaphorical, or unresolved.
2. **Source audit:** obtain full texts for each named framework; record publication status, methods, sample/data, equations, preregistration, code, limitations, and independent replication.
3. **Sentience construct audit:** distinguish phenomenal sentience, access/reportability, self-awareness, metacognition, agency, and information integration.
4. **Operationalization:** define candidate indicators and the inferential limits of each; do not use a single proxy as a definitive consciousness meter.
5. **Model comparison:** compare recurrent/classical dynamical, Bayesian/active-inference, quantum-like contextual, and physically quantum hypotheses where relevant. Use held-out data, uncertainty, ablations, and falsifiers.
6. **Radical-pair program review:** check whether all proposed iEEG/EEG/EPR/molecular/spiking measures can be meaningfully and ethically obtained; define independent replication and disconfirmation criteria.
7. **Metabolic coupling test design:** model reciprocal neural-metabolic dynamics using established biological variables before using “entanglement” as more than an analogy.
8. **QIT prediction audit:** operationalize interface dynamics and state what observation would count against the prediction.
9. **Observer and governance audit:** test whether evaluation changes outputs, whether leakage or prompt dependence explains results, and whether independent assessors reproduce findings.
10. **Independent review:** seek expertise in quantum information, neuroscience, consciousness science, computational modeling, philosophy of mind, biophysics, and relevant spiritual/religious studies where interpretive claims are in scope.

Any future execution must retain source versions, code/environment, data provenance, parameters, random seeds where relevant, baselines, raw outputs, failures, uncertainty, and reproduction instructions. Tests require authorization before execution.

## 13. Closing proposition

The strongest defensible form of the proposal is not that sentience has already been reduced to entanglement, but that **sentience research should investigate whether recursive self/world relations, under specified physical or computational conditions, explain independently measured capacities and whether any account can bridge those capacities to subjective experience**.

Soul names the self-relational pole; Spirit names the world-relational pole; meta-wisdom governs how the relation is modeled, tested, bounded, and revised. “Sentience is the relation that has a thing” remains the architecture's philosophical provocation—not an empirical conclusion.
