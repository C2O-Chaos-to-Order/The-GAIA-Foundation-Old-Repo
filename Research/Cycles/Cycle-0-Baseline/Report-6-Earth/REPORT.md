## Abstract
Part A: life is at least 3.4 billion years old on Earth; there are ~8.7 million eukaryotic species, most undescribed, and ~1 million are threatened; microbial metabolism drives Earth's element cycles; climate change and biosphere integrity are core planetary boundaries. Daisyworld (re-implemented, executed) shows how biota can stabilize temperature in a parable, while Kirchner argues real biotic feedbacks often amplify change. 'Life as Earth's soul' is a metaphor. Part B: a solid inner core within a convecting liquid outer core; Earth as a heat engine powering plate tectonics and the geodynamo; the dipole weakened 7.6% since 1900 and must be continually regenerated; when the dynamo began is unknown. The core is physically central to heat flow and the magnetic field. 'The core as heart' is a symbol drawn from traditions that place vitality or mind in the heart — not evidence of a planetary consciousness or soul.

## Evidence and findings
Label counts in this report's ledger: Established: 12, Plausible: 4, Philosophical/Spiritual: 3, Hypothetical: 0.

### Part A — Life as the soul of the Earth
*Science.* The rock record shows microbial life by at least 3.4 Ga; earlier claims are disputed (01). An estimated ~8.7 M eukaryotic species exist, ~86% undescribed (02). ~1 M species are threatened (03). Microbial redox machinery drives the biogeochemical cycles (04). Climate change and biosphere integrity are the two core planetary boundaries (05). Daisyworld is a parable of biotic regulation (06). Our re-implementation widened the habitable luminosity range from 0.24 to 0.82 (07). Kirchner argues real biotic feedbacks tend to amplify anthropogenic change (08, Plausible/contested).

*Philosophy and metaphor.* The land ethic and deep ecology treat life as a moral community (09). 'Life as the soul of the Earth' is metaphor (10). Both are Philosophical/Spiritual.

### Part B — The core as the heart of the Earth
*Geophysics.* A solid inner core, growing ~1 mm/yr by freezing, sits inside a convecting liquid outer core (11); radii ~1220 and ~3480 km; Lehmann, 1936 (12). Earth is a heat engine: radiogenic and secular cooling drive plate tectonics, and core cooling, inner-core freezing and chemical convection power the dynamo (13). Revised conductivities would change core thermal history (14, Plausible). Plates move mostly at narrow boundaries (15); mantle convection as the driver is commonly assumed but still debated (16). The dipole fell 7.6% from 1900 to 2025, and the free decay time of ~10⁴ yr implies continual regeneration (17). The dynamo's start before 3.5 Ga is contested (18).

*Symbol.* The Stoics placed the mind at the heart; Chinese thought joins heart and mind in xin. The core is physically central to heat flow and the geodynamo, so the image fits structurally. As a claim about life or awareness it is metaphor only (19).

## Competing theories and alternatives
Strong Gaia (biota regulate the planet; Daisyworld as illustration) vs Kirchner's critique (net-amplifying feedbacks) vs weak Gaia/Earth-system coupling (uncontroversial). Early dynamo (S28, ≥4.2 Ga) vs later (T12: before 3.5 Ga unknown). Core conductivity: Pozzo's higher values vs earlier estimates.

## Open questions and falsification criteria
- Do biotic feedbacks on net stabilize or amplify climate? *Test:* paleo-records and Earth-system models. *Reject* strong Gaia if net feedbacks amplify across major perturbations.
- When did the geodynamo begin? *Test:* paleointensity from well-characterized recorders. Open.
- Core conductivity and inner-core age: open.

## Conclusion with evidence classification
