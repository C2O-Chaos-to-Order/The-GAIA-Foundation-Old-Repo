# Cycle-2 R3 codebook for soul-conception attributes (written before the second coding)

Rule for every cell: code **1** only if the cited source passage states or directly implies the attribute FOR THIS conception;
code **0** if the passage states or directly implies its absence/denial, or if the conception is described without it where the
attribute would be expected; mark **uncertain** whenever the decision needs inference beyond the passage. Evidence: quote <= 40 words.
The coder does not look at the Cycle-1 value of the cell (the sample file holds no coded values).

| attr | 1 if the source says the soul/self/life-principle in this conception ... |
|---|---|
| A1 immaterial | is not a body / not made of matter (incorporeal, non-physical) |
| A2 survives death | continues to exist after the death of the body (incl. rebirth/transmigration) |
| A3 principle of life/breath | is what makes a body alive, or is identified with breath/life |
| A4 seat of thought/identity | thinks / is the knower / is what the person most truly is |
| A5 bearer of moral character | carries virtue, vice, karma or moral standing / is the locus of moral evaluation |
| A6 oriented to purpose/final end | has a goal, final end, liberation or telos as part of its nature |
| A7 enduring unchanging self | is permanent / eternal / unchanging |
| A8 form of / inseparable from body | is the form, function or organisation of the body, or cannot exist apart from it |
| A9 enduring self denied | the conception denies that there is an enduring self/soul |
| A10 relational/communal | is constituted by relations or community (personhood conferred or achieved socially) |
| A11 identical with universal ground | is identical with the ultimate reality / universal principle |
| A12 extends to non-human beings | is also attributed to animals, plants or other non-human beings |
