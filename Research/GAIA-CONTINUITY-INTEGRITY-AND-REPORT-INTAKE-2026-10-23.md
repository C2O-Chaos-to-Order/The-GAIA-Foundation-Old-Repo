## Required reporting discipline

Every report or workstream must distinguish, as applicable:
- proposed vs. documented;
- designed vs. executed;
- smoke-tested vs. empirically validated;
- internally reproduced vs. independently reproduced;
- reviewed internally vs. reviewed by an external specialist;
- ready for integration vs. approved for integration;
- implemented vs. tested vs. verified.

A plan is not execution. A source located is not proof that it supports every associated claim. A citation count is not a source audit. A synthetic test is not empirical validation. An “indeterminate” result is not a pass. Do not invent missing artifacts, results, titles, sources, run counts, or reviewer approvals.

1. Preserve the exact supplied text, title, version, and available source identity before edits.
