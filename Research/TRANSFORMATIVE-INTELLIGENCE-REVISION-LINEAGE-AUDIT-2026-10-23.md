## 2. Dependency map

The documented architecture implies this dependency chain, but the arrows below mean conceptual dependence, not validated derivation:

1. **Seven foundational reports (Reports 1–7)** provide domain content and evidence constraints.
2. **v3.0.0** maps the five-phase governance cycle and x3/x6/x9/x12/x13 progression onto the research process.
3. **v4.0.0** proposes a formal hypergraph/state-space representation for governed changes.
4. **v5.0.0** expresses invariants, failure handling, relation types, and integration requirements.
5. **v7.0.0** proposes a non-separable/quantum-relational interpretation and adds CHSH-style test proposals.
6. **v8.0.0** asks whether recursive self/world modeling, regulation, and relational integration predict independently justified sentience indicators.

The sequence is not a chain of empirical proofs. Each later conceptual layer inherits all unresolved definitions and claims from its predecessors unless the newer version explicitly revises them. No formal derivation currently establishes that the seven reports constitute a physical quantum system or that recursive relationality is sufficient for phenomenal sentience.

## 3. Cross-version technical consistency findings

### 3.1 Quantum language is explicitly bounded in v7 and v8

Both revisions warn against equating ordinary coupling, statistical dependence, quantum-like probability models, and physical quantum entanglement. v7 states that a Hilbert-space representation alone is insufficient to establish physical entanglement. v8 states that non-zero entanglement or reduced-state entropy cannot serve as a validated general sentience scale. These are necessary safeguards and should be preserved in all derivative material.

### 3.2 CHSH values are theoretical bounds, not architecture results

The architecture files distinguish the classical CHSH bound (|S| ≤ 2) and the standard quantum maximum (2√2) from an observed result. No reviewed revision reports an independently reproducible human cognitive–affective CHSH violation or a physical brain-entanglement measurement. Any future test requires explicit observables, compatibility/independence assumptions, preregistration, missing-data handling, uncertainty, alternatives, and reproducible data/code.

### 3.3 “Invariant” is a specification obligation until proven

The v5 invariant contract and the v7/v8 “entanglement/sentience invariant” language are not formal proofs. To upgrade any invariant claim, the repository must provide a mathematical statement, assumptions, proof or counterexample search, implementation semantics, and tests against adversarial cases. A governance principle can be valuable without being a proven mathematical invariant; the label must remain accurate.

### 3.4 Source status and evidence type must remain explicit

The v8 source ledger includes published hypotheses, theoretical frameworks, and preprints. These source types can establish that a proposal exists, but do not establish its ontology or efficacy. This distinction is especially important for NMDAR radical-pair hypotheses, Neurometabolic Entanglement, “The Entangled Loop,” and Quantum Interface Theory.

### 3.5 Architecture and report dependencies are not fully reconciled

The current seven-report reconciliation identifies open claim-level audits, Report 18's missing numbered slot in the broader corpus, and unresolved package-cycle claims. The architecture's references to “seven reports,” “eight domains,” and x13 tiers need a single explicit mapping table defining each entity, version, direction of dependency, and whether the relation is normative, conceptual, empirical, or computational. No mapping should imply that a shared metaphor or shared source creates independent confirmation.

## 4. Required reconciliation records

Before this lineage can pass, record:

- The provenance and intended status of v6.0.0, if such a revision existed; otherwise document that the version number is intentionally unused.
- A v3 → v4 → v5 → v7 → v8 change map listing retained, modified, removed, and newly introduced claims/definitions.
- A symbol/term dictionary for five phases, x3/x6/x9/x12/x13, hypergraph objects, invariant, non-separability, quantum-like, physical entanglement, recursive relationality, sentience, and meta-wisdom.
- A dependency graph mapping each architecture claim to report/source IDs and classifying the edge (supports, contradicts, depends on, derived from, tests, falsifies, or governance requirement).
- A claim register for each architecture revision with source type, version/hash, exact claim, falsifier, evidence state, and unresolved objections.
- A test plan with formal acceptance criteria. Proposed tests must not be counted as executed; physical quantum claims require physical-system evidence; sentience claims require independently justified operational indicators and rival explanations.
- Independent review readiness packages for formal methods, consciousness science, quantum foundations, and Earth-system science as applicable.

## 5. Decision

**The located architecture versions are coherent in their repeated warning against category collapse, but the version lineage and dependencies are not fully reconciled.** v6.0.0 is absent from the located sequence, the v5-to-v7 transition is not documented as a complete change map, and no architecture revision has executed its proposed simulations or established a formal proof. Keep this audit open, preserve all revisions, and do not describe the architecture as formally verified, physically quantum-entangled, or a validated theory of sentience.
