## Readings
- **Soul.** Cycle D read the primary 24-country continuity study (K61), which upgrades TD-SOUL-01 to Established: mental continuity is judged more likely than bodily continuity, religiosity and religious framing raise continuity judgments, yet cessation is the modal answer in 19 of 24 countries. Judgments cluster within persons, as broad stances (TD-SOUL-28), and a single exception country is expected from noise (TD-SOUL-29). Soul and afterlife beliefs predict lower approval of mind upload (K55), though K55's 'full mediation' follows from collinearity (TD-SOUL-31). A new veridical-NDE scale (K53) has only moderate, imprecisely estimated agreement (TD-SOUL-33), and hidden-target designs need thousands of arrests under central assumptions (TD-SOUL-35).
- **Spirit.** Cycle D finally read the 'complete mystical experience' criterion (K49): at least 60% on all four MEQ30 subscales, with good agreement against the MEQ43 (kappa 0.80) but a reported d' that cannot be reproduced (TD-SPIRIT-26) and a noisy all-or-none boundary (TD-SPIRIT-27). Psychedelic trials are functionally unblinded (K50; K51 blinding index 0.88-1.00), and expectancy alone could produce effects of K51's size (TD-SPIRIT-30). Death anxiety falls after psychedelics in uncontrolled pre-post studies (K52), but the prediction interval spans zero and regression to the mean can mimic part of the effect (TD-SPIRIT-32). Transformative spiritual experiences predict later volunteering and some vulnerability markers (K62), with an E-value of 1.71 (TD-SPIRIT-34).
- **Subconscious.** Cycle D deepened Mind x Justice. Implicit race bias can operate without awareness; its proponents argue that controllability implies responsibility (K56). Small individual-level biases can yield 1.2-1.6-fold disparities in selective decisions (TD-SUBC-37), and K56's state-level r = 0.64 is compatible with an individual r near 0.05 (TD-SUBC-38). A registered report found that lowering free-will belief changed neither control nor automaticity (K63), though at its size small effects remain possible (TD-SUBC-35). With K35 and K57, beliefs about agency appear to shape punishment attitudes more than behaviour (TD-SUBC-39, Plausible).
- **Consciousness.** Cycle D found an independent analysis arguing that IIT's silent-neuron predictions are testable (K58); a cartoon of auxiliary assumptions shows failures still weigh even when test conditions are uncertain (TD-CONS-26). A second adversarial protocol (K59) can confirm large predicted effects but, at 9-15 sessions, almost never yields strong evidence for a null (TD-CONS-28; one validation Fail disclosed). Consciousness during deep hypothermic arrest (K60) is feasibility-level, and its implicit-learning result depends on an unreported chance rate (TD-CONS-30). An IPD meta-analysis finds cognitive motor dissociation common, varying by cause and state (K64).
