# Framework addendum: the Soul, Spirit, Subconscious and Consciousness track

**Owner instruction (2026-10-24):** "Provide research as well for The Soul, The Spirit, The Subconscious, The Consciousness, in the same pattern (x3)(x6)(x9)", and "this will still be required to map and matrix to all current work". The track runs after the five main cycles (C0–C4) and the Master Report.

**Standing rules (unchanged from the Seven-Report Framework, `Research/Cycles/Cycle-0-Baseline/FRAMEWORK.md`):** Label every conclusion as Established, Plausible, Philosophical/Spiritual or Hypothetical. Cite a source you actually read for every factual claim. Keep metaphor separate from mechanism. Record what remains unresolved. Never present an unexecuted simulation as a result. GAIA is the motherthread, a Philosophical/Spiritual organising principle that is kept separate from scientific findings. Traditions' views are reported as descriptions, never as proven. Indeterminate is never Pass.

**Relation to the seven reports:** each topic **deepens** an existing report rather than duplicating it. Track claims carry a `deepens` column naming the report claims they extend. Track sources use the prefix **K**. Main-cycle sources that are reused keep their IDs.

## Topic 1 — The Soul (deepens Report 3)
Report 3 compares traditions' accounts of the soul and tests a typology of soul conceptions. The track adds what can be studied empirically **about soul beliefs**: their cognitive origins (intuitive dualism and intuitive materialism), how they vary across people and cultures, how scientists and lay people hold them, and how such beliefs are measured and coded. It also adds the philosophical persistence question (connectedness, continuity, fission). **Boundary:** report beliefs and arguments as what they are; don't declare the soul's existence or nonexistence proven. Evidence about beliefs is not evidence about their object.

## Topic 2 — The Spirit (deepens Report 4)
Report 4 covers spirit as vitality, transcendence, connection and inspiration, with the measured links to health handled as epidemiology. The track adds **self-transcendent experience**, meaning mystical-type experience and awe: how it is measured, whether experimental effects replicate, and how traditions describe transcendence. **Boundary:** keep spiritual interpretation separate from what can be independently demonstrated. A questionnaire score measures a report of an experience, not the reality of what was experienced.

## Topic 3 — The Subconscious (new)
**Cover:** mental processing that influences experience and behaviour without being consciously reported. That includes (a) **psychodynamic** accounts (the repressed unconscious, repression, dissociative amnesia), (b) the **cognitive unconscious** and **automaticity** (automatic, involuntary processes that precede and interact with awareness), (c) **implicit memory and priming**, including the replication problems of behavioural priming and the measurement problem of 'unaware' participants, (d) **dreams and sleep** (memory reactivation and consolidation, and dream generation), and (e) **contemplative and traditional views** (for example the Yogācāra repository consciousness *ālaya-vijñāna*, Advaita's waking, dream and deep-sleep states, and Leibniz's *petites perceptions*).
**Domains:** cognitive psychology, memory research, sleep science, clinical psychology, history of ideas, comparative philosophy.
**Deliverable:** what is established about non-conscious processing, where the evidence depends on how awareness is measured, which popular claims failed replication, and what remains open.
**Boundary:** 'the subconscious' is not one thing. Claims about one sense (for example implicit learning) may not be carried to another (for example repression). Evidence of non-conscious processing requires a sensitive, adequately powered awareness measure, and a null awareness test is not proof of unawareness. Practitioner or public belief in repression is not evidence that repression occurs. Traditional doctrines are described, not tested. 'Subconscious' is used here as the everyday umbrella term, and the literature mostly says 'unconscious' or 'implicit'.
**Placement in the matrix:** Being axis = **Mind** (pre-declared, because the axis has no Subconscious category). Links to Consciousness are recorded in the link graph.

## Topic 4 — Consciousness (deepens Report 5)
Report 5 covers awareness, attention, self-awareness, altered states and the main theories (with the adversarial-collaboration and complexity-measure work). The track adds **how theories are tested**: report and no-report confounds, testability of theory-specific predictions, metacognition and measurement of 'pure awareness' in contemplative practice. **Boundary:** don't treat any theory as having solved consciousness. Contemplative reports are data about reported experience.
