## Reading of this cycle
Two matrix gaps were addressed. For Consciousness x Justice, covert consciousness in unresponsive patients is common enough that a negative test leaves a substantial chance of consciousness (K26, K30, TB-CONS-11), and K30's presumption of consciousness is recorded as an ethical position. For Consciousness x Love, brief meditation showed no total effect on compassion, the pattern expected for a small mediated effect (K28; flagged run). ## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **TB-CONS-01** [E] In Whyte, Hohwy & Smith's active-inference model of masking, late P3b-like potentials and increased PFC activity were produced by the working-memory demands of self-report and vanished without report, yet simulated PFC activity on visible trials still crossed the reportability threshold. On this model, no-report results do not necessarily contradict cognitive (global-workspace or higher-order) theories. *Sources:* K13. *Limits:* One computational model; shows compatibility, not that the theories are right. *Deepens:* R5:C1-R5-02;R5:C2-R5-03.
- **TB-CONS-02** [E] In SIM-CONS-A's minimal threshold toy (gating + report demand), removing report cut the visible-minus-invisible PFC contrast by 60% while 84% of visible trials still exceeded the reportability threshold. K13's qualitative pattern follows from these two assumptions, so the toy shows the logic is coherent but adds no evidence about brains. *Sources:* SIM-CONS-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-CONS-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-CONS-A/iter-1#H2. *Limits:* Assumption-driven by construction. *Deepens:* R5:C1-R5-02.
- **TB-CONS-04** [E] The Minimal Phenomenal Experience questionnaire (92 items; 1,403 usable of 3,627 responses from meditators, mostly Vipassana 43.9% and Zen 34.9%) yielded 12 factors explaining 44% of variance, including 'Pure Awareness in Dream and Sleep', 'Luminosity', 'Wakeful Presence' and 'Witness Consciousness'. *Sources:* K14. *Limits:* Self-selected meditators; exploratory factor names are interpretive. *Deepens:* R5:C3-R5-07;R5:C0v2-R5-04.
- **TB-CONS-05** [E] In SIM-CONS-A, 92 structureless items (n = 1,403) gave about 43 'factors' by the eigenvalue-above-1 rule but about 0 by parallel analysis; with 12 true factors parallel analysis recovered exactly 12 at loadings of .4 to .75, and '12 components explain 44%' corresponded to loadings near .6. K14's dimensionality is statistically plausible for data of its size; what the factors are experiences of is a separate question. *Sources:* SIM-CONS-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-CONS-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-CONS-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-CONS-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-CONS-A/iter-3#H2. *Limits:* Principal components on simulated normal items; K14 used factor analysis on real data (not re-run). *Deepens:* R5:C3-R5-07.
- **TB-CONS-08** [E] An individual-participant-data meta-analysis (56 studies, 1,248 patients with disorders of consciousness) found cognitive motor dissociation (volitional brain responses without behavioural response) less often after anoxic (OR 0.35) and cerebrovascular (OR 0.51) injury than after traumatic brain injury, and more often in MCS-minus than in UWS (OR 1.50). Time since injury and test modality were not independently associated. *Sources:* K26. *Limits:* Heterogeneous paradigms. QUADAS-2 shows some concerns for index tests and flow and timing. *Deepens:* R5;TB-CONS-01.
- **TB-CONS-11** [E] Assume prevalence .25, specificity .95 and sensitivity .5 (illustrative). A patient with a NEGATIVE covert-consciousness test is then still conscious with probability .15 (H1 supported). A negative test does not rule consciousness out, which is the probabilistic core of K30's presumption argument. Separately, K26's OR of 0.35 at a baseline detection rate of .25 corresponds to a rate of .10 and a risk ratio of .42, not .35 (H1 supported). *Sources:* SIM-CONS-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-1#R1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-2#R1. *Limits:* Assumed test characteristics; CMD is not identical to consciousness. *Deepens:* TB-CONS-08;TB-CONS-09;TB-CONS-10.
- **TB-CONS-13** [E] In a model where the stimulus category is coded on every trial and awareness is independent of that code, a decoder trained on seen trials classified unseen trials at 72% (signal 0.1) and 90% (signal 0.2; H1 supported). Decodability alone is therefore not a marker of conscious content, which supports the logical point in K27 without deciding the empirical dispute. *Sources:* SIM-CONS-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-3#R1. *Limits:* Toy decoder; independence of awareness assumed. *Deepens:* TB-CONS-12.
- **TB-CONS-15** [E] Sensitivity checks on Cycle A. With weak report demand and a higher reportability threshold, removing report reduced the PFC contrast by only 33% (H1: not supported), and only 65% of visible trials exceeded threshold (H2: not supported). The Cycle A no-report pattern depends on parameters. With 5-point skewed ordinal items, the Kaiser rule still retained about 44 spurious factors while parallel analysis retained none (supported). *Sources:* SIM-CONS-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-5#H2;SIM-CONS-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Toy models. *Deepens:* TB-CONS-02;TB-CONS-05.
- **TB-CONS-03** [P] Integrated information theory predicts that silencing all active neurons in the main complex leaves consciousness present (silent-brain prediction) and that disabling silent neurons can change experience (disabled-neuron prediction). Against the objection that these are untestable, Ponce de Leon & Yoshimi (2026) argue both can be tested, while noting why such theoretical disputes are hard to resolve (Lakatos). *Sources:* K12. *Limits:* Philosophical analysis; no experiment run. *Deepens:* R5:C0v2-R5-08;R5:C2-R5-03.
- **TB-CONS-09** [P] A neuropalliative review reports evidence that up to one in four patients diagnosed with unresponsive wakefulness syndrome may have covert consciousness, and that cognitive motor dissociation is associated with better functional outcomes. *Sources:* K30. *Limits:* 'Up to' figure reported secondarily. Prevalence depends on the tests used (TB-CONS-08). *Deepens:* TB-CONS-08;R5.
- **TB-CONS-12** [P] Proponents of global neuronal workspace theory argue that several analyses in the IIT-GNWT adversarial collaboration support GNWT predictions, such as transient ignition. They also argue that posterior decoding, presented as evidence for IIT, could have arisen from purely non-conscious processes. *Sources:* K27. *Limits:* Partisan commentary. The original Nature article and the IIT replies were not read in this cycle. *Deepens:* TB-CONS-03;R5.
- **TB-CONS-14** [P] Consciousness and love: in a preregistered trial (n = 80), one brief mindfulness meditation did not change compassionate responses overall. However, a serial path ran through mindfulness state and decentration (K28). In SIM-CONS-B a small, fully mediated effect gave total-effect power of only .13 at n = 80, while the indirect path was jointly significant in 92% of studies. The 'no total effect, significant indirect path' pattern is what a small mediated effect predicts. Disclosure: this run's validation check V1 failed narrowly (null indirect rate .055 against a pre-registered limit of .05, within Monte Carlo error of .05), so the claim is labelled Plausible. *Sources:* K28;SIM-CONS-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-CONS-B/iter-4#H2. *Limits:* Illustrative path sizes. Joint-significance test rather than bootstrap. Validation V1 Fail recorded as is (see VERIFICATION). *Deepens:* R5;R6;MR.
- **TB-CONS-06** [PS] 'Pure awareness' (interpretive): contemplative traditions describe consciousness without ordinary content (for example turiya in Advaita, S31), and meditators' reports of such states have a measurable structure (K14). Whether awareness without content exists as more than a reported state is a philosophical question the track does not settle. *Sources:* S31;K14;S32;G03. *Limits:* Interpretive. *Deepens:* R5:C0v2-R5-12;R5:C3-R5-07.
- **TB-CONS-07** [PS] Boundary: no theory has solved consciousness. Track Cycles A and B sharpened two methodological questions (report confounds, testability of IIT's silent-neuron predictions) but decided none of the theories. *Sources:* S16;S19;G03. *Limits:* Framework statement. *Deepens:* R5:C0v2-R5-12.
- **TB-CONS-10** [PS] Consciousness and justice: Hawkins (2026) argues that suffering in disorders of consciousness includes existential dimensions beyond pain, and that a presumption of consciousness should guide clinical care regardless of imaging findings. The track records this as an ethical position about the dignity and moral standing of possibly conscious patients, not as an empirical result. *Sources:* K30;G03. *Limits:* Normative argument; one author. *Deepens:* R5;R6;MR.
- **TB-CONS-16** [PS] Josipovic and Miskovic (2020) propose that consciousness-as-such is a non-conceptual, nondual awareness of a unique kind, not captured by the two-dimensional model of arousal plus phenomenal content. This contrasts with the minimal-phenomenal-experience framing of K14. The track records both as positions and links them to contemplative accounts (Report 3). *Sources:* K29;K14. *Limits:* Theoretical proposal. *Deepens:* TB-CONS-06;R3;R5.
