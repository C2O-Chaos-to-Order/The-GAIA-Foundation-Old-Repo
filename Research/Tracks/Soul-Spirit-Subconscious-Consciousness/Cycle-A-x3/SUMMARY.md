## Readings
- **Soul.** Cycle A turned from what traditions say about the soul (Report 3) to what can be studied about **beliefs** in souls. The most useful finding is a reading problem. People judge mental states more likely than physical states to continue after death, yet most expect everything to cease (K01, described; SIM-SOUL-A iter-1 shows both summaries can hold for the same data). Survey marginals also cannot show how many scientists hold dualism and physicalism together (K03; SIM-SOUL-A iter-2, H1 not supported). Kappa needs prevalence beside it (SIM-SOUL-A iter-3), which feeds back into Report 3's coding work.
- **Spirit.** Cycle A added self-transcendent experience. Mystical-type experience can be measured with acceptable psychometric fit (K04), but widely cited experimental effects of awe were not reproduced in six studies (K05). SIM-SPIRIT-A shows that three failed replications argue against medium effects more than small ones (H1 not supported), and that binary 'complete experience' classifications are noisy (H1 not supported). The hypothesis that awe links spirituality to well-being stays Hypothetical.
- **Subconscious.** The Subconscious is new. Cycle A set its scope (five senses of the term, kept apart) and found that the strongest current evidence is **methodological**. 'Unconscious' learning often rests on awareness tests too small to detect awareness (K09, reproduced in SIM-SUBC-A iter-1), and the classic behavioural-priming result failed replication, with signs of experimenter expectancy and awareness (K08). One simulation had a design flaw, disclosed and scheduled for Cycle B (iter-2). Traditional and historical views (Yogacara, Advaita, Leibniz) are described from sources already in the corpus.
- **Consciousness.** Cycle A deepened Report 5 on **how theories are tested**. No-report results need not refute cognitive theories, because report demands add late activity (K13, with a toy check in SIM-CONS-A iter-1 that adds logic, not evidence). IIT's silent-neuron predictions are argued to be testable (K12). The structure of meditators' 'pure awareness' reports is statistically plausible for data of its size (K14; SIM-CONS-A iter-2 and iter-3). No theory is decided.
