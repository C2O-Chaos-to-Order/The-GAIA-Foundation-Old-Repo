## Readings
- **Soul.** Cycle C turned to near-death experiences. A clinical summary reports them in 15-17% of critically ill patients and urges clinicians not to pathologise them (K37); its claim that biomedical models cannot account for them is recorded as Hypothetical. Reliving NDE memories engaged autobiographical-memory networks in 15 people (K38), but at n = 15 and p < .001 uncorrected both the activations and the null regions are weak evidence (TC-SOUL-23). Chinese NDE narratives share common features and occasionally carry local motifs (K46), in a sample too small to separate real from chance contrasts (TC-SOUL-25). Five of nine passes added no new source, one fetched source was a duplicate (K44 = K01), and K01's primary paper is still missing.
- **Spirit.** Cycle C examined whether the mystical experience itself does therapeutic work. Strong experience-outcome correlations exist, but non-hallucinogenic compounds challenge the idea that the experience is necessary (K39). A common cause alone reproduces correlations of that size, at any sample size (TC-SPIRIT-18). LSD's subjective effects plateau around 100 ug (K40), though sparse study counts make plateaus hard to tell from slow rises (TC-SPIRIT-20). Mystical scales are highly reliable and the visual scale less so (K43), which attenuates some correlations (TC-SPIRIT-22). Baseline wellbeing and spirituality predict mystical experiences (K45), and modelled attrition barely moves such correlations (TC-SPIRIT-24). Two passes added nothing and four were re-reads.
- **Subconscious.** Cycle C filled the Mind x Justice gap. Belief in free will predicts punitiveness across 44 countries (K35), reliably but weakly for individuals (53% concordance), and the country-level death-penalty null is uninformative at power 0.14 (TC-SUBC-29). A hypothesis that felt free will is conditioned by unconscious premotor activity (K36) is coherent in a toy model but untested (TC-SUBC-31). Methods matter: the PDP's assumptions can shift estimates of unconscious influence by a few points (TC-SUBC-25), and 'not significantly above chance' calibrations let partly visible stimuli through (TC-SUBC-27). A 19-lab Registered Report supports a small unconscious working-memory effect tied to perceptual sensitivity (K48, TC-SUBC-33).
- **Consciousness.** Cycle C read the primary IIT-GNWT adversarial collaboration (K42), which challenged key tenets of both theories. Scoring shows one failed prediction is weak evidence against a theory, but two of three central failures are substantial (TC-CONS-19), consistent with K41's caution. Dreaming under anaesthesia is common, mostly pleasant, and a clear case of disconnected consciousness (K32); its reported independence from awareness rests on low-powered studies, and interview timing explains under 10% of the clinical-experimental recall gap (TC-CONS-21). The Consciousness x Justice cell gained a survey of neurologists' diagnosis and treatment attitudes (K47), a reporting inconsistency the track could not resolve (TC-CONS-23), and an ethical-position statement (TC-CONS-24).
