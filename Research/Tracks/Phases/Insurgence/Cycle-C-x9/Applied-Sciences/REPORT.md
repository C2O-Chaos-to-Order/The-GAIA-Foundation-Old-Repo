## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **INC-APP-01** [E] R0, the expected number of secondary cases one case produces in a completely susceptible population, is usually read as a threshold (an outbreak continues if R0 > 1 and ends if R0 < 1), but it is not a biological constant, not a rate (it says nothing about how fast cases appear), not a measure of severity, and vaccination lowers the effective reproduction number R, not R0; reported values vary widely (measles 3.7-203.3 in one review) and some are obsolete. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = emergence of an outbreak when cases more than replace themselves. *Sources:* P26. *Limits:* Perspective article; the figures are P26's citations.
- **INC-APP-03** [E] P26 notes that removing susceptible people by vaccination does not technically reduce R0, because R0 assumes a completely susceptible population; for vaccination the appropriate metric is the effective reproduction number R, which does not assume complete susceptibility. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = outbreak potential in a partly immune population. *Sources:* P26. *Limits:* Definitional point; P26 gives no formula for R.
- **INC-APP-07** [E] P32's review of SARS-CoV-2 transmission found that 67% of index cases infected no one and 3% (287) infected more than five; a negative binomial fit gave mean 0.88 and dispersion k = 0.27, implying that the top 17% of index cases generate 80% of secondary cases; k was within the 0.1-0.7 range of earlier estimates. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = emergence driven by a few superspreaders. *Sources:* P32. *Limits:* Data mostly 2020, China and India; incomplete contact tracing; the offspring analysis uses 9,591 of 9,883 index cases.
- **INC-APP-02** [P] In a branching model of one introduced case, no outbreak reached 1,000 cases at R0 = 0.8; at R0 = 1.5 and 3 major outbreaks occurred in 58.1% and 94.1% of 20,000 runs (theory 58.3%, 94.0%), so even above the threshold many introductions die out. Changing the generation time from 3 to 15 days left the outbreak probability unchanged and stretched the time to 1,000 cases five-fold (44 vs 219 days at R0 = 1.5), illustrating P26's point that R0 is not a rate. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = outbreak emergence above R0 = 1. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-IN-APP-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-IN-APP-0/iter-1#H2. *Limits:* Homogeneous Poisson offspring; fully susceptible population.
- **INC-APP-04** [P] Near the threshold the share of introductions reaching 1,000 cases was 9.36% at R0 = 1.05 (theory 9.37%) and 31.6% at 1.2 (31.4%); 'no major outbreak below 1' was not supported: at R0 = 0.95, 0.16% of chains (32 of 20,000) still reached 1,000 cases, after 64 generations on average. Just below the threshold, rare large chains occur. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = outbreak emergence near R0 = 1. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-1#H2. *Limits:* 'Major' means reaching K = 1000; V1 passed at R0 = 0.95 only through the pre-set 0.002 allowance (disclosed).
- **INC-APP-05** [P] With superspreading (negative-binomial offspring, k = 0.1) the major-outbreak share fell to 7.0% at R0 = 1.5 (theory 6.8%; Poisson 58%) and 16.5% at R0 = 3 (16.2%; Poisson 94%): heterogeneity makes most introductions fail even at high R0, but those that take off are explosive. 'No major outbreak at R0 = 0.8' was not supported: one chain in 20,000 reached 1,000 cases. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = emergence with heterogeneous transmission. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-2#H2. *Limits:* k = 0.1 illustrative; standard extinction equation, not from P26.
- **INC-APP-06** [P] With 60% random immunity, R0 = 2 gave R = 0.8 and no chain reached 1,000 cases in 20,000; R0 = 3 and 4 gave R = 1.2 and 1.6 and major-outbreak shares 30.9% and 64.4%, matching 1 - q at the reduced R (31.4%, 64.2%): what decides emergence is the effective number R (P26), not R0. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = emergence suppressed by immunity. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-IN-APP-A/iter-3#H2. *Limits:* Perfect random immunity, homogeneous mixing.
- **INC-APP-08** [P] P32's overall fit is internally consistent: NB(0.88, 0.27) gives 67.6% zero, 3.6% above five and 16.5% producing 80% (P32: 67%, 3%, 17%). For the symptomatic subgroup the fit NB(2.06, 0.43) is not supported against P32's reported shares (47% zero vs 44%, 11.6% above five vs 9%); the top-80% check there used a placeholder (no P32 value). *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = superspreading profile. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-1#H3;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-2#H3. *Limits:* Checks P32's summary numbers against its own fit, not the raw data.
- **INC-APP-09** [P] With P32's dispersion k = 0.27, the major-outbreak share was 15.1% at R0 = 1.5 and 29.9% at 2.5 (theory 15.3%, 30.2%; Poisson about 58% and 89%); at P32's own mean 0.88, 'no major outbreak' was not supported (7 of 20,000 chains reached 1,000 cases). With 50% immunity, R0 = 1.6 gave none, 2.5 gave 9.0% and 4.0 gave 24.2%, as theory. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = emergence with observed heterogeneity. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-4#H2. *Limits:* Mixing P32's k with other R0 values is the agent's; homogeneous immunity.
- **INC-APP-10** [P] INA's below-threshold result replicated at K = 1,000 (R0 = 0.95: 0.15% of chains, H1 again not supported), but with K = 10,000 no chain at R0 = 0.95 reached the threshold while R0 = 1.05 kept 9.3%: 'major outbreaks below 1' are an artefact of a small K, not emergence. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = what counts as an outbreak. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-5#H2;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-IN-APP-B/iter-6#H2. *Limits:* Poisson offspring.
- **INC-APP-11** [P] Analysing frequency records of the 10 August 1996 Western Interconnection blackout, P41 finds a persistent change (a jump in noise level) about 2 minutes before the official triggering line trip, then abrupt, nearly discontinuous state changes linked to discrete events (loss of the McNary units, failing damping) rather than smooth critical slowing down; P41 argues that common leading indicators work only for bifurcation-induced tipping, while noise-induced tipping can occur far from any bifurcation. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = a technical system tipping into cascading failure. *Sources:* P41. *Limits:* Single event; causal link to the tree fault is stated as plausible, not certain (P41); P41's synthetic Eq. 2 prints a noise ramp inconsistent with its own plateaus (agent's check, see INC-APP-12).
- **INC-APP-12** [P] In P41's N-tipping system the switch time is broadly spread: 23% of 200 realisations had not switched by t = 2000 and the median among those that did was 1759, so 'switch during the ramp' was not supported (P41's single run switched at 1387). Before the switch the rolling standard deviation rose strongly (Kendall tau 0.89) and a simple least-squares estimator recovered the true drift slope (-1.92 vs -2) and noise level (1% error), but lag-1 autocorrelation also trended upward (tau 0.32), so 'no trend' was not supported narrowly. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = noise-induced tipping and its warning signs. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-2#H2;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-2#H3. *Limits:* Switch criterion and estimator are the agent's (not P41's BLE); P41's printed ramp formula was replaced by the offset form that joins its plateaus.
- **INC-APP-13** [P] B-tipping comparator on P41's fold drift: with a slow ramp and weak noise the system tipped at r = -0.387 and -0.384 (fold -0.385) with a clear critical-slowing-down signal (AR1 Kendall tau 0.84); stronger noise tipped it early (-0.235), a faster ramp late (-0.431). Together with INC-APP-12: autocorrelation warns well before a fold, much less before noise-induced switching. *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = bifurcation-induced tipping and its warning. *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-3#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-5#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Agent's comparator (P41 ramps r upward); detrending and window choices are the agent's.
- **INC-APP-14** [P] Replications: P32's summary shares again consistent with its fit (67.7% zero, 3.6% > 5, 16.6%); emergence with k = 0.27 again 15.1% and 30.1% at R0 1.5 and 2.5, and at R0 0.88 10 of 20,000 again reached K = 1,000, so 'no major outbreak below 1' was again not supported at K = 1,000; at K = 10,000 none at R0 0.95 (as in INB). *Operationalization:* APP Insurgence = superspreading (replications). *Sources:* SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-7#H2;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-7#H3;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-8#H2;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-9#H1;SIM-IN-APP-C/iter-9#H2. *Limits:* Replications only.
