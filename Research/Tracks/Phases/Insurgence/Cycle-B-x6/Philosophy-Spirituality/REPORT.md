## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **INB-PHI-02** [P] In a Chinese-restaurant process with concentration 5, distinct forms kept appearing (26.8 after 1,000 draws, 37.7 after 10,000, 49.7 after 100,000; analytic 27.0, 38.5, 50.0) while the rate of new forms fell to 0.00005 per draw: novelty continues but slows logarithmically. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = continuing emergence of new forms. *Sources:* SIM-IN-PHI-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-0/iter-1#H2. *Limits:* Formal stand-in only; it does not test Bergson's thesis.
- **INB-PHI-03** [P] P30 fits a two-group competition model (dx/dt = y P_yx - x P_xy, P_yx = c x^a u_x) to census data on religious non-affiliation in 85 regions; a broad error minimum near a = 1 gives logistic growth with rate c(2u_x - 1), and the fits indicate higher perceived utility for non-affiliation, from which P30's model predicts that religion will be driven toward extinction in those societies. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = growth of a once-marginal stance by conversion. *Sources:* P30. *Limits:* The prediction is conditional on the model and on constant utilities (P30 notes abrupt utility changes are excluded); supplementary analyses not read; journal version not read.
- **INB-PHI-05** [P] With a = 1 and u = 0.65 a 1% minority reached 50% at t = 15.3 (logistic rate 0.3, RK4 within 1e-6). With a = 1.5 and u = 0.6 an unstable interior point appeared at x = 0.308: minorities starting at 5% and 20% died out despite their higher utility, while 40% and 60% took over. With strong conformity a preferred stance still needs a critical mass. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = critical mass for a new stance. *Sources:* SIM-IN-PHI-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-A/iter-2#H2. *Limits:* a = 1.5 illustrative; P30's data favoured a near 1.
- **INB-PHI-06** [P] In a mean-field two-clique version of P30's experiment (minority clique of 10%, out-group weight p) the time to reach 50% grew from 11.0 at p = 1 to 39.0 at p = 0.001, linear in -ln p/(2u - 1) (r^2 = 0.995, slope 0.79), consistent with P30's reported delay d ~ -ln p/(2u - 1): cliques delay the spread of a stance but do not stop it. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = spread out of a clique. *Sources:* SIM-IN-PHI-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Not P30's N = 500 stochastic network; slope not compared with P30 (P30 gives proportionality only).
- **INB-PHI-07** [P] P35, reading Nimuendajú's 1914 ethnography, reports that from the early nineteenth century Apapocúava-Guaraní medicine men, inspired by visions and dreams, arose as prophets of the near end of the world and led migrations in search of the 'land without evil'; after news of the 1906 San Francisco earthquake (read as the western world collapsing in flames) the group completely abandoned their hopes, still expecting the end in 1912. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = a prophetic movement rising and collapsing. *Sources:* P35. *Limits:* P35 states there can be no conclusive confirmation of the link, yet later attributes the tribe's extinction to its worldview (internal tension); P35 also gives an implausible figure ('about 84 people') for the deaths in the 2010 Haiti earthquake (source quality flag, agent's knowledge).
- **INB-PHI-09** [P] Adding the abrupt utility change P30 excludes: a stance at 16.8% (t = 10) or at 98.7% (t = 30) fell below 0.001% by the end of the run once utility dropped from 0.65 to 0.4. With neutral utility (0.5) the stance did not grow (time to 50% about 2.3e7, so the pre-stated rise by t = 20 was not supported, as the formula implies): in this model growth needs a utility edge and lasts only while it holds. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = reversal by a shock; no growth without advantage. *Sources:* SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-5#H1. *Limits:* Toy; utility is a single number.
- **INB-PHI-10** [P] With a = 1.2 the critical mass fell to 11.6% (from 30.8% at a = 1.5): 5% and 10% minorities died out, 30% and 50% took over. Clique delay stayed linear in -ln p/(2u - 1) at u = 0.7 (r^2 = 0.997, slope 0.88 vs 0.79 at u = 0.6). The CRP replication matched within 4 SE (27.6, 39.0, 49.8 forms vs 27.0, 38.5, 50.0). *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = critical mass; clique delay; novelty (replications). *Sources:* SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-IN-PHI-B/iter-6#H2. *Limits:* As INA.
- **INB-PHI-01** [PS] Bergson, as presented in P08, argues for an original common impulse, the vital impulse (élan vital), that explains the creation of all living species, together with a principle of divergence and differentiation; read in this track, the vital impulse is a philosophical account of insurgence as the upsurge of the new. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = the upsurge of the new from a common impulse. *Sources:* P08. *Limits:* Metaphysical thesis; the 'insurgence' reading is the track's interpretation.
- **INB-PHI-04** [PS] Read philosophically, P30's model treats the rise or decline of a religious or irreligious stance as conversion driven by group size and perceived utility; it says nothing about the truth of either stance, and its 'extinction' result holds only for constant utilities. Set beside Bergson's vital impulse (P08), it is a mechanical account of how a new stance spreads, not of where the new comes from. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = spreading versus originating the new. *Sources:* P30;P08. *Limits:* Interpretation by this track; neither source frames its claim as insurgence.
- **INB-PHI-08** [PS] Read together, P35 and P30 show two sides of religious insurgence: a prophetic hope can rise from visions and gather followers, but a stance's persistence depends on how its adherents read events; P30's model treats utility as constant, while P35's case is exactly an abrupt collapse of perceived hope after news from outside. *Operationalization:* PHI Insurgence = hope rising and collapsing. *Sources:* P35;P30. *Limits:* Interpretation by this track; P35 is a single historical case on fragmentary sources.
