# Insurgence — operationalizations and competing definitions

Status: Proposed working definitions for the GAIA Phase axis (Research/README.md §9.3, §12 Q1, Q4). Each field keeps its own meaning; no single cross-field definition is assumed. None of the sources uses the word "insurgence"; the mapping is this track's. Competing definitions are recorded, not resolved.

| field | working operationalization (this track) | competing or neighbouring definitions | sourced? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural sciences | a new state rising from a small seed when a parameter crosses a threshold (transcritical exchange of stability) | nucleation; ignition; onset of convection or lasing; invasion of a mutant | P22 (transcritical) |
| Formal sciences | phase transition: sudden emergence of a giant component at a critical mean degree | percolation threshold; branching-process survival; sharp thresholds for graph properties | P27 |
| Social sciences | threshold-triggered uprising (riots becoming revolution) with multiple outcomes | insurgency as armed rebellion; Granovetter threshold cascades; preference falsification (Kuran, as reported in P25) | P25 |
| Applied sciences | outbreak emergence when each case causes more than one new case (R0 > 1) | effective reproduction number R(t); cascading failure in engineered systems | P26 |
| Humanities | a rare style rising rapidly to dominance | artistic avant-gardes; linguistic innovation spreading in an S-curve | P21 |
| Philosophy / spirituality | the upsurge of the new from a common impulse (Bergson's élan vital) | emergence (philosophy of mind and science); religious revival and prophetic movements | P08 (interpretive) |

## Cross-field observations (Baseline)
- **Threshold plus amplification** recurs: exchange of stability at r = 0, giant component at c = 1, outbreaks at R0 = 1, uprisings at a rebel-to-police ratio n.
- **Most beginnings fail** even above threshold: introductions die out (branching), single new styles are lost (drift), riots stay riots (P25). PL-21, Hypothetical.
- **The same equation** s = 1 - exp(-cs) gives the giant-component fraction and the major-outbreak probability (PL-20).

## Cross-field observations (x3)
- **Bottleneck latency**: near a saddle-node threshold the time to break out scales as distance^(-1/2) in both the NAT normal form and P28's model of semantic change (PL-23, Plausible).
- **Critical mass**: with strong conformity a preferred stance needs a minimum share (P30's model, a > 1) and threshold cascades hinge on single individuals (PL-24, Hypothetical).
- **Thresholds blur under noise and heterogeneity**: noise removes the sharp HUM threshold (P28), rare large chains occur just below R = 1, and superspreading makes most introductions fail even at high R0.
- Competing definitions added: semantic expansion with latency (HUM, P28); conversion between groups (PHI, P30); effective reproduction number (APP, P26).

## Cross-field observations (x6)
- **Critical nucleus / critical mass**: classical nucleation (P37) gives a size threshold with near-even odds at the top; P30's model with a > 1 gives a critical mass of adherents (PL-25, Hypothetical).
- **Concentration**: a few superspreaders (P32) and a few targeted pillars (P31) decide whether a beginning spreads (PL-26, Hypothetical).
- **Push versus imitation**: P29 separates exogenous and endogenous adoption from the shape of the start of an S-curve.
- Competing definitions added: nucleation (NAT, P37); critical window (FOR, P36); nonviolent defection-driven revolution (SOC, P31); superspreading (APP, P32); exogenous vs endogenous adoption (HUM, P29); prophetic movement (PHI, P35).
