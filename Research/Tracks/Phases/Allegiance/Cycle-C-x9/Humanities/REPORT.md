## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALC-HUM-01** [P] P50 fits language-competition models with prestige and bilingualism to public population data for six bilingual cases (e.g. Gaelic-English 1891-1971, Welsh-English 1901-2001); Bayes factors favour the model with bilingualism, which decelerates the shift from minority to majority language, and in the four unequal-prestige cases the majority language has higher prestige (e.g. Spanish-English in the USA, s2 = 1.2553 > s1 = 0.0691). *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = language loyalty under prestige pressure. *Sources:* P50. *Limits:* Six cases; model rates are estimates; P50 has small slips (cites figure 1a for results in figure 2; 'minority Catalonia').
- **ALC-HUM-02** [P] Plugging P50's reported rates into its constant-rate Eq. 4.4 gives positive minority equilibria in all four unequal-prestige cases (Canada 0.186, USA 0.081, Wales 0.018, Scotland 0.061), and the prestige-weighted flow runs minority-to-majority in each (USA 0.134 vs 0.012, although r12 < r21); by contrast, in P30's exponent form the weaker group survives only for a < 1 (0.31 at a = 0.5, 0 at a = 1 and 1.31). *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = persistence of a minority language. *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-0/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-0/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Agent's simplification: rates estimated in P50's bilingual model are used in a model without bilinguals; not a re-fit.
- **ALC-HUM-03** [P] Counterfactual: giving both languages the same prestige (the mean of P50's two values) raises the minority equilibrium in every case (Canada 0.19 to 0.41, Wales 0.02 to 0.22, Scotland 0.06 to 0.07) and in the USA reverses the flow (majority-to-minority 0.114 vs 0.071; Spanish equilibrium 0.62), so the minority-to-majority prediction is not supported in every case: in P50's rates the USA shift is driven by prestige, not by the transition rates. *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = language loyalty without a prestige gap. *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Counterfactual in the agent's simplified model without bilinguals; not a forecast.
- **ALC-HUM-04** [P] In P30's form at status 0.3 the weaker group kept 0.24 at a = 0.25 but only 0.033 at a = 0.75 and vanished at a = 1.5 and 2: the AL0 rule 'coexistence for a < 1' is not supported at the 0.05 level near a = 1, because the surviving share shrinks continuously as a approaches 1. *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = minority persistence in the exponent form. *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-2#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-2#H3. *Limits:* Deterministic; the 0.05 coexistence threshold is the agent's.
- **ALC-HUM-05** [P] Replication with step 0.001: the Eq. 4.4 equilibria (0.186, 0.081, 0.018, 0.061) and the exponent-form results are unchanged. *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = persistence of a minority language (replication). *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-3#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-A/iter-3#H3. *Limits:* Numerical replication only.
- **ALC-HUM-06** [P] In P50's model the minority's equilibrium depends only on the ratio of rates: halving all rates leaves it unchanged; doubling the majority's prestige lowers it to 0.10, 0.04, 0.009, 0.03 (Wales below 0.01: not supported as surviving); raising the minority's prestige to the majority's raises it to 0.41, 0.62, 0.22, 0.07, and in the USA the flow then reverses (not supported in every case). *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = language loyalty and prestige. *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-1#H3;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-2#H3;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-6#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-6#H3. *Limits:* Counterfactuals in the agent's model without bilinguals.
- **ALC-HUM-07** [P] In P30's exponent form near a = 1 the weaker group keeps 0.017 at a = 0.9 and 0.0003 at a = 0.95 and vanishes above 1, so coexistence above 0.05 for a < 1 is not supported near the threshold; at status 0.45 it keeps 0.40 at a = 0.5; a coarse step (0.05) reproduces the equilibria. *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = minority persistence near the conformity threshold. *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-3#H3;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-4#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-4#H3;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-5#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-B/iter-5#H3. *Limits:* Deterministic toy; threshold 0.05 the agent's.
- **ALC-HUM-08** [P] A scoping review of Chinese heritage-language maintenance in Inner Circle countries (28 studies, over 80% from the US and Australia) finds that research equates Chinese with Mandarin, so non-officialised varieties such as Cantonese are under-studied, and none of the studies is set in formal government schools. P60 proposes a three-party game of a dominant language, an officialised and a non-officialised heritage language, while stating that such a model requires primary empirical data. *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = loyalty to a non-official heritage language. *Sources:* P60. *Limits:* Scoping review (no quality appraisal); slips: 'chronically organized', a Germany study counted among Inner Circle countries, funding statement inconsistent.
- **ALC-HUM-10** [P] In P50's model, halving the minority's prestige drops Wales to 0.009 (survival above 0.01 not supported in every case); a strongly asymmetric P30 status (0.3) still allows coexistence below a = 1; doubling all rates leaves the equilibria unchanged. *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = language loyalty under prestige. *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-7#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-7#H3;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-8#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-8#H3;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-9#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-9#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-9#H3. *Limits:* Counterfactuals in the agent's model without bilinguals; largely an empty pass.
- **ALC-HUM-09** [H] In the agent's three-language Abrams-Strogatz toy of P60's 'three-party game', with exponent 1.31 the dominant language always wins and the non-officialised language vanished first (t 10.4 vs 12.2) when it was lowest in status. When it had higher status but a smaller share, it still vanished first (t 10.1 vs 11.8), so 'the lower-status one goes first' is not supported: share mattered more than status. Below the exponent 1 (a = 0.8) the smallest language still fell to 0.001 by T 200, so coexistence of all three is not supported. At a = 1 the highest-status language won from equal shares, and with equal status the majority won on frequency. *Operationalization:* HUM Allegiance = which heritage language loses first. *Sources:* SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-3#H2;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-5#H1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-AL-HUM-C/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Hypothetical toy; statuses and exponents are the agent's; no data (P60 itself says data are needed).
