## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALC-SOC-01** [P] Identity fusion is a visceral, near-irrevocable sense of oneness in which personal and social identities overlap completely; it arises through shared biology or shared (often dysphoric) experiences and predicts willingness to fight and die for the group; P48 proposes, from labelling theory, a loop in which external condemnation amplifies fusion to a deviant group, which in turn leads to more deviant behaviour. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = identity fusion with a group. *Sources:* P48. *Limits:* P48 is a conceptual proposal for criminology; the loop is a hypothesis it raises, and it notes research has yet to show how individuals de-fuse and that fusion to large groups has sometimes decreased (tension with 'irrevocable').
- **ALC-SOC-03** [P] In three samples (Palestinians in Gaza, N = 333; Muslims in Turkiye after the Charlie Hebdo cartoons, N = 382; football fans in Turkiye, N = 413) P52 finds that, with both in the model, group identification goes with peaceful pro-group action and identity fusion with violent pro-group action (SEM betas .50, .31, .38); in Gaza fusion was also negatively related to a peaceful solution, and among football fans fusion was more strongly related to peaceful than to violent action. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = fusion versus identification. *Sources:* P52. *Limits:* Cross-sectional, so no causal inference (P52 says so); intentions, not behaviour. Slips: the Study 2 discussion says group identification in Study 1 was negatively associated with a peaceful solution (the Study 1 results report this for fusion); Table 2 skips number 3; Study 3 Model 1 gives identical betas and CIs (.50 [0.17, 0.84]) for both outcomes while calling one stronger.
- **ALC-SOC-04** [P] Two-predictor regressions computed from P52's printed correlation tables reproduce the sign of 'identification -> peaceful' in all three studies (0.16, 0.31, 0.30), but 'fusion -> violent' is reproduced only in Studies 1 and 2 (0.38, 0.27); in Study 3 the printed correlations (fusion-violent .21, identification-violent .31, fusion-identification .82) give fusion -> violent = -0.13 (-0.23 disattenuated), against P52's SEM path +.38 (not supported). Disattenuation flipped one near-zero coefficient (Study 2 identification -> violent, 0.012 to -0.015; H3 not supported). *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = re-checking fusion effects from printed statistics. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Manifest regression on scale-score correlations is not P52's latent SEM with item parcels, and the agent cannot see P52's item-level data (available on OSF, not read); the Study 3 discrepancy is reported, not explained.
- **ALC-SOC-07** [P] Correlations computed from P52's own OSF data match its printed tables within 0.005 in Studies 1 and 2, but not in Study 3 (0.10; not supported); swapping the fusion and identification entries of the printed Study 3 table matches the data within 0.004, so ALA's unreproducible Study 3 path is a transposition in Table 3. P52's Study 3 SEM also has a Heywood case (Mplus: 'THE RESIDUAL COVARIANCE MATRIX (THETA) IS NOT POSITIVE DEFINITE', variable CENTRAL), and its implied latent correlations keep fusion-violent (0.31) above identification-violent (0.21) but differ from the disattenuated raw values by up to 0.10 (not supported within 0.05). *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = auditing a fusion study against its data. *Sources:* P57;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Scale means use the items P52's Mplus models used (6 of 7 peaceful, 8 of 11 violent in Study 3). Raw data have no stated licence and are not redistributed. P52's identification-only Model 5 output on OSF is an Mplus error ('Unexpected end of file').
- **ALC-SOC-08** [P] With correlations from P52's raw data, fusion predicts violent action positively when identification is controlled in all three studies (0.37, 0.28, 0.43) and identification predicts peaceful action positively (0.15, 0.32, 0.34), as P52's SEM reports; only Study 2's near-zero identification-to-violent coefficient (0.012) changes sign when disattenuated (-0.044), so 'disattenuation changes no sign' is not supported. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = fusion versus identification from raw data. *Sources:* P57;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#H3. *Limits:* Alphas used for disattenuation are computed from the raw items, not printed; the H3 wording says 'printed alphas' (disclosed). Foreknowledge by hand arithmetic.
- **ALC-SOC-10** [P] P61 reports a 'bright side' of identity fusion: in Study 1 (N = 609) fusion correlated with group efficacy (.75) and intergroup prosocial intention (.40), with efficacy partly mediating; in Study 2 (N = 72) activating the social self raised fusion (F(2, 69) = 5.94) and prosocial intention (F = 30.67, eta squared .47). *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = fusion and prosociality. *Sources:* P61. *Limits:* Cross-sectional mediation; small experiment; slips: a direct-effect CI labelled 'partial mediation', unspecified post hoc test for fusion, shares that sum to 0.20 of 0.21.

- **ALC-SOC-12** [P] P61's post-test fusion F (5.93 vs 5.94) is reproduced, and social > control survives Tukey HSD (3.79 > 2.69), but P61's 'social > personal' (2.54) is not supported by Tukey HSD (critical difference 2.69); P61 does not name the test. - **ALC-SOC-02** [H] Formalizing P48's labelling loop as dF/dt = a k D (1 - F) - d F, dD/dt = b F - g D gives a threshold at loop gain R = a k b/(g d) = 1: below it fusion dies out (R = 0.4: F = 0), above it a fused state F* = 1 - 1/R persists (R = 2: 0.50; R = 4: 0.75); lowering condemnation below the threshold dissolves it, and with condemnation removed fusion halves in ln2/d (13.9 time units). *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = self-reinforcing fusion under condemnation. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* The equations are the agent's formalization of a verbal hypothesis; parameters arbitrary.
- **ALC-SOC-05** [H] In the fusion-loop toy, making fusion ten times slower to decay (delta 0.005) leaves the threshold and fused levels unchanged (R = 0.4, 2, 4 gives 0, 0.50, 0.75) but stretches the half-life after condemnation stops to 138.7 time units (ln2/delta = 138.6): 'irrevocability' in this toy is slowness, not permanence. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = near-irrevocable fusion. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Agent's toy; parameters arbitrary.
- **ALC-SOC-06** [H] Around the threshold the toy's fused state appears continuously: F = 0 at R = 0.9, 0.091 at R = 1.1 and 0.333 at R = 1.5 (F* = 1 - 1/R), so in this formalization fusion grows gradually with stigma rather than switching on abruptly. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = threshold of the fusion loop. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* A different (e.g. sigmoidal) amplification would give an abrupt switch; the form is the agent's choice.
