## Reading of this cycle
x9: P54 balanced allegiance in its steering group; its null is underpowered for small differences.

## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALC-APP-01** [E] Researcher allegiance, 'a belief in the superiority of a treatment', was coded in 50 RCTs comparing humanistic with other psychotherapies; at end of treatment allegiance against humanistic therapy was not a significant moderator (beta = -0.183, SE 0.125, p = .144, N = 47), only 10% of studies showed allegiance in favour of humanistic therapy, and allegiance was significant in 2 of 5 comparisons; the authors conclude allegiance 'was not as relevant as expected' while noting absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = researcher allegiance in clinical trials. *Sources:* P49. *Limits:* One meta-analysis of one therapy family; allegiance coded from publications.
- **ALC-APP-02** [P] With P49's own estimate (beta = -0.183, SE = 0.125) the reported p was reproduced (0.143) and the two-sided power to detect a moderator of that size at alpha 0.05 was only 0.31; a simulated 47-study random-effects meta-regression gave SE 0.124 and power 0.33. P49's null for allegiance is therefore weak evidence of no effect, as P49 itself cautions; by chance alone, at least 2 of 5 independent null tests would be significant 2.3% of the time. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = power to detect allegiance effects. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-APP-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-APP-0/iter-1#H2. *Limits:* Simulated design (moderator share 0.3, tau 0.25, study SE 0.2-0.4) assumed; the five comparisons are not independent in P49.
- **ALC-APP-03** [P] With more heterogeneity (tau 0.4 instead of 0.25) the simulated 47-study meta-regression has SE 0.161 and power 0.22 for P49's moderator size, so the AL0 match to P49's SE 0.125 is not supported under this setting; P49's reported SE fits low-to-moderate heterogeneity in this design. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = power under heterogeneity. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-1#H2. *Limits:* Design values (study SEs 0.2-0.4, moderator share 0.3) are the agent's.
- **ALC-APP-04** [P] Doubling the number of studies to K = 100 lowers the moderator SE to 0.085 and raises power to 0.58 (H2, designed for K = 47, not supported as expected): even a meta-analysis twice the size of P49's would miss a true allegiance effect of P49's estimated size about 4 times in 10. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = studies needed to detect allegiance. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-2#H2. *Limits:* Simple scaling design; real meta-analyses have correlated moderators.
- **ALC-APP-05** [P] If only 10% of studies fall in the moderator category, the SE rises to 0.194 and power falls to 0.18 (H2 not supported): unbalanced allegiance coding, as P49's 10% figure suggests is common, makes allegiance effects even harder to detect. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = power with unbalanced allegiance. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-APP-A/iter-3#H2. *Limits:* The mapping of P49's 10% to the moderator category is illustrative.
- **ALC-APP-06** [P] In a meta-analysis of 244 randomized trials of parenting programs (1,100 effect sizes, 28,916 families), the overall effect on disruptive behaviour was d = -0.36 [-0.42, -0.31]; effects declined in the first decades and stabilized around 2000. Developer involvement, a form of researcher allegiance, was not associated with effect size (-0.002 [-0.12, 0.11]) though it became less common over time, while active control conditions (0.17 [0.07, 0.27]) and preregistration were associated with smaller effects. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = developer involvement in trials. *Sources:* P56. *Limits:* Observational moderator analysis; one field (parenting programs). Slips: 'Observational outcome 11.46 [-16.26, -6.66]' (sign inconsistent), '22 databases' vs '26 international databases'.
- **ALC-APP-07** [P] Unlike P49's null for allegiance, P56's null for developer involvement is informative: its CI lies within +/-0.15 and it had power 0.93 for an effect of 0.2 (0.40 for 0.1), whereas P49's had power 0.36 for 0.2 and a CI reaching -0.43 (both checks not supported). P56's active-control moderator is a detected effect (p 0.0009, power 0.98 for 0.2); its delivery-staff moderator is borderline (p 0.055 by normal approximation, not supported as significant, though P56's CI excludes 0). *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = how informative an allegiance null is. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-4#H2. *Limits:* Normal approximation to P56's robust-variance CIs; asymmetry of the delivery-staff CI shows the approximation's limit.
- **ALC-APP-08** [P] With low heterogeneity (tau 0.1) the 47-study design gives SE 0.100 and power 0.46, not supported as matching P49's SE 0.125; with a balanced moderator (pi 0.5) and tau 0.25 SE is 0.113 and power 0.37, within the check. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = power under alternative designs. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-5#H2;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-AL-APP-B/iter-6#H2. *Limits:* Design values are the agent's.
- **ALC-APP-09** [P] In a trial of short-term psychodynamic psychotherapy versus CBT for depression (N = 100) run by a steering group of both CBT and STPP therapists to lower allegiance bias, there was no significant difference in HDRS change (-1.50 [-4.25, 1.26], p 0.283) or BDI (-0.12 [-0.76, 0.53]); between-group d 0.27 (HDRS), 0.14 (BDI), 0.39 (WSAS); 81 were evaluated at 28 weeks. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = an allegiance-balanced head-to-head trial. *Sources:* P54. *Limits:* Underpowered for small differences (see ALC-APP-10); slips: Table 4 '0.14 (1.00)', 'Cohens d', '23 participants' vs '23%'.
- **ALC-APP-10** [P] P54's stated power 0.84 for d 0.6 with N 100 is confirmed (0.844), but its power for the observed HDRS d of 0.27 was 0.27, and for d 0.4 it was 0.51 (below 0.5 not supported, borderline). With the 81 evaluated at 28 weeks power for d 0.6 falls to 0.76 (>= 0.8 not supported). P61's pairwise contrasts (24 per group) had power 0.77 for d 0.8 (>= 0.8 not supported) and 0.40 for d 0.5. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = what an allegiance-balanced trial can detect. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-2#H2;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-3#H2;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-4#H1. *Limits:* Two-sample t approximation; P54 used mixed models on all 100 patients.
- **ALC-APP-11** [P] P54's HDRS null is not informative at a 3-point margin: its CI reaches -4.25 (within +/-3 not supported) and power for 3 points was 0.57 (not supported). Its BDI CI lies within +/-1 with power 0.86 for 1 unit, so on the printed scale it does exclude 1-unit differences. The log odds-ratio run failed validation (the estimate is 0.011 off the CI midpoint on the log scale), so it is indeterminate; for the record its CI included log 2 and power was 0.33. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = how informative an allegiance-balanced null is. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-5#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-5#H2;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-6#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-6#H2;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-7#H2. *Limits:* Normal approximation; BDI units as printed (not re-derived); 3 HDRS points is the agent's margin.
- **ALC-APP-12** [P] Doubling P49's design to 94 studies gives SE 0.087 and power 0.57; with a rare moderator (pi 0.15) SE is 0.165 and power 0.21; both are not supported as matching P49's SE 0.125, as expected by construction. *Operationalization:* APP Allegiance = power under alternative designs. *Sources:* SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-8#H2;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-9#H1;SIM-AL-APP-C/iter-9#H2. *Limits:* Design values are the agent's.
