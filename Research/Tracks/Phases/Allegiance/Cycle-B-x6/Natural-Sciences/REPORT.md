## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALB-NAT-01** [E] Cooperative binding is described by the Hill equation Y = L^n/(Kd + L^n), with Kd = P50^n, and the Hill coefficient n is the slope of the Hill plot log(Y/(1-Y)) vs log L; for haemoglobin a fit gives n = 2.8, but the real Hill plot is curvilinear: its ends approach slope 1 and its slope is highest in the middle, so cooperativity is maximal near half-saturation. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding (each bond makes the next more likely). *Sources:* P46. *Limits:* Textbook page; the Hill equation is an approximation (P46 notes it is not a mechanism). P46 contains a slip ('log 1 = log 100 = 0') and typos; reading binding as 'allegiance' is this track's operationalization.
- **ALB-NAT-03** [E] In the MWC model haemoglobin switches between a low-affinity T and a high-affinity R quaternary state (approximately K_T 0.001 and K_R 1 per Torr, L = [T0]/[R0] about 5 x 10^5); binding within each state is non-cooperative, and cooperativity comes from the shift from T to R as oxygen binds. After a 30-year controversy, single-crystal and gel experiments confirmed non-cooperative binding to T; MWC's failure is that protons and 2,3-DPG change K_T as well as L, and the tertiary two-state (TTS) model adds tertiary conformations within each quaternary state. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding through a shared conformational switch. *Sources:* P53. *Limits:* Historical review with simplifications the author acknowledges; slips: 'delta T = delta T = 1' (should pair delta T and delta R), 'confromation', 'when oxygen bounds'.
- **ALB-NAT-02** [P] A four-site Adair model with stepwise constants rising 100-fold (0.02, 0.05, 0.2, 2.0) reproduced P46's qualitative Hill plot: end slopes 1.0001, maximal slope 3.63 at log L = 0.883, 0.03 log units from half-saturation (0.853); independent sites gave slope 1.000 throughout, and the Hill equation gave Y = 0.5 at P50 with Kd = P50^n. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Constants are the agent's choice, not haemoglobin data; Adair's polynomial is standard theory not stated in P46.
- **ALB-NAT-04** [P] With P53's approximate MWC values the Hill plot has end slopes 1.000 and P50 26 Torr, and binding within T alone or R alone has slope 1 (within 0.0004), but the maximal Hill slope is 3.64, not within 0.5 of P46's 2.8 (not supported), and changing only L from 5 x 10^4 to 5 x 10^6 changed it by only 0.08 (3.56-3.64; not supported): near L c^2 = 1 the maximal slope is insensitive to L. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = MWC cooperativity. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* P53's values are order-of-magnitude ('~'); P46's 2.8 is a fit to real Hb. Both outcomes were known from the smoke test.
- **ALB-NAT-05** [P] With P53's approximate TTS values the maximal Hill slope is 2.55, within 0.5 of 2.8, with end slopes 1 and non-cooperative binding inside each quaternary state; unlike MWC near its optimum, the TTS maximal slope falls steadily as L rises (2.88, 2.55, 1.94 for L = 10^5, 10^6, 10^7), so allosteric effectors that raise L also lower cooperativity, in line with P53's point that n depends on conditions. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding with tertiary switches. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-2#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-2#H3. *Limits:* The fraction layout of Eq. (13) is lost in the extracted text; the agent's reading (T term scaled by L / l_T^4) is disclosed.
- **ALB-NAT-06** [P] Replication of AL0-NAT-02 with different Adair constants (0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 1.0): end slopes 1.0001, maximal slope 3.02 at 0.007 log units from half-saturation, independent sites slope 1.000. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding (replication). *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-3#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-3#H3. *Limits:* Agent-chosen constants; replication only.
- **ALB-NAT-07** [P] For ligand-gated channels with two to five binding sites, P55 shows on simulated data (noise 0.25 R Po(1 - Po), seven concentrations, five points each) that all equilibrium constants of a cyclic model can be identified only by concatenating subunits, mutating every binding site and fitting all concentration-activation curves globally; identifiability is best with five sites and 41 parameters, and the first and last constants are less precise than the intermediate ones. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = identifiability of cooperative binding constants. *Sources:* P55. *Limits:* Simulated data only; slips recorded ('CNA 5', 'heteroterameric', 'LGIC 2 s, LGIC 4 s', a Fig. 8 legend citing Eq. 21 for Eq. 22).
- **ALB-NAT-08** [P] Fitting one Adair binding curve (constants 0.02, 0.05, 0.2, 2.0) with P55's noise and design, the maximal Hill slope is well determined (3.64, CV 0.022) but the constants are not (SD of log10 K 1.3 to 3.6); the end constants are better determined than the middle ones, so P55's end-versus-middle pattern is not supported for a single curve. With a tenth of the noise the slope CV falls to 0.003 and the end constants are determined to 0.04-0.06 log units, but the middle two still only to 0.73. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = what one cooperative curve can reveal. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-2#H2. *Limits:* Agent's single-curve setting; P55 fits many mutant curves jointly, so this is not a test of P55. Smoke foreknowledge for iter-1.
- **ALB-NAT-09** [P] With 21 concentrations the noise-free refit failed validation (log K error 82.8; the optimiser did not return to the true constants), so this run is indeterminate; for the record its H1 (end constants less precise) was not supported and the slope CV was 0.013. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = identifiability with more data (indeterminate). *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-3#H2. *Limits:* V1 Fail: results not interpretable; cause (likely optimiser start or a flat direction) not investigated in ALB; no rerun with changed settings.
- **ALB-NAT-10** [P] In the MWC model the maximal Hill slope peaks at L c^2 = 1: with c = 0.001 it is 3.64 at L = 10^6 and 2.57 at L = 10^2 or 10^10 (H2 near 2.8 not supported at the optimum); with a weaker affinity contrast (c = 0.01) the peak is 3.01 at L = 10^4 and 2.47 two decades away, within 0.5 of P46's 2.8 for haemoglobin. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = MWC cooperativity versus L and c. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-4#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-4#H3;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-5#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-5#H3. *Limits:* Agent's parameter choices; not fitted haemoglobin values (still unsourced).
- **ALB-NAT-11** [P] In the TTS model a weaker tertiary bias in T (l_T 50 instead of 200) lowers the maximal Hill slope from 2.55 to 1.44 (not supported as near 2.8), and it falls further as L rises (2.05, 1.44, 1.08 for L = 10^5 to 10^7). *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = TTS cooperativity versus tertiary bias. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-6#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-B/iter-6#H3. *Limits:* Agent's variant of P53's approximate values.
