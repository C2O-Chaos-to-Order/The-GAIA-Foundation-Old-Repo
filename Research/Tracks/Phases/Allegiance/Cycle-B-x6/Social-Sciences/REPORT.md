## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALB-SOC-01** [P] Identity fusion is a visceral, near-irrevocable sense of oneness in which personal and social identities overlap completely; it arises through shared biology or shared (often dysphoric) experiences and predicts willingness to fight and die for the group; P48 proposes, from labelling theory, a loop in which external condemnation amplifies fusion to a deviant group, which in turn leads to more deviant behaviour. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = identity fusion with a group. *Sources:* P48. *Limits:* P48 is a conceptual proposal for criminology; the loop is a hypothesis it raises, and it notes research has yet to show how individuals de-fuse and that fusion to large groups has sometimes decreased (tension with 'irrevocable').
- **ALB-SOC-03** [P] In three samples (Palestinians in Gaza, N = 333; Muslims in Turkiye after the Charlie Hebdo cartoons, N = 382; football fans in Turkiye, N = 413) P52 finds that, with both in the model, group identification goes with peaceful pro-group action and identity fusion with violent pro-group action (SEM betas .50, .31, .38); in Gaza fusion was also negatively related to a peaceful solution, and among football fans fusion was more strongly related to peaceful than to violent action. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = fusion versus identification. *Sources:* P52. *Limits:* Cross-sectional, so no causal inference (P52 says so); intentions, not behaviour. Slips: the Study 2 discussion says group identification in Study 1 was negatively associated with a peaceful solution (the Study 1 results report this for fusion); Table 2 skips number 3; Study 3 Model 1 gives identical betas and CIs (.50 [0.17, 0.84]) for both outcomes while calling one stronger.
- **ALB-SOC-04** [P] Two-predictor regressions computed from P52's printed correlation tables reproduce the sign of 'identification -> peaceful' in all three studies (0.16, 0.31, 0.30), but 'fusion -> violent' is reproduced only in Studies 1 and 2 (0.38, 0.27); in Study 3 the printed correlations (fusion-violent .21, identification-violent .31, fusion-identification .82) give fusion -> violent = -0.13 (-0.23 disattenuated), against P52's SEM path +.38 (not supported). Disattenuation flipped one near-zero coefficient (Study 2 identification -> violent, 0.012 to -0.015; H3 not supported). *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = re-checking fusion effects from printed statistics. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Manifest regression on scale-score correlations is not P52's latent SEM with item parcels, and the agent cannot see P52's item-level data (available on OSF, not read); the Study 3 discrepancy is reported, not explained.
- **ALB-SOC-07** [P] Correlations computed from P52's own OSF data match its printed tables within 0.005 in Studies 1 and 2, but not in Study 3 (0.10; not supported); swapping the fusion and identification entries of the printed Study 3 table matches the data within 0.004, so ALA's unreproducible Study 3 path is a transposition in Table 3. - **ALB-SOC-08** [P] With correlations from P52's raw data, fusion predicts violent action positively when identification is controlled in all three studies (0.37, 0.28, 0.43) and identification predicts peaceful action positively (0.15, 0.32, 0.34), as P52's SEM reports; only Study 2's near-zero identification-to-violent coefficient (0.012) changes sign when disattenuated (-0.044), so 'disattenuation changes no sign' is not supported. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = fusion versus identification from raw data. *Sources:* P57;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-2#H3. *Limits:* Alphas used for disattenuation are computed from the raw items, not printed; the H3 wording says 'printed alphas' (disclosed). Foreknowledge by hand arithmetic.
- **ALB-SOC-02** [H] Formalizing P48's labelling loop as dF/dt = a k D (1 - F) - d F, dD/dt = b F - g D gives a threshold at loop gain R = a k b/(g d) = 1: below it fusion dies out (R = 0.4: F = 0), above it a fused state F* = 1 - 1/R persists (R = 2: 0.50; R = 4: 0.75); lowering condemnation below the threshold dissolves it, and with condemnation removed fusion halves in ln2/d (13.9 time units). *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = self-reinforcing fusion under condemnation. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* The equations are the agent's formalization of a verbal hypothesis; parameters arbitrary.
- **ALB-SOC-05** [H] In the fusion-loop toy, making fusion ten times slower to decay (delta 0.005) leaves the threshold and fused levels unchanged (R = 0.4, 2, 4 gives 0, 0.50, 0.75) but stretches the half-life after condemnation stops to 138.7 time units (ln2/delta = 138.6): 'irrevocability' in this toy is slowness, not permanence. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = near-irrevocable fusion. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Agent's toy; parameters arbitrary.
- **ALB-SOC-06** [H] Around the threshold the toy's fused state appears continuously: F = 0 at R = 0.9, 0.091 at R = 1.1 and 0.333 at R = 1.5 (F* = 1 - 1/R), so in this formalization fusion grows gradually with stigma rather than switching on abruptly. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = threshold of the fusion loop. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* A different (e.g. sigmoidal) amplification would give an abrupt switch; the form is the agent's choice.
- **ALB-SOC-09** [H] In the fusion-loop toy the threshold at R = 1 and the fixed points (0.5 at R = 2, 0.75 at R = 4) are unchanged by faster fusion decay, stronger deviance response or slower deviance decay; only the half-life after the loop stops changes (ln 2 / delta). Just above threshold (R = 1.02) the fused state reaches 0.018 of its 0.0196 fixed point after T = 3000, showing slow approach near the threshold. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = fusion loop sensitivity. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-B/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Hypothetical toy (P48 qualitative).
