## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALD-SOC-01** [P] Identity fusion is a visceral, near-irrevocable sense of oneness in which personal and social identities overlap completely; it arises through shared biology or shared (often dysphoric) experiences and predicts willingness to fight and die for the group; P48 proposes, from labelling theory, a loop in which external condemnation amplifies fusion to a deviant group, which in turn leads to more deviant behaviour. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = identity fusion with a group. *Sources:* P48. *Limits:* P48 is a conceptual proposal for criminology; the loop is a hypothesis it raises, and it notes research has yet to show how individuals de-fuse and that fusion to large groups has sometimes decreased (tension with 'irrevocable').
- **ALD-SOC-03** [P] In three samples (Palestinians in Gaza, N = 333; Muslims in Turkiye after the Charlie Hebdo cartoons, N = 382; football fans in Turkiye, N = 413) P52 finds that, with both in the model, group identification goes with peaceful pro-group action and identity fusion with violent pro-group action (SEM betas .50, .31, .38); in Gaza fusion was also negatively related to a peaceful solution, and among football fans fusion was more strongly related to peaceful than to violent action. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = fusion versus identification. *Sources:* P52. *Limits:* Cross-sectional, so no causal inference (P52 says so); intentions, not behaviour. Slips: the Study 2 discussion says group identification in Study 1 was negatively associated with a peaceful solution (the Study 1 results report this for fusion); Table 2 skips number 3; Study 3 Model 1 gives identical betas and CIs (.50 [0.17, 0.84]) for both outcomes while calling one stronger.

- **ALD-SOC-14** [P] The first identity-fusion meta-analysis (90 studies from 55 reports, 106 effects, N = 36,880) finds a strong average association between fusion and extreme pro-group orientations (r .495 [.461, .528], I^2 90.6; PEESE-corrected .46), stronger than identification (.37) in matched studies; the fusion-over-identification advantage holds in published (B .24 [.14, .33]) but not unpublished studies (B -.01 [-.14, .12]). - **ALD-SOC-02** [H] Formalizing P48's labelling loop as dF/dt = a k D (1 - F) - d F, dD/dt = b F - g D gives a threshold at loop gain R = a k b/(g d) = 1: below it fusion dies out (R = 0.4: F = 0), above it a fused state F* = 1 - 1/R persists (R = 2: 0.50; R = 4: 0.75); lowering condemnation below the threshold dissolves it, and with condemnation removed fusion halves in ln2/d (13.9 time units). *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = self-reinforcing fusion under condemnation. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* The equations are the agent's formalization of a verbal hypothesis; parameters arbitrary.
- **ALD-SOC-05** [H] In the fusion-loop toy, making fusion ten times slower to decay (delta 0.005) leaves the threshold and fused levels unchanged (R = 0.4, 2, 4 gives 0, 0.50, 0.75) but stretches the half-life after condemnation stops to 138.7 time units (ln2/delta = 138.6): 'irrevocability' in this toy is slowness, not permanence. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = near-irrevocable fusion. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Agent's toy; parameters arbitrary.
- **ALD-SOC-06** [H] Around the threshold the toy's fused state appears continuously: F = 0 at R = 0.9, 0.091 at R = 1.1 and 0.333 at R = 1.5 (F* = 1 - 1/R), so in this formalization fusion grows gradually with stigma rather than switching on abruptly. *Operationalization:* SOC Allegiance = threshold of the fusion loop. *Sources:* SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-SOC-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* A different (e.g. sigmoidal) amplification would give an abrupt switch; the form is the agent's choice.
