# Allegiance — operationalizations and competing definitions

Status: Proposed working definitions for the GAIA Phase axis (Research/README.md §9.3, §12 Q1, Q4). No repository document defines "Allegiance" (Research/README.md only lists it as a phase), so each field's mapping below is this track's choice; only P49 (researcher allegiance) and P51 (allegiance as a medieval oath of fealty, within loyalty) use the word. Competing definitions are recorded, not resolved.

Owner context (recorded 2026-10-24; does not change methods): the owner maps phases to upgrade levels (Base = Divergence, Meta = Insurgence, Super = Allegiance, Hyper = Convergence, Ultra = Ascendence, Magic = Transcendence). "Allegiance corresponds to Super / Superphysical" is the owner's **Hypothetical** claim; it is not tested here.

## Cross-field observations (Baseline)
- **Positive feedback makes attachments steep and sticky**: cooperative binding (NAT), the fusion loop (SOC toy) and conformity-like language dynamics show threshold-like responses (PL-31, Hypothetical).
- **Continuation conditions**: reciprocity needs a high chance of another round (P47) and loyalty keeps members long enough for voice to work (P51) (PL-32, Hypothetical).
- **Weak evidence is not absence**: P49's null for researcher allegiance had about 31% power.

## Cross-field observations (x3)
- **Shared switches**: cooperativity in haemoglobin comes from a T-to-R switch with non-cooperative binding inside each state (MWC, P53); in the TTS refinement cooperativity weakens as the switch is biased toward T.
- **Sample size decides verdicts**: the 1/3 rule needs weak selection (FOR) and allegiance effects need far more studies than P49 had (APP) (PL-34, Plausible).
- **Two forms of group bonding**: identification goes with peaceful action and fusion with violent action (P52), though one of P52's key paths is not reproducible from its printed correlations (PL-35).
- **Loyalty has conditions**: in toy models loyalty helps only when decline is recoverable, shocks are modest and loyalty exceeds a minimum.
- Competing definitions added: allosteric switching (NAT, P53); group identification as distinct from fusion (SOC, P52).

## Cross-field observations (x6)
- **Summaries can be precise while parts are not**: one binding curve fixes the maximal Hill slope but not the Adair constants (NAT, P55), as a pooled effect can be precise while moderators are not (APP) (PL-37, Plausible).
- **Checking printed numbers changes conclusions**: P52's unreproducible Study 3 path is a transposed table in its own data, and P56's null for developer involvement, unlike P49's, could have detected an effect of 0.2 (PL-36, Plausible).
- **Finite-population corrections are now sourced**: the one-third law's finite-N condition (P58) matches the runs.
- **Forfeiture**: loyalty is forfeited when its object proves unworthy or cannot be recuperated (P59); fusion, by contrast, goes with violent readiness (PL-38, Hypothetical).
- Competing definitions added: loyalty as an executive virtue that can be forfeited (PHI, P59); developer involvement as a form of researcher allegiance (APP, P56).

## Cross-field observations (x9)
- **Lumping hides structure**: a T-state binding curve that looks weakly anti-cooperative is two non-cooperative populations (NAT, P62), as "Chinese" hides a non-officialised variety in heritage-language research (HUM, P60) (PL-41, Hypothetical).
- **Switching allegiance gets harder with conformity strength**: in P58's Moran model the basin a new convention needs grows toward 1 as Nw grows; in the three-language toy the smaller language loses even with higher status (PL-42, Plausible).
- **Re-computing printed summaries finds real issues**: P61's "social > personal" fusion contrast is not supported by Tukey HSD, and P54's allegiance-balanced null cannot exclude 3 HDRS points (PL-40, Plausible).
- **Betrayal and repair**: Royce (P63) on treason, atonement and "unscarred love"; in the agent's repeated-game analogy retaliation alone never ends the feud, forgiveness or contrition restores cooperation at a lasting small cost (PL-43, Hypothetical); Royce's loyalty-to-loyalty criterion as a test for fusion's "bright side" (PL-39, Hypothetical).
- Competing definitions added: loyalty to loyalty versus predatory loyalty (PHI, P63); allegiance balance in a trial's steering group as a design safeguard (APP, P54); identity fusion as a source of intergroup prosociality (SOC, P61).

## Cross-field observations (x12)
- **Fitted constants at last**: P64 supplies fitted haemoglobin MWC constants; with them the two-state model gives P50 44 torr and maximal slope 2.60, near measured values, while P62 (x9) finds the T state heterogeneous (PL-45, Plausible).
- **Allegiance reaches meta-analysis**: the first identity-fusion meta-analysis (P66), by originators of the theory, finds fusion's advantage over identification only in published studies, and the unpublished null is well powered (PL-44, Plausible). Its printed counts and one CI contain slips.
- **Stable in-between loyalties**: P65 argues structured ambivalence between a heritage dialect and a standard is stable, though its own counts show resistance more frequent; in the toy, coexistence needs weak conformity (a < 1) (PL-47, Hypothetical).
- **Chosen loyalty**: Ruth's "whither thou goest, I will go" against Orpah's return frames allegiance as a chosen, costly bond (P67), the reverse direction of heritage loyalty (PL-46, Philosophical/Spiritual).
- Competing definitions added: covenant loyalty / kindness (hesed) as chosen fidelity (PHI, P67); structured ambivalence as a stable attitude configuration (HUM, P65); fusion as a meta-analytic predictor of extreme pro-group orientation (SOC, P66).
