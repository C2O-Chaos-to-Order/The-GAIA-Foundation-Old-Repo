## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALD-NAT-01** [E] Cooperative binding is described by the Hill equation Y = L^n/(Kd + L^n), with Kd = P50^n, and the Hill coefficient n is the slope of the Hill plot log(Y/(1-Y)) vs log L; for haemoglobin a fit gives n = 2.8, but the real Hill plot is curvilinear: its ends approach slope 1 and its slope is highest in the middle, so cooperativity is maximal near half-saturation. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding (each bond makes the next more likely). *Sources:* P46. *Limits:* Textbook page; the Hill equation is an approximation (P46 notes it is not a mechanism). P46 contains a slip ('log 1 = log 100 = 0') and typos; reading binding as 'allegiance' is this track's operationalization.
- **ALD-NAT-03** [E] In the MWC model haemoglobin switches between a low-affinity T and a high-affinity R quaternary state (approximately K_T 0.001 and K_R 1 per Torr, L = [T0]/[R0] about 5 x 10^5); binding within each state is non-cooperative, and cooperativity comes from the shift from T to R as oxygen binds. After a 30-year controversy, single-crystal and gel experiments confirmed non-cooperative binding to T; MWC's failure is that protons and 2,3-DPG change K_T as well as L, and the tertiary two-state (TTS) model adds tertiary conformations within each quaternary state. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding through a shared conformational switch. *Sources:* P53. *Limits:* Historical review with simplifications the author acknowledges; slips: 'delta T = delta T = 1' (should pair delta T and delta R), 'confromation', 'when oxygen bounds'.
- **ALD-NAT-02** [P] A four-site Adair model with stepwise constants rising 100-fold (0.02, 0.05, 0.2, 2.0) reproduced P46's qualitative Hill plot: end slopes 1.0001, maximal slope 3.63 at log L = 0.883, 0.03 log units from half-saturation (0.853); independent sites gave slope 1.000 throughout, and the Hill equation gave Y = 0.5 at P50 with Kd = P50^n. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = cooperative binding. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-0/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Constants are the agent's choice, not haemoglobin data; Adair's polynomial is standard theory not stated in P46.
- **ALD-NAT-04** [P] With P53's approximate MWC values the Hill plot has end slopes 1.000 and P50 26 Torr, and binding within T alone or R alone has slope 1 (within 0.0004), but the maximal Hill slope is 3.64, not within 0.5 of P46's 2.8 (not supported), and changing only L from 5 x 10^4 to 5 x 10^6 changed it by only 0.08 (3.56-3.64; not supported): near L c^2 = 1 the maximal slope is insensitive to L. *Operationalization:* NAT Allegiance = MWC cooperativity. *Sources:* SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-AL-NAT-A/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* P53's values are order-of-magnitude ('~'); P46's 2.8 is a fit to real Hb. Both outcomes were known from the smoke test.
