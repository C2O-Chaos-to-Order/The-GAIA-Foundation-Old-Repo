## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **ALD-PHI-01** [PS] P51 treats loyalty as a practical disposition to persist in an intrinsically valued (though not necessarily valuable) associational attachment, an executive virtue whose value depends on its object; following Hirschman, loyalty keeps members in a declining institution while they use voice to bring it back on track rather than exiting, and medieval allegiance was an oath of fealty. *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = loyalty as a disposition to persist. *Sources:* P51. *Limits:* Interpretive; P51 discusses competing accounts (e.g. Fletcher, Royce) and whistle-blowing limits.
- **ALD-PHI-08** [PS] P59 treats loyalty as a practical disposition to persist in an intrinsically valued association, an executive virtue whose worth depends on its object. On Hirschman's account it should be given until it becomes clear that the institution cannot be recuperated, and loyalty to a particular object is forfeited when the object shows itself no longer worthy or capable of being a source of associational satisfaction or identity, though the breakpoint differs between people (P59's example of a spouse's infidelity). A loyal opposition stays within bounds compatible with the object's flourishing; whistle blowing should be a last resort, for serious wrongdoing, well grounded and likely to be effective. On religious roots P59 notes only that Old Testament writers framed fickleness as (un)faithfulness and that Confucian thought has long engaged loyalty. *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = when loyalty is forfeited. *Sources:* P59. *Limits:* Philosophical survey by one author; not a religious or spiritual-tradition source, so the gap on fidelity in a spiritual tradition remains. Slips: 'the one's who are loyal', 'self-maximation', a stray ';)' after 'Spencer, 2017'.
- **ALD-PHI-09** [PS] Royce, as surveyed in P63, makes loyalty to loyalty the test of a good cause, so that 'predatory' loyalty that destroys others' loyalty is evil; communities of memory and hope ground a religion of loyalty in which the betrayed community lives in 'the hell of the irrevocable' after treason, and atonement comes from a steadfastly loyal servant whose creative deed makes the world better than had the treason not occurred, though forgiveness does not restore the innocent state of 'unscarred love'. *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = betrayal and atonement in a community of loyalty. *Sources:* P63. *Limits:* Secondary survey of a Christian philosopher's account, not a primary religious text, so the spiritual-tradition gap is only partly filled; P63 records Curry's racism critique. Slips: 'scrutinze', 'versons', 'were the truth of the conditional'.
- **ALD-PHI-12** [PS] In the Book of Ruth, two widowed daughters-in-law face the same choice: Orpah kisses Naomi and returns, while Ruth 'clave unto her' and binds herself to Naomi's people and God ('whither thou goest, I will go'). The story praises kindness 'to the living and to the dead' and ends with Ruth called 'better to thee than seven sons'; the nearer kinsman declines to redeem 'lest I mar mine own inheritance', so loyalty is shown as costly and chosen, not owed by blood. *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = chosen covenant loyalty. *Sources:* P67. *Limits:* KJV translation only (the Hebrew 'hesed' is rendered 'kindness'); no commentary read; interpretation is the agent's. Partly fills the primary religious-text gap noted in ALC.
- **ALD-PHI-02** [H] In an exit-voice toy of a recoverable institution (decline 0.02, voice 0.1, threshold 0.5), members with no loyalty exited within 0.1 time units, nobody voiced and quality collapsed (0.002); with loyalty tolerances up to 0.2 or 0.6, members stayed, voiced, and quality recovered to the threshold (0.50). *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = loyalty buying time for voice. *Sources:* SIM-AL-PHI-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-PHI-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Agent's formalization of Hirschman as reported in P51; parameters arbitrary; recovery requires v(1 - theta) > d by construction.
- **ALD-PHI-03** [H] When decline outpaces what voice can repair (d = 0.06 > 0.05), quality collapses whatever the loyalty: loyalty only delays exit (all members gone after 0.06 time units without loyalty, after 8.0 with tolerances up to 0.2), and with high loyalty (up to 0.6) 16% of members remain in the collapsed institution. *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = loyalty to a failing institution. *Sources:* SIM-AL-PHI-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-PHI-A/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Agent's toy of Hirschman as reported in P51; P51 discusses when loyalty should be forfeited, which the toy does not model.
- **ALD-PHI-04** [H] With five times more noise, moderate loyalty (up to 0.2) no longer saved the institution (quality 0.04, everyone gone by 38 time units) and high loyalty (up to 0.6) only partly did (quality 0.42, 53% remaining), so the AL0 result is not supported under noise: random shocks push quality below members' tolerance before voice can act. *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = loyalty under shocks. *Sources:* SIM-AL-PHI-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-PHI-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Agent's toy; noise level arbitrary.
- **ALD-PHI-05** [H] With a higher standard (theta 0.7) there is a minimum loyalty: with tolerances up to 0.1 members left too early and the institution collapsed (quality 0.002, last exit at 66.9), while tolerances up to 0.3 kept 86% voicing and quality recovered to 0.69. *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = how much loyalty is enough. *Sources:* SIM-AL-PHI-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-AL-PHI-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Agent's toy; the outcome was anticipated by reasoning before the run (disclosed).
- **ALD-PHI-06** [H] In the exit-voice toy the minimum loyalty at a high standard (theta 0.7) lies between tolerances 0.15 and 0.25 (quality 0.24, 0.51, 0.69); a small group (N 30) behaves as AL0; under intermediate noise (0.03) high loyalty still saves the institution (0.50) while moderate loyalty does not (0.03). *Operationalization:* PHI Allegiance = how much loyalty is enough. *Sources:* SIM-AL-PHI-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-AL-PHI-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-AL-PHI-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-AL-PHI-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-AL-PHI-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-AL-PHI-B/iter-5#H1. *Limits:* Agent's toy of Hirschman as reported in P51.

- **ALD-PHI-10** [H] In the agent's iterated-dilemma analogy to treason and atonement, one betrayal locks tit-for-tat into an endless echo (never restored without errors), while generous TFT restores cooperation in about 1/q rounds (9.8, 3.2, 1.7 for q 0.1, 0.33, 0.6) and contrite TFT, where the betrayer accepts one round of punishment, restores it in exactly 2 rounds.
