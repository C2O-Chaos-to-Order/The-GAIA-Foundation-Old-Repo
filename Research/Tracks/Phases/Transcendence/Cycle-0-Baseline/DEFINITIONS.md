No repository document defines "Transcendence", so each field's reading below is this track's choice, taken in the sense of going beyond a level, limit or frame (passing to a new level, beyond the algebraic, beyond the self, beyond concepts). Competing definitions are recorded, not resolved.

## Cross-field observations (Baseline)
- **A new level versus a better point on the old one**: in P97's model, tradeoff breaking raises both life-cycle and counterfactual fitness, unlike moving along the tradeoff, which raises one and lowers the other (TR0-NAT-02; PL-76, Hypothetical).
- **Long-run rates decide**: life-cycle fitness is a dominant-eigenvalue growth rate and Liouville-ness is super-linear lookahead; both separate short-run from long-run behaviour (PL-73, Established).
- **Agreement is not a core**: shared heritage alone produces cross-tradition agreement in the mysticism toy (TR0-PHI-02; PL-75, Hypothetical).
- Source caution: all three audited self-transcendence studies print numbers that do not follow from their own inputs (PL-74, Plausible).
