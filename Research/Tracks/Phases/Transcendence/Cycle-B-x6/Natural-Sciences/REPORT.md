## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **TRB-NAT-01** [P] P97 argues that 'fitness decoupling' between particles and collectives in an evolutionary transition in individuality (ETI) is a metaphor with limits: computed commensurably over equivalent time intervals, particle and collective fitness are the same once a stable collective size distribution is reached. Instead it proposes that early stages of ETIs involve tradeoffs among particle traits and later, critical stages their rupture ('tradeoff breaking'), which could be used to detect an ETI. In its projection model the optimum for counterfactual fitness f2 is always zero investment in collective survival, while the optimum for whole-life-cycle fitness is positive. *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = an evolutionary transition in individuality. *Sources:* P97. *Limits:* The eLife reviewers state that the incommensurability result is not novel (Michod 2005; Shelton & Michod 2014; Bourrat 2015) and that tradeoff breaking is not sufficient as an ETI marker; P97's revision softens the claim. Notation slips: the investment trait is called theta in Section 3 and p in Section 4; the Fig. 7 caption's t1/t2 ordering conflicts with the main text.
- **TRB-NAT-02** [P] In the agent's implementation of P97's projection model (N 30 size classes, eta 8, p1 0.1, mu 0), the counterfactual f2 is maximised at theta 0 while the life-cycle growth rate F is maximised at theta* = 0.348; moving from 0 to theta* raises F from 0.512 to 0.809 and lowers f2 from 0.400 to 0.007, the pattern read as 'fitness decoupling'. Breaking the s + b = 1 tradeoff by 0.05 raises both (F 0.843, f2 0.074). Particle fitness f1 stays near 0.095 and differs from F. The particle growth rate from iterating the model equals ln(lambda) at theta* within 1e-8. *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = life-cycle fitness versus counterfactual fitness. *Sources:* SIM-TR-NAT-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-0/iter-1#H2;SIM-TR-NAT-0/iter-1#H3;SIM-TR-NAT-0/iter-1#H4;SIM-TR-NAT-0/iter-1#H5. *Limits:* beta_i is the agent's reading of a formula garbled in the extracted text; reproduces P97's qualitative Fig. 5 result, not its exact numbers.
- **TRB-NAT-03** [P] At P97's Fig. 7 size (N 15, eta 8, p1 0.1) the TR0 pattern holds: f2 is maximised at zero investment, F at theta* = 0.326; moving from 0 to theta* raises F (0.512 to 0.798) and lowers f2 (0.400 to 0.037); breaking the tradeoff by 0.05 raises both (F 0.837, f2 0.102). *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = life-cycle vs counterfactual fitness. *Sources:* SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-1#H3;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-1#H4. *Limits:* Agent's implementation; P97's Fig. 7 adds a cell-type-switching model (rho -0.9) not implemented here.
- **TRB-NAT-04** [P] The decoupling pattern is robust to the two variations tried, but theta* moves a lot: with a smaller growth benefit (eta 4) theta* = 0.519 and F rises from -0.108 (a declining population at theta 0) to 0.532 while f2 falls from -0.229 to -0.833; with high single-cell survival (p1 0.5) theta* is only 0.056 and F rises by 0.010 (1.811 to 1.822). In every case f2's optimum is zero investment. *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = sensitivity of the transition optimum. *Sources:* SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-2#H2;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-2#H3;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-3#H2;SIM-TR-NAT-A/iter-3#H3. *Limits:* Two variations only; beta_i reading as in TR0.
- **TRB-NAT-05** [P] P104 models how a nascent multicellular lineage can be stabilised against reversion to unicellularity when the environment fluctuates: type 1 ratcheting (mutations beneficial in the multicellular context but costly in the unicellular one) lengthens the time revertants need to take over, and type 2 ratcheting (lower switch rate) is favoured by prolonged growth in the multicellular-favouring environment (switch rate evolving from 0.1 to about 0.001); each type can create the conditions favouring the other. *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = stabilising a transition to a higher level against reversion. *Sources:* P104. *Limits:* General cell-level model without group structure, as P104 states; equations 2.1-2.3 are missing from the extracted text; the beneficial-mutation formula prints as 1 - (1 - e^(lambda c))^(nm), presumably e^(-lambda c).
- **TRB-NAT-06** [P] The agent's reconstruction of P104's type 2 model (G_{t+1} = (2 - p) G_t, I_{t+1} = (2 - c) I_t + p G_t) reproduces P104's printed c = 2 special case exactly and gives total growth falling with the switch rate for every cost tried (c 0.1 to 2), so with any cost to unicellular cells the growth-maximising switch rate is zero. *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = selection against reversion. *Sources:* SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Reconstruction of a missing equation; checked against one printed special case only.
- **TRB-NAT-07** [P] In the agent's reconstruction of P104's fluctuating-environment model, symmetric costs (c_g = c_i = 0.1, 10 generations each) give an optimal switch rate of 0.14 (P104: about 0.2; within the pre-registered 0.1-0.3), longer periods (50 generations) lower it to 0.028, and any asymmetry c_g > c_i (0.07, 0.05, 0.01) makes never switching optimal, with the growth penalty at p 0.2 rising as the asymmetry grows: type 1 ratcheting can promote type 2, as P104 states. *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = switch-rate optimum under fluctuation. *Sources:* SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-4#H1. *Limits:* Reconstruction; the exact optimum differs from P104's printed 0.2.
- **TRB-NAT-08** [P] P97's decoupling pattern also holds with small collectives (N 5: theta* 0.230) and a large growth benefit (eta 16: theta* 0.105); in both the counterfactual f2 optimum is zero investment and the life-cycle optimum positive. *Operationalization:* NAT Transcendence = robustness of the decoupling pattern. *Sources:* SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-5#H2;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-5#H3;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-6#H2;SIM-TR-NAT-B/iter-6#H3. *Limits:* Seven parameter sets; beta_i reading as in TR0.
