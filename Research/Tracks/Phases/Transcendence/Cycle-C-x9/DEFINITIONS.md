# Transcendence — operationalizations and competing definitions

Status: Proposed working definitions for the GAIA Phase axis. Transcendence was added by the owner on 2026-10-25 as the sixth phase (Phases `PLAN.md`, amendment v2; the earlier five-phase plan is kept as `PLAN_v1_five_phases.md`). No repository document defines "Transcendence", so each field's reading below is this track's choice, taken in the sense of going beyond a level, limit or frame (passing to a new level, beyond the algebraic, beyond the self, beyond concepts). Competing definitions are recorded, not resolved.

Owner context (recorded 2026-10-25; does not change methods): the owner maps phases to upgrade levels (Base = Divergence, Meta = Insurgence, Super = Allegiance, Hyper = Convergence, Ultra = Ascendence, Magic = Transcendence). "Transcendence corresponds to Magic" is the owner's **Hypothetical** claim; it is not tested here and no claim depends on it. ## Cross-field observations (Baseline)
- **A new level versus a better point on the old one**: in P97's model, tradeoff breaking raises both life-cycle and counterfactual fitness, unlike moving along the tradeoff, which raises one and lowers the other (TR0-NAT-02; PL-76, Hypothetical).
- **Long-run rates decide**: life-cycle fitness is a dominant-eigenvalue growth rate and Liouville-ness is super-linear lookahead; both separate short-run from long-run behaviour (PL-73, Established).
- **Agreement is not a core**: shared heritage alone produces cross-tradition agreement in the mysticism toy (TR0-PHI-02; PL-75, Hypothetical).
- Source caution: all three audited self-transcendence studies print numbers that do not follow from their own inputs (PL-74, Plausible).

## Cross-field observations (x3)
- **Printed numbers need recomputing**: P103's life-satisfaction p is .047, not .470, and P101's text prints the reciprocal of its own Bayes factor (PL-77, Established).
- **Aggregates hide structure**: Universalism and Benevolence rank SVO groups differently, and cross-tradition agreement does not reveal its source in the mysticism toy (PL-78, Hypothetical).
- **The transition optimum moves**: theta* ranges from 0.056 to 0.519 across the tried parameters while the decoupling pattern holds (TRA-NAT-04).

## Cross-field observations (x6)
- **Holding a new level in place**: in P104's model unequal costs make never reverting optimal (TRB-NAT-07); in the instrument toy a scale built on one tradition favours it only if experience is constructed (TRB-PHI-05). Analogy only (PL-79, Hypothetical).
- **Small studies, weak evidence**: at n 10-50 a t near 2 gives no evidence either way, and Pahnke's 4/10 vs 0/10 (as P105 reports it) is not significant two-sided (PL-80, Established).
- **Conventions decide verdicts**: P100's content validity is 0.92 by averaging and 0.27 by universal agreement (TRB-APP-07).

## Cross-field observations (x9)
- **Measures, not experiences**: P107's factor pattern shared across countries and the instrument toy's template advantage are both properties of measures (PL-81, Hypothetical; TRC-PHI-06).
- **Graded versus dichotomised**: P106 finds mystical-type ratings graded with dose, while the 'complete mystical experience' threshold dichotomises them and small groups cannot test the dichotomy (PL-82, Plausible).
- **A withdrawn attribution**: TRB's 'too few digits' explanation for base-3 Liouville lookahead did not survive a longer run (TRC-FOR-06).
