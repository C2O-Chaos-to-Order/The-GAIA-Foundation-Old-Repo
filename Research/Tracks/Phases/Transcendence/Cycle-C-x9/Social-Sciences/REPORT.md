- **TRC-SOC-08** [E] Power at P99-type designs is sensitive to size: a Stimulation-sized effect has power 0.60 even with doubled groups, a Conformity-sized effect 0.63 with halved groups, and a Hedonism-sized effect 0.84 at alpha .01. *Operationalization:* SOC Transcendence = design sensitivity. *Sources:* SIM-TR-SOC-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-TR-SOC-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-TR-SOC-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-TR-SOC-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-TR-SOC-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-TR-SOC-C/iter-9#H1. *Limits:* Printed effect sizes taken as true values.
- **TRC-SOC-01** [P] P99 finds that prosocial participants (social value orientation, 1151 of 1655) endorse self-transcendence values, Universalism and Benevolence, more strongly than individualists and competitors, and Power values less strongly (largest effects Universalism eta^2 .037 and Power .040). P99 cautions that combining Universalism and Benevolence into a higher-order Self-transcendence score can obscure differences between them. *Operationalization:* SOC Transcendence = self-transcendence values and prosocial orientation. *Sources:* P99. *Limits:* Student sample; small effects. Slips: the Results text says pro-socials endorsed Conformity 'less strongly' although Table 3's means (-.513 vs -.692 and -.675) and the planned comparison show the opposite; Table 2 prints Power's mean as .469 (Universalism's value); see TR0-SOC-02.
- **TRC-SOC-06** [P] P107 derives five dimensions of spirituality (religious and spiritual reflection and commitment, contemplative practice, unifying interconnectedness, love, altruism) by EFA and ESEM in 5512 online respondents from China, India and the US, reports a multi-group ESEM for configural invariance with marginally acceptable fit (RMSEA 0.043, CFI 0.897-0.899), and finds that high love and altruism go with lower odds of depression, suicidal ideation and anxiety in all three countries, while reflection and commitment and contemplative practice go with higher odds in China. *Operationalization:* SOC Transcendence = cross-cultural dimensions of spirituality. *Sources:* P107. *Limits:* Cross-sectional; only configural invariance tested; CFI below the 0.90 it cites as acceptable; online crowdsourced samples.
