## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVC-APP-01** [E] Multiregion genome and epigenome profiling of nine colorectal tumours showed clonally shared early alterations followed, in the late phase, by branching of a parental clone into numerous subclones; known driver alterations were frequent among early and rare among late alterations. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branching of subclones (intratumour heterogeneity). *Sources:* P05. *Limits:* Nine tumours, one centre; region sampling limits subclone detection.
- **DVC-APP-09** [E] A numerical solver can diverge from a true solution: for forward Euler a perturbation is multiplied by (1 + a h) each step, so it grows when |1 + a h| > 1; on the simple harmonic oscillator the growth factor |1 +/- i h w0| exceeds 1 for every h > 0, so the computed solution always grows. Stability depends on both the solver and the ODE. *Operationalization:* Divergence = solver instability. *Sources:* P17. *Limits:* Linear test problems.
- **DVC-APP-02** [P] Whether late subclonal divergence is mostly neutral is contested: P05's simulation suggests extensive intratumour heterogeneity could be generated by neutral evolution, and P04 presents punctuated ('Big Bang') evolution with predominantly neutral expansion as an alternative to gradualist selection-driven models. *Operationalization:* Competing models of tumour divergence: neutral vs selective, gradual vs punctuated. *Sources:* P05;P04. *Limits:* P04 is a narrative review; neutrality tests from frequency spectra are themselves disputed (not yet sourced here). DVB re-read: P04 contains an unedited AI-style sentence ('While a specific reference from the search results isn't provided here ...') and states AI was used for language editing, so P04's own statements are treated with extra caution.
- **DVC-APP-03** [P] A 2026 review reports evidence that the linear-progression model of tumour evolution is less frequent than expected and that parallel or branched evolution is common, with the clinical consequence that a single primary-tumour biopsy may miss divergent subclones. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branched rather than linear progression. *Sources:* P04. *Limits:* Secondhand report of the cited studies, which were not read here. DVB re-read: P04 contains an unedited AI-style sentence and states AI was used for language editing (quality flag), so this rests on a review of uncertain editorial quality.
- **DVC-APP-04** [P] In one neutral birth-death growth run to 5,000 cells, the cumulative count of subclonal mutations was approximately but not tightly linear in 1/f (R^2 = 0.958); the pre-registered criterion R^2 >= 0.98 was not supported. *Operationalization:* Divergence = neutral clonal branching signature. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Single run, cell fractions without sequencing noise; the criterion may be too strict for one stochastic realisation. To be tested with replicates in x3.
- **DVC-APP-05** [P] Across 10 replicates of DV0's neutral clonal-branching toy (5 at N = 5,000 and 5 at N = 20,000 cells), R^2 of the M(f) ~ 1/f fit ranged from 0.916 to 0.985 (medians 0.954 and 0.976); the pre-registered median >= 0.98 was not supported at either size, so DV0's single value of 0.958 was typical rather than an outlier. *Operationalization:* Divergence = neutral branching clonal evolution (1/f signature). *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Cell fractions without sequencing noise; the 0.98 threshold is the agent's, not a published criterion; 4 extinct starts were restarted and are reported.
- **DVC-APP-06** [P] Explicit Euler on y' = -lam y converged for h lam = 0.5 to 1.9 and diverged for h lam = 2.1 and 2.5 (|y| about 2.6e12 and 6.7e52 after 300 steps), exactly as the amplification factor |1 - h lam| predicts: numerical divergence of a solver is a property of the method and step size, not of the system solved. *Operationalization:* Divergence = numerical instability of an iterative method. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-3#V2. *Limits:* Linear test equation only; no source on numerical analysis was read in this track.
- **DVC-APP-07** [P] A 2026 review distinguishes divergent, convergent and parallel evolution in tumours and stresses that distinguishing among evolutionary modes requires careful consideration of evidentiary standards. *Operationalization:* Divergence vs convergence vs parallel evolution in tumours. *Sources:* P04. *Limits:* Narrative review with an unedited AI-style sentence elsewhere in the text (quality flag).
- **DVC-APP-08** [P] In multiregion colorectal-cancer data, known driver alterations were frequent among early (founder) alterations but rare among late (progressor) ones, and the authors' simulation likewise put progressor mutations rarely in driver genes. *Operationalization:* Divergence = late branching mostly by neutral mutations. *Sources:* P05. *Limits:* Small cohort; sampling of about 1e6 of 1e9-1e10 cells; a published Correction (PMC5438101) was not read.
- **DVC-APP-10** [P] In a branching-growth toy, driver mutations were over-represented among high-frequency (CCF >= 0.5) mutations relative to low-frequency ones (0.21 vs 0.12 with driver effect 0.5; 0.19 vs 0.10 with 1.0), so the pre-registered 2-fold enrichment was not supported. In the neutral control none of 41 high-CCF mutations was a driver (0.046 among low-CCF); the 'no difference' test was not supported only because a zero share gives a zero binomial SE, a flaw in the test, not evidence of depletion. *Operationalization:* Divergence = selection shaping which branches become clonal. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Non-spatial toy; whole-population CCF; binomial SE ignores clone structure and is degenerate at zero.
- **DVC-APP-11** [P] P17's statements reproduced exactly: forward Euler on the oscillator grew by 37% (h = 0.01) and 3.2% (h = 0.001) over 10 periods, matching (1 + h^2 w^2)^(n/2), while semi-implicit Euler stayed within 0.25%; the global error slope was 1.003 (O(h)); and h|lambda| = 1.99 decayed while 2.01 grew without bound. *Operationalization:* Divergence = method-induced numerical divergence. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-6#V2. *Limits:* Linear problems; semi-implicit Euler is the agent's comparison, not in P17.
- **DVC-APP-12** [P] With a pooled-SE test (fixing the DVB design flaw) the no-advantage control showed no enrichment (driver share 0.038 high-CCF vs 0.049 low-CCF: supported), strong drivers (sd 1.0) were enriched among clonal mutations (0.20 vs 0.09: supported), and moderate drivers (sd 0.5, 0.22 vs 0.12) were not supported at 4 pooled SE. *Operationalization:* Selection shaping clonal divergence. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Toy branching model; few high-CCF mutations (79-125).
- **DVC-APP-13** [P] P17's forward-Euler results held: on the oscillator amplitude grew 22.7-fold over 10 periods at h = 0.1 and 1.37-fold at h = 0.01, exactly as (1 + h^2)^(n/2); at h = 1e-4 growth was only 0.31% (the pre-stated 'not supported'); error slopes were 1.018 and 1.001 (O(h)); and |1 - h lam| > 1 diverged exactly as predicted. *Operationalization:* Numerical divergence (replications). *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-6#V2;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-8#H2;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-9#H1. *Limits:* Linear test problems only.
- **DVC-APP-14** [P] The DVA finding replicated: the neutral clonal tail's 1/f fit had median R^2 0.90 (range 0.88-0.96), not supported at the 0.98 criterion, so that criterion looks too strict for tumours of 5,000 cells. *Operationalization:* Neutral subclonal tail (replication). *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-7#H1. *Limits:* Five replicates; the 0.98 criterion is from DVA's SPEC, not from P04.
