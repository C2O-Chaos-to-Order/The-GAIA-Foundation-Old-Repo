Phase report: Divergence (`../../README.md`). Definitions and competing definitions: `../DEFINITIONS.md`. Labels: **E** Established · **P** Plausible · **PS** Philosophical/Spiritual · **H** Hypothetical. GAIA's Phase axis is a framework category (PS), kept separate from the findings.

## Reading of this cycle
x12 adds no FOR source; finite sums hide convergence more as p nears 1 (1.4% of the limit at p = 1.001), and Lorenz trajectory divergence vanishes below the chaotic range while operator divergence stays the same.

## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVD-FOR-01** [E] In dynamical-systems usage, the global Lyapunov exponent is taken to represent the average rate of divergence of neighbouring trajectories, and the most popular candidate definition of chaos (Chaos_lambda) is a positive global Lyapunov exponent. *Operationalization:* Divergence = exponential separation of nearby trajectories. *Sources:* P09. *Limits:* P09 also reports objections: Lyapunov exponents are asymptotic quantities and Chaos_lambda is one of several definitions.
- **DVD-FOR-02** [E] There is no consensus on a precise definition of chaotic behaviour: mathematicians often prefer one definition and scientists another, so 'divergence of trajectories' is one operationalization among competitors, not the definition of chaos. *Operationalization:* Competing definitions of chaos (sensitive dependence vs topological definitions). *Sources:* P09. *Limits:* Reference-work summary of the debate.
- **DVD-FOR-08** [E] Divergence of trajectories is not sufficient for chaos: P09 notes that the map x_{n+1} = 1.1 x_n has only unstable, aperiodic orbits that never return near the start, so a definition based on instability and aperiodicity would count it as chaotic, yet it does not exhibit chaotic behaviour. *Operationalization:* Divergence (sensitive dependence) vs chaos. *Sources:* P09. *Limits:* Definitional point; P09 reports no consensus definition of chaos.
- **DVD-FOR-09** [E] In chaotic dynamics, stretching and folding mechanisms cause some trajectories to converge rapidly while others diverge rapidly; P09 also states that the Lyapunov definition of chaos is trivially false for finite uncertainties in real systems and calls the idea that chaotic systems are unpredictable a myth. *Operationalization:* Divergence co-occurring with local convergence. *Sources:* P09. *Limits:* Reference-work summary of a debated literature.
- **DVD-FOR-10** [E] Kullback-Leibler divergence is non-negative, not symmetric, zero only when the distributions match and possibly infinite; it equals the negative logarithm of the average multinomial likelihood of infinite data from p under the model q, so as observed frequencies diverge from the model the average likelihood falls toward zero. *Operationalization:* Divergence = statistical divergence D(p||q). *Sources:* P16. *Limits:* An expository arXiv note (not peer reviewed); the derivation uses Stirling's approximation.
- **DVD-FOR-14** [E] A series diverges when its sequence of partial sums does not converge; the Divergence Test is one-directional (terms not tending to 0 imply divergence, but 'a series whose terms go to zero may either converge or diverge', e.g. the harmonic series); p-series converge only for p > 1; and two divergent series can add term by term to a convergent one. *Operationalization:* FOR Divergence = non-convergence of partial sums. *Sources:* P23. *Limits:* Real series only.
- **DVD-FOR-03** [P] For the logistic map the computed Lyapunov exponent at r = 4 was 0.69313 (ln 2 = 0.69315) and negative at r = 3.2; two orbits started 1e-10 apart at r = 4 separated beyond 0.1 after a median 31 steps, close to the predicted ln(1e9)/ln 2 = 29.9. *Operationalization:* Divergence = trajectory separation rate. *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-0/iter-1#V2;SIM-DV-FOR-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Code and analytic check of a textbook map; it shows what 'trajectory divergence' means formally and nothing about any physical or social system.
- **DVD-FOR-04** [P] For 1,000 random pairs of distributions on 10 points, Kullback-Leibler divergence was never negative, was zero for identical distributions, matched its Monte Carlo estimate within 4 standard errors in 50 of 50 pairs, and was asymmetric (median relative difference between D(p||q) and D(q||p) 0.20). *Operationalization:* Divergence = asymmetric statistical dissimilarity (not a distance). *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-1#V2;SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-1#V3;SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Code and analytic check only; no source on KL divergence was read in this track, so no Established claim is made about its literature.
- **DVD-FOR-05** [P] Harmonic partial sums tracked ln n + gamma within 1/(2n) up to n = 100,000 and first exceeded 10 only at n = 12,367, while the p = 2 and alternating harmonic series converged within their bounds: a divergent series can grow extremely slowly. *Operationalization:* Divergence = partial sums without a finite limit. *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-2#V2;SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-2#V3;SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Deterministic floating-point check of standard results; H1 was predictable from general knowledge.
- **DVD-FOR-06** [P] For F = (x^3 + y, x y^2, z x^2) on the unit cube, a finite-difference divergence matched the analytic one to 1e-8, and the Monte Carlo volume integral of div F (1.8380, SE 0.0034) matched the outward flux 11/6 = 1.8333, as the divergence theorem requires. *Operationalization:* Divergence = net outflow per unit volume (div F). *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-A/iter-3#V2. *Limits:* One polynomial field; a code check of the operator sense only.
- **DVD-FOR-11** [P] P16's relation held numerically: for counts from p, -(1/n) log L(c|q) approached D(p||q) with errors 0.12, 0.018, 0.0021, 0.00015 nats at n = 100 to 100,000 (K = 6) and 0.69 to 0.0026 (K = 50); the likelihood-ratio rate matched D within 4 SE at every n. With 50 categories the approximation needs about ten times more data for the same accuracy. *Operationalization:* Divergence = KL as likelihood penalty. *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-2#H2. *Limits:* One random (p, q) pair per K.
- **DVD-FOR-12** [P] The map x -> 1.1 x has exponent ln 1.1 = 0.095 but grows without bound and never brings nearby points closer; the logistic map at r = 4 (exponent 0.693) stays in [0, 1] and nearby separations shrank in 16.1% of steps, matching the analytic share of time spent where |f'| < 1 (0.1609; observed 0.1607). All finite-time exponents over 20,000 steps stayed positive. *Operationalization:* Divergence with folding vs divergence without it. *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-3#V2;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-3#V3;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-4#H2. *Limits:* One-dimensional maps.
- **DVD-FOR-13** [P] In the Lorenz system the vector-field divergence is -13.67 everywhere (phase volumes contract), yet the largest Lyapunov exponent was +0.90 at rho = 28 (nearby trajectories diverge) and -0.40 at rho = 14 (they converge): the operator sense and the trajectory sense of 'divergence' are different quantities. *Operationalization:* Divergence (operator) vs divergence (trajectories). *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-B/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* No Lorenz source read; RK4, dt 0.01, T 500.
- **DVD-FOR-15** [P] P23's printed partial sums were reproduced exactly (harmonic S_1000 = 7.485470861; S_10 of 1/k^2 = 1.549767731), and H_n - ln n = 0.577216 at n = 10^6 (Euler's gamma: divergence is only logarithmic). Finite sums cannot reveal the limit: after a million terms the convergent p = 1.01 series had reached only 13.4% of its sum (zeta(1.01) = 100.58), while the divergent p = 0.99 series stood at just 15.4. The ratio test example converged (ratio 0.6667) and P23's two divergent series summed to 2. *Operationalization:* Series divergence and its invisibility in finite data. *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-1#V2;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-5#H1. *Limits:* Floating-point sums; 'looks divergent' thresholds were the agent's.
- **DVD-FOR-16** [P] Replications: P16's likelihood reading of KL held with 10 categories (error 0.0006 nats at n = 10^5); the logistic map spent 0.1609 of time in locally contracting regions (analytic 0.1609); x -> 1.01x has exponent ln 1.01 = 0.00995 without chaos; the Lorenz exponent was 0.90 with a 1e-10 initial separation. *Operationalization:* Statistical and dynamical divergence (replications). *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-6#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-6#H2;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-7#H2;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-8#V2;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-8#V3;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-C/iter-9#H1. *Limits:* As DVB.
- **DVD-FOR-17** [P] In the formal sciences 'divergence' names at least four distinct properties in this track's sources: non-convergent partial sums (P23), an asymmetric statistical distance (P16), exponential separation of nearby trajectories (P09), and growth of a numerical solution away from the true one (P17); plus the operator sense (div F), still unsourced. They share a word, not a definition. *Operationalization:* Competing formal definitions. *Sources:* P23;P16;P09;P17. *Limits:* Survey of sources read here only.
- **DVD-FOR-18** [P] Finite sums hide convergence more the closer p is to 1: after a million terms p = 1.001 had reached 14.30, 1.4% of its limit 1000.58 (supported), while p = 1.1 had reached 76% of 10.58 (below-half not supported); the divergent p = 0.999 stood at 14.49 (below 20, supported). Two divergent series with 100,000 terms summed to 2.0. *Operationalization:* FOR Divergence = non-convergent partial sums (P23). *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-4#H1. *Limits:* Floating-point sums; zeta from scipy.
- **DVD-FOR-19** [P] Replications and variants: the log-likelihood-ratio rate matched D(p||q) within 0.001 nats at n = 1e5 for 20 categories, for 10 (new seed) and for a peaked q (0.0004); local shrinking held for 0.161 of steps (DVC 0.161); x -> 1.001x has exponent ln 1.001 = 0.0010, positive without chaos. *Operationalization:* FOR Divergence = statistical divergence; divergence without chaos. *Sources:* SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-5#H2;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-6#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-6#H2;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-12#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-12#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-12#H2;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-7#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-7#H1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-8#V1;SIM-DV-FOR-D/iter-8#H1. *Limits:* As DVB/DVC.
