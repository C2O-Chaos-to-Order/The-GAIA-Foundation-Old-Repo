## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVD-HUM-01** [E] For the Andic branch of East Caucasian, geographic distances correlate with linguistic divergence for four of six tested classifications (permutation statistics 0, 0.007, 0.026 and 0.046); the two Swadesh-list (lexicostatistical) classifications give 0.088 and 0.116, and qualitative classifications fit geography better than cognacy-based ones. *Operationalization:* Divergence = genealogical distance between languages. *Sources:* P06. *Limits:* One small language group; correlation, not mechanism; six classifications, so small-sample power matters (see DVB-HUM-13).
- **DVD-HUM-03** [E] Historians disagree about the image of divergence in Darwin: some argue the Origin diagram has the structure not of a tree but of a coral, and P02 argues the sketches had a heuristic function and became more abstract diagrams over time. *Operationalization:* Divergence images: tree vs coral vs seaweed. *Sources:* P02. *Limits:* One historiographical article's survey.
- **DVD-HUM-05** [E] Zhang and Gong derive a 'conventional' meaning-list size n = N U^2 p(1-p) / (N eps^2 + U^2 p(1-p)); for total vocabularies of 4,000-5,000 words and alpha, eps between 0.05 and 0.1 it lies in [67, 357], so both Swadesh lists (100 and 200 meanings) are statistically large enough to estimate relatedness, while 15-40-item lists are not. *Operationalization:* Divergence measured on a finite meaning list. *Sources:* P12. *Limits:* Assumes random (i.i.d.) sampling of words, justified by arbitrariness of form-meaning mappings; glosses over semantic ambiguity, as the authors say.
- **DVD-HUM-07** [E] Lexicostatistics is a broader approach than glottochronology and does not assume a constant rate of word retention or loss; the constant-rate dating used in DV0's HUM simulation is the glottochronological special case. *Operationalization:* Competing methods for measuring lexical divergence. *Sources:* P12. *Limits:* Definitional; P12 does not evaluate glottochronological dating itself.
- **DVD-HUM-08** [E] Tree diagrams of divergence in the humanities are older than Darwin's: the first recorded manuscript stemma was probably Collins and Schlyter's 1827 tree of medieval Swedish legal texts, and Schleicher published trees of languages from the 1850s onwards. *Operationalization:* Divergence = genealogical branching of texts and languages. *Sources:* P13. *Limits:* Reported in an essay that cites a review; primary documents not read.
- **DVD-HUM-15** [E] In 17,094 Billboard Hot 100 songs (1960-2010), US pop evolved continuously but with three stylistic 'revolutions' (around 1964, 1983 and 1991, the largest being the 1991 rise of rap); the rate of change during revolutions varied four- to six-fold; diversity dipped around 1986 and then recovered, with no evidence for progressive homogenisation; and the 1964 changes began before the British Invasion. *Operationalization:* HUM Divergence = stylistic divergence in a cultural record. *Sources:* P21. *Limits:* Hot 100 only (commercially successful songs); audio features capture a fraction of musical complexity (P21's own limits).
- **DVD-HUM-02** [P] The authors interpret the better geographic fit of qualitative classifications as possible implicit geographic bias, so that how linguistic divergence is measured (cognacy vs expert classification) can change what it appears to show. *Operationalization:* Measurement-dependence of 'divergence'. *Sources:* P06. *Limits:* An interpretation offered by the authors; not tested against an independent benchmark.
- **DVD-HUM-04** [P] In a formal lexical-replacement model, a constant-rate shared-cognate estimate recovered a 3,000-year split without bias when rates were equal across meanings, but underestimated it by 16% when rates varied (gamma shape 1). *Operationalization:* Divergence time from shared cognates. *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-0/iter-1#V2;SIM-DV-HUM-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Formal check of a dating method; parameters illustrative; borrowing and chance resemblance ignored.
- **DVD-HUM-06** [P] P12's binomial principle implies that the number of matching instances needed to call a sound correspondence recurrent must rise with the combined frequency of the segments involved; a fixed threshold of two would wrongly count /b/-zero and /f/-zero in English-Latin as recurrent. *Operationalization:* Separating inherited correspondences (divergence from a common ancestor) from chance. *Sources:* P12. *Limits:* Some linguists consider the binomial approach too rigorous (Greenberg, cited in P12); results depend on alpha and list size.
- **DVD-HUM-09** [P] Phylogenetic programs applied to manuscript traditions generally agree with conventional scholarship and with blind analyses of artificial copying experiments; the main objection is contamination (a scribe copying from more than one exemplar), which parallels horizontal gene transfer. *Operationalization:* Divergence of texts by error-prone copying. *Sources:* P13. *Limits:* Essay by proponents; critics are cited but not summarised in detail.
- **DVD-HUM-10** [P] P12's Table 1 values (356.750 and 66.526) and worked example (0.0032) were reproduced exactly. Sampling error alone gave a 100-meaning list a 95% range of 2.05-4.14 millennia for a true 3.0 (about +/-35%), so the pre-registered +/-25% was not supported. Alpha = 0.01 reproduced P12's k = 2 range exactly but placed p = 0.0043 in the k = 3 range where P12 writes [0.0015, 0.0043), so the pre-registered exact match was not supported by one grid point. *Operationalization:* Precision of lexical divergence measures. *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-1#V2;SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-2#V2;SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Rate r = 0.07 per millennium remains illustrative; P12 does not state alpha for its Figure 5 (0.01 was our reading).
- **DVD-HUM-11** [P] In a toy manuscript tradition (8 surviving copies, 500 word positions, 2% change per copying), average-linkage reconstruction recovered every leaf triplet in 50 of 50 clean replicates, and contaminating 30% of positions in 2 copies lowered triplet accuracy to 0.90 (a drop of 0.099). *Operationalization:* Divergence of texts and the effect of contamination. *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-A/iter-3#H2. *Limits:* Balanced, clock-like tree with no lost exemplars, which favours the method; real traditions are messier.
- **DVD-HUM-12** [P] P06 observes that, contrary to first impressions, the more assumedly precise a classification is (model-based phylogeny vs impressionistic grouping), the lower its correlation with geography. *Operationalization:* Divergence measurement method affects the divergence pattern found. *Sources:* P06. *Limits:* Six classifications of one group; the authors discuss implicit geographic bias as one explanation.
- **DVD-HUM-13** [P] A tree-geography permutation test with eight languages had power 1.00, 0.94 and 0.43 at geographic noise 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0 ('power >= 0.8' not supported at noise 1.0) (null rejection 0.04-0.07); 16 languages restored power to 0.88 at noise 1.0. A non-significant result for a small family, like two of P06's six classifications, is therefore weak evidence against a geographic signal. *Operationalization:* Divergence detectability vs sample size. *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Balanced equal-branch trees and a Mantel-type statistic, not P06's trees or test.
- **DVD-HUM-14** [P] With four of eight manuscript copies contaminated, stemma triplet accuracy fell by 0.26 from near 1.0 (two contaminated copies in DVA); at doubled lexical replacement (r = 0.14) the 95% range of estimated divergence time with a 100-meaning list was +/-28%, outside the pre-registered +/-25% (not supported). *Operationalization:* Divergence reconstruction error. *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-5#V2;SIM-DV-HUM-B/iter-5#H1. *Limits:* Toy models as in DVA.
- **DVD-HUM-16** [P] P21 frames styles as populations of songs that evolved unique characters in partial geographical or cultural isolation within 'one large, highly structured, metapopulation of songs' linked by imitation, with the hip hop/rap style as 'the fourth, most divergent, cluster': divergence by analogy with speciation, which the authors say they suppose rather than show. *Operationalization:* Cultural divergence as modification by descent. *Sources:* P21. *Limits:* Analogy; P21 does not reconstruct ancestry.
- **DVD-HUM-17** [P] P21's Foote-novelty method recovered 87% of three synthetic change points of moderate size with no false peaks; detection fell for smaller jumps (0.80, just under the 0.8 criterion in floating point: not supported), under fast drift (0.65) and with a one-year kernel (0.18), and four-fold deeper sampling did not help (0.73; not supported). Under the null, 5% of series showed a false 'revolution' (alpha 0.05). *Operationalization:* Detecting divergence (revolutions) in cultural time series. *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-1#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-2#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-3#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-9#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-9#H2. *Limits:* Synthetic data only. DVD seed replication of the iteration-1 setting gave 0.70 instead of 0.87 (DVD-HUM-19): 20-replicate detection rates move by about 0.17 between seeds, so the 87% figure is not stable.
- **DVD-HUM-18** [P] Replications: one contaminated copy lowered stemma accuracy by only 0.04 (not supported at 0.05); at a slow replacement rate (0.035) a 100-word list still left dating error of +/-44% (not supported at 25%); with 16 languages and low noise a geography-tree Mantel test had power 1.0. *Operationalization:* Textual and lexical divergence (replications). *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-6#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-6#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-7#V2;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-C/iter-8#H1. *Limits:* As DVA/DVB.
- **DVD-HUM-19** [P] The Foote-novelty detection rate is not stable at 20 replicates: a seed replication of DVC's 0.87 setting gave 0.70. With 100 replicates a small jump (0.75) gave 0.77; jump 1.0 gave 0.80 (just under the criterion in floating point: not supported), kernel half-width 8 gave 0.50, drift 0.1 gave 0.77 and deeper samples with jump 0.5 gave 0.73 (all not supported). No false peaks occurred, and the null false-positive rate was 0.04 at alpha 0.05. *Operationalization:* HUM Divergence = detectable stylistic revolutions (P21 method). *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-1#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-2#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-3#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-6#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-6#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-7#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-7#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-7#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-5#V1. *Limits:* Synthetic data; detection depends on jump size, kernel and drift.
- **DVD-HUM-20** [P] Replications repeated DVC's verdicts: light contamination barely lowered stemma accuracy (drop 0.013; not supported) and a 100-meaning list's date range stayed wider than +/-25% (0.445; not supported); heavy contamination (60% of positions) cut accuracy by 0.32 (supported), and the geography-tree Mantel test kept full power with noisier distances and a smaller family. *Operationalization:* HUM Divergence = manuscript and lexical divergence. *Sources:* SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-8#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-8#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-8#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-9#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-9#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-9#H2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-10#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-10#V2;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-10#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-11#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-11#H1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-12#V1;SIM-DV-HUM-D/iter-12#H1. *Limits:* As DVA-DVC.
