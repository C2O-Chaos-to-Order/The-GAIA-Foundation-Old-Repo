# Divergence in the Applied Sciences — Cycle-D-x12 (DVD)

Phase report: Divergence (`../../README.md`). Definitions and competing definitions: `../DEFINITIONS.md`. Labels: **E** Established · **P** Plausible · **PS** Philosophical/Spiritual · **H** Hypothetical. GAIA's Phase axis is a framework category (PS), kept separate from the findings.

## Reading of this cycle
x12 sources design-process divergence (P24): divergent thinking is the main measure of creative ideation, and its scores are confounded by fluency; toys reproduce the confound and the serial-order effect, and show that 18 trials give noisy individual 'originality costs'.

## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVD-APP-01** [E] Multiregion genome and epigenome profiling of nine colorectal tumours showed clonally shared early alterations followed, in the late phase, by branching of a parental clone into numerous subclones; known driver alterations were frequent among early and rare among late alterations. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branching of subclones (intratumour heterogeneity). *Sources:* P05. *Limits:* Nine tumours, one centre; region sampling limits subclone detection.
- **DVD-APP-09** [E] A numerical solver can diverge from a true solution: for forward Euler a perturbation is multiplied by (1 + a h) each step, so it grows when |1 + a h| > 1; on the simple harmonic oscillator the growth factor |1 +/- i h w0| exceeds 1 for every h > 0, so the computed solution always grows. Stability depends on both the solver and the ODE. *Operationalization:* Divergence = solver instability. *Sources:* P17. *Limits:* Linear test problems.
- **DVD-APP-15** [E] Creative ideation has been measured mainly by divergent thinking tasks such as the Alternate Uses Task, scored by the number (fluency), uncommonness (originality) and diversity (flexibility) of responses; serial-order research reports that response rates fall and originality rises as people keep generating ideas. *Operationalization:* APP Divergence = divergent thinking in idea generation (design-process divergence). *Sources:* P24. *Limits:* Report of a theory-and-methods paper (single author) that summarises cited studies; P24 itself presents no data.
- **DVD-APP-16** [E] P24 lists three shortcomings of divergent-thinking tasks: stimulus-dependency (up to half of fluency variance is stimulus-specific), fluency-dependency (fluency is confounded in summative quality scores, with fluency-originality correlations often above 0.80) and static cumulative scores; it proposes a Multi-Trial Creative Ideation framework with one response per trial and time-based indicators. *Operationalization:* APP Divergence measurement caution. *Sources:* P24. *Limits:* The figures are P24's citations of other studies (Silvia et al. 2008; Barbot et al. 2016a; Said-Metwaly et al. 2017), not read here.
- **DVD-APP-02** [P] Whether late subclonal divergence is mostly neutral is contested: P05's simulation suggests extensive intratumour heterogeneity could be generated by neutral evolution, and P04 presents punctuated ('Big Bang') evolution with predominantly neutral expansion as an alternative to gradualist selection-driven models. *Operationalization:* Competing models of tumour divergence: neutral vs selective, gradual vs punctuated. *Sources:* P05;P04. *Limits:* P04 is a narrative review; neutrality tests from frequency spectra are themselves disputed (not yet sourced here). DVB re-read: P04 contains an unedited AI-style sentence ('While a specific reference from the search results isn't provided here ...') and states AI was used for language editing, so P04's own statements are treated with extra caution.
- **DVD-APP-03** [P] A 2026 review reports evidence that the linear-progression model of tumour evolution is less frequent than expected and that parallel or branched evolution is common, with the clinical consequence that a single primary-tumour biopsy may miss divergent subclones. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branched rather than linear progression. *Sources:* P04. *Limits:* Secondhand report of the cited studies, which were not read here. DVB re-read: P04 contains an unedited AI-style sentence and states AI was used for language editing (quality flag), so this rests on a review of uncertain editorial quality.
- **DVD-APP-04** [P] In one neutral birth-death growth run to 5,000 cells, the cumulative count of subclonal mutations was approximately but not tightly linear in 1/f (R^2 = 0.958); the pre-registered criterion R^2 >= 0.98 was not supported. *Operationalization:* Divergence = neutral clonal branching signature. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Single run, cell fractions without sequencing noise; the criterion may be too strict for one stochastic realisation. To be tested with replicates in x3.
- **DVD-APP-05** [P] Across 10 replicates of DV0's neutral clonal-branching toy (5 at N = 5,000 and 5 at N = 20,000 cells), R^2 of the M(f) ~ 1/f fit ranged from 0.916 to 0.985 (medians 0.954 and 0.976); the pre-registered median >= 0.98 was not supported at either size, so DV0's single value of 0.958 was typical rather than an outlier. *Operationalization:* Divergence = neutral branching clonal evolution (1/f signature). *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Cell fractions without sequencing noise; the 0.98 threshold is the agent's, not a published criterion; 4 extinct starts were restarted and are reported.
- **DVD-APP-06** [P] Explicit Euler on y' = -lam y converged for h lam = 0.5 to 1.9 and diverged for h lam = 2.1 and 2.5 (|y| about 2.6e12 and 6.7e52 after 300 steps), exactly as the amplification factor |1 - h lam| predicts: numerical divergence of a solver is a property of the method and step size, not of the system solved. *Operationalization:* Divergence = numerical instability of an iterative method. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-3#V2. *Limits:* Linear test equation only; no source on numerical analysis was read in this track.
- **DVD-APP-07** [P] A 2026 review distinguishes divergent, convergent and parallel evolution in tumours and stresses that distinguishing among evolutionary modes requires careful consideration of evidentiary standards. *Operationalization:* Divergence vs convergence vs parallel evolution in tumours. *Sources:* P04. *Limits:* Narrative review with an unedited AI-style sentence elsewhere in the text (quality flag).
- **DVD-APP-08** [P] In multiregion colorectal-cancer data, known driver alterations were frequent among early (founder) alterations but rare among late (progressor) ones, and the authors' simulation likewise put progressor mutations rarely in driver genes. *Operationalization:* Divergence = late branching mostly by neutral mutations. *Sources:* P05. *Limits:* Small cohort; sampling of about 1e6 of 1e9-1e10 cells; a published Correction (PMC5438101) was not read.
- **DVD-APP-10** [P] In a branching-growth toy, driver mutations were over-represented among high-frequency (CCF >= 0.5) mutations relative to low-frequency ones (0.21 vs 0.12 with driver effect 0.5; 0.19 vs 0.10 with 1.0), so the pre-registered 2-fold enrichment was not supported. In the neutral control none of 41 high-CCF mutations was a driver (0.046 among low-CCF); the 'no difference' test was not supported only because a zero share gives a zero binomial SE, a flaw in the test, not evidence of depletion. *Operationalization:* Divergence = selection shaping which branches become clonal. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Non-spatial toy; whole-population CCF; binomial SE ignores clone structure and is degenerate at zero.
- **DVD-APP-11** [P] P17's statements reproduced exactly: forward Euler on the oscillator grew by 37% (h = 0.01) and 3.2% (h = 0.001) over 10 periods, matching (1 + h^2 w^2)^(n/2), while semi-implicit Euler stayed within 0.25%; the global error slope was 1.003 (O(h)); and h|lambda| = 1.99 decayed while 2.01 grew without bound. *Operationalization:* Divergence = method-induced numerical divergence. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-APP-B/iter-6#V2. *Limits:* Linear problems; semi-implicit Euler is the agent's comparison, not in P17.
- **DVD-APP-12** [P] With a pooled-SE test (fixing the DVB design flaw) the no-advantage control showed no enrichment (driver share 0.038 high-CCF vs 0.049 low-CCF: supported), strong drivers (sd 1.0) were enriched among clonal mutations (0.20 vs 0.09: supported), and moderate drivers (sd 0.5, 0.22 vs 0.12) were not supported at 4 pooled SE. *Operationalization:* Selection shaping clonal divergence. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Toy branching model; few high-CCF mutations (79-125).
- **DVD-APP-13** [P] P17's forward-Euler results held: on the oscillator amplitude grew 22.7-fold over 10 periods at h = 0.1 and 1.37-fold at h = 0.01, exactly as (1 + h^2)^(n/2); at h = 1e-4 growth was only 0.31% (the pre-stated 'not supported'); error slopes were 1.018 and 1.001 (O(h)); and |1 - h lam| > 1 diverged exactly as predicted. *Operationalization:* Numerical divergence (replications). *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-6#V2;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-8#H2;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-9#H1. *Limits:* Linear test problems only.
- **DVD-APP-14** [P] The DVA finding replicated: the neutral clonal tail's 1/f fit had median R^2 0.90 (range 0.88-0.96), not supported at the 0.98 criterion, so that criterion looks too strict for tumours of 5,000 cells. *Operationalization:* Neutral subclonal tail (replication). *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-DV-APP-C/iter-7#H1. *Limits:* Five replicates; the 0.98 criterion is from DVA's SPEC, not from P04.
- **DVD-APP-17** [P] P24's printed conversions are consistent: 600/50 = 12, 600/23 = 26.1 ('about 26'), 600/48 = 12.5 ('about 12'), 600/15 = 40 s; and the fluency ranking reverses when only Exploration time is used (Sj1 faster in total, Sj2 in Exploration). P24's subjects are fictive (its footnote 1), and P24 once prints 'MCTI' for MTCI. *Operationalization:* APP Divergence = ideas per unit time. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-1#H2;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-1#H3. *Limits:* Checks printed arithmetic only.
- **DVD-APP-18** [P] In a toy where fluency and true originality are unrelated, a summative originality score still correlated 0.90 with fluency (supported), 0.92 when they were related (rho 0.3), 0.82 with less fluency spread and 0.90 in a short task; the average score tracked true originality less well in the low-fluency half (0.78 vs 0.89; 0.55 vs 0.77 in the short task). This reproduces in a model the fluency-dependency P24 describes. *Operationalization:* APP Divergence measurement caution. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-2#H2;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-3#H2;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-4#H2;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-5#H2. *Limits:* Agent's toy; the 0.8 threshold is from P24's cited range.
- **DVD-APP-19** [P] Retrieving ideas without replacement from a skewed accessibility distribution reproduced both serial-order trends (uncommonness rising, waits lengthening: supported for a = 1 and a = 2); with equal accessibility (a = 0) neither trend appeared (not supported, as expected). The skew, not the sequence itself, drives the effect in this model. *Operationalization:* APP Divergence = idea generation moving from common to uncommon. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-6#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-6#H2;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-7#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-7#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-7#H2;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-8#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-8#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-8#H2. *Limits:* One candidate mechanism; P24 reports the effect, not this mechanism (it cites executive and associative accounts).
- **DVD-APP-20** [P] P24's proposed 'originality cost' (the extra exploration time for a more uncommon response) was recovered without bias by per-person regression, but with P24's 18 trials individual estimates correlated only 0.77 with the true cost (not supported at > 0.8), 0.56 with noisier times; 40 trials gave 0.85 (supported). *Operationalization:* APP Divergence = effort-time to reach uncommon ideas. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-9#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-9#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-10#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-10#H1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-11#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-11#H1. *Limits:* Agent's parameter choices; P24 gives no data.
- **DVD-APP-21** [P] Seed replication of the DVC pooled-SE driver test repeated its verdict: driver share 0.207 among high-CCF vs 0.117 among low-CCF mutations, difference below 4 pooled SE (0.030), not supported. *Operationalization:* APP Divergence = clonal branching. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-12#V1;SIM-DV-APP-D/iter-12#H1. *Limits:* As DVC.
- **DVD-APP-22** [H] In design processes, divergence (generating many ideas) is paired with a selecting step: P24 notes that divergent thinking itself involves monitoring and selection of ideas. This parallels the divergence-with-counter-movement pattern seen in other fields (DVB cross-field notes). *Operationalization:* APP Divergence paired with convergent selection. *Sources:* P24. *Limits:* Framework interpretation; P24 does not draw the cross-field parallel.
