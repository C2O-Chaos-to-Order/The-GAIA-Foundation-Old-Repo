# Divergence in the Applied Sciences — Cycle-0-Baseline (DV0)

Phase report: Divergence (`../../README.md`). Definitions and competing definitions: `../DEFINITIONS.md`. Labels: **E** Established · **P** Plausible · **PS** Philosophical/Spiritual · **H** Hypothetical. GAIA's Phase axis is a framework category (PS), kept separate from the findings.

## Reading of this cycle
In medicine, divergence is branching clonal evolution; that tumours branch is established for the sampled cases, but whether the branching is neutral is contested.

## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DV0-APP-01** [E] Multiregion genome and epigenome profiling of nine colorectal tumours showed clonally shared early alterations followed, in the late phase, by branching of a parental clone into numerous subclones; known driver alterations were frequent among early and rare among late alterations. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branching of subclones (intratumour heterogeneity). *Sources:* P05. *Limits:* Nine tumours, one centre; region sampling limits subclone detection.
- **DV0-APP-02** [P] Whether late subclonal divergence is mostly neutral is contested: P05's simulation suggests extensive intratumour heterogeneity could be generated by neutral evolution, and P04 presents punctuated ('Big Bang') evolution with predominantly neutral expansion as an alternative to gradualist selection-driven models. *Operationalization:* Competing models of tumour divergence: neutral vs selective, gradual vs punctuated. *Sources:* P05;P04. *Limits:* P04 is a narrative review; neutrality tests from frequency spectra are themselves disputed (not yet sourced here).
- **DV0-APP-03** [P] A 2026 review reports evidence that the linear-progression model of tumour evolution is less frequent than expected and that parallel or branched evolution is common, with the clinical consequence that a single primary-tumour biopsy may miss divergent subclones. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branched rather than linear progression. *Sources:* P04. *Limits:* Secondhand report of the cited studies, which were not read here.
- **DV0-APP-04** [P] In one neutral birth-death growth run to 5,000 cells, the cumulative count of subclonal mutations was approximately but not tightly linear in 1/f (R^2 = 0.958); the pre-registered criterion R^2 >= 0.98 was not supported. *Operationalization:* Divergence = neutral clonal branching signature. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Single run, cell fractions without sequencing noise; the criterion may be too strict for one stochastic realisation. To be tested with replicates in x3.
