# Divergence in the Philosophy Spirituality — Cycle-B-x6 (DVB)

Phase report: Divergence (`../../README.md`). Definitions and competing definitions: `../DEFINITIONS.md`. Labels: **E** Established · **P** Plausible · **PS** Philosophical/Spiritual · **H** Hypothetical. GAIA's Phase axis is a framework category (PS), kept separate from the findings.

## Reading of this cycle
x6 re-reads P07 and P08 (P07's author corrected to Kalligas) and adds a non-Western source (P18, Laozi, cited passages). Procession comes paired with reversion in Plotinus and contraction in Bergson; a formal split-merge model shows paired branching and merging gives bounded multiplicity.

## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVB-PHI-01** [E] Bergson held that if the original vital impulse is common to all life, there must also be a principle of divergence and differentiation explaining evolution, and that the successive bifurcations of life organise into two great opposite tendencies, instinct and intelligence, which remain inseparable tendencies rather than self-contained states. *Operationalization:* Divergence = differentiation of tendencies from a common impulse. *Sources:* P08. *Limits:* Reports a philosophical position, not its truth.
- **DVB-PHI-02** [E] In Plotinus, the 'theory of the two activities' is the cornerstone of the so-called process of 'emanation', by which the principles of reality other than the first come to be constituted. *Operationalization:* Divergence = procession of the many from the One. *Sources:* P07. *Limits:* The SEP entry itself flags 'emanation' as a conventional label ('so-called').
- **DVB-PHI-05** [E] Across 640,110 respondents in 117 countries (World Values Survey and European Values Study), religion is the cultural trait with the largest cross-country divergence by the Cultural Fixation Index: personal emphasis on religion varies 5-10 times more across countries than emphasis on family, politics, or work effort. *Operationalization:* Divergence = between-country share of variance in religious values (CFst). *Sources:* P15. *Limits:* Self-reported values; the surveys may miss traits (e.g. national identity) that diverge even more, as the authors note.
- **DVB-PHI-08** [E] In cultural evolution, phylogenetic trees are one line of evidence among several: horizontal transmission, incomplete lineage sorting and independent invention can make histories non-tree-like, and phylogenetic networks allow hybrid nodes with two parents. *Operationalization:* Limits of the tree image of divergence. *Sources:* P14. *Limits:* Filed under PHI as philosophy of the branching image; it is equally a HUM/SOC methods point (overlap disclosed).
- **DVB-PHI-13** [E] In Plotinus, procession is never alone: the three stages of abiding, procession and reversion comprise a dialectical schema that runs through his entire system. *Operationalization:* Divergence (procession) paired with return (reversion). *Sources:* P07. *Limits:* SEP summary (Kalligas 2024) of a contested interpretive field.
- **DVB-PHI-14** [E] According to P07, in Plotinus love constitutes the main motive for the re-ascent to the Good through reversion. *Operationalization:* Return movement motivated by love (historical doctrine). *Sources:* P07. *Limits:* A report of a doctrine, not evidence that love causes anything.
- **DVB-PHI-15** [E] Bergson's memory cone moves from unity to multiplicity, and the reverse process (contraction) is also virtual; Bergson lectured many times on Plotinus, though for him thinking is not mere contemplation. *Operationalization:* Two-way movement between unity and multiplicity. *Sources:* P08. *Limits:* SEP summary.
- **DVB-PHI-16** [E] The Laozi (ch. 42) describes a process of differentiation from unity to multiplicity ('Dao gives birth to One; One gives birth to Two ...'), and speaks of reversal and return to Dao; Chan cautions that ch. 42 states a logical relation (duality presupposes unity), not a cosmogonic event. *Operationalization:* Divergence = differentiation from unity to multiplicity (Daoist). *Sources:* P18. *Limits:* Read at cited passages only; commentarial readings differ (Heshang Gong cosmological, Wang Bi logical).
- **DVB-PHI-06** [P] About one-third of religion's cross-country divergence is attributable to affiliation with different traditions and about two-thirds to differences in the intensity of religiosity; the authors suggest secularization now contributes more to religious divergence than differences between faiths. *Operationalization:* Divergence in intensity vs divergence by tradition. *Sources:* P15. *Limits:* Decomposition by residualising on denomination dummies; the secularization reading is the authors' interpretation.
- **DVB-PHI-07** [P] Evans et al. note that the spread of world religions might show tree-like nested schisms, but such trees would record much more recent events than language trees; reconstructing Christianity's history on an Indo-European language tree 'would thus make no sense'. *Operationalization:* Divergence = religious schism as branching. *Sources:* P14. *Limits:* A remark in a methods review, not a phylogeny of religions.
- **DVB-PHI-09** [P] Re-running DV0's 'one -> many' process (alpha = 1, n = 1,000, 300 replicates, new seed) with a tolerance of 4 Monte Carlo standard errors gave 7.543 branches against the analytic 7.485 (SE 0.146): Pass. DV0's V1 Fail is therefore attributed to its fixed 5% band, which ignored sampling error; DV0's Fail stays on record and DV0-PHI-04 keeps its Hypothetical label. *Operationalization:* Formal skeleton of 'one becoming many'. *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Formal process only; models no tradition.
- **DVB-PHI-10** [P] On synthetic data with a known population value of 0.20, the Cultural Fixation Index recovered 0.2016 for a continuous trait, but the same trait coded yes/no gave 0.131 (35% lower): CFst comparisons are only meaningful between items on the same response scale, which is why P15 compares within question sets. *Operationalization:* Measurement of cross-population divergence. *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Normal latent traits and a median split; real items differ in skew and wording.
- **DVB-PHI-11** [P] A constant-rate splitting ('schism') process gave 20.18 groups at time 3 against the expected e^3 = 20.09; when splitting rates were heritable and variable, trees grown to 30 groups were much more lopsided (normalised Colless index 0.319 vs 0.174): uneven branching of traditions is what one would expect if propensity to split is inherited. *Operationalization:* Divergence = nested schism tree shape. *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Pure-birth model with no extinction or mergers; P14 stresses reticulation, which this toy ignores. It models no real tradition.
- **DVB-PHI-17** [P] When branching (rate 1 per group) is paired with merging (rate mu per pair), the number of groups settles at an exact zero-truncated-Poisson level (simulated 19.97, 4.06, 100.2 vs exact 20.00, 4.07, 100.0 for mu = 0.1, 0.5, 0.02); the mean-field estimate 1 + 2/mu is within 10% only when multiplicity is large (not supported at mu = 0.5: 5.0 vs 4.06). Without merging, groups grow exponentially (Yule control). *Operationalization:* Divergence bounded by a return movement (formal). *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-1#V2;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-2#V2;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-3#V2;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Formal skeleton only; models no tradition and is not evidence about Plotinus, Bergson or the Laozi.
- **DVB-PHI-18** [P] Replications: the one-to-many process matched its analytic branch counts at alpha 0.5, 1, 2 (top-branch share 0.78 at alpha 0.5); dichotomising a trait cut CFst by 33% at a small true divergence (0.06); heritable splitting-rate variation (SD 1.0) raised schism-tree imbalance from 0.17 to 0.53. *Operationalization:* Divergence measurement and branching structure. *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-B/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Formal toys; P14/P15 caveats as in DVA.
- **DVB-PHI-03** [PS] Plotinian procession and Bergsonian divergence are both 'one-to-many' accounts but differ in kind: one derives a hierarchy of principles from a first principle, the other derives diverging life tendencies from a common impulse in time; treating both as one 'Divergence phase' is an interpretive mapping, not a shared mechanism. *Operationalization:* Divergence as a cross-tradition philosophical category. *Sources:* P07;P08. *Limits:* Interpretation by this report; other readings (e.g. Bergson's criticism of finalism) are not yet compared.
- **DVB-PHI-12** [PS] Treating religious schism, the cross-country spread in religiosity and the metaphysical 'one into many' as instances of one Divergence phase is a GAIA framework reading: the sources show different structures (branching of traditions in P14, between-country variance in intensity of belief in P15, metaphysical procession in DV0's P07/P08). *Operationalization:* Cross-structure reading of spiritual/religious divergence. *Sources:* P14;P15. *Limits:* Framework interpretation; not evidence that a single phase exists.
- **DVB-PHI-19** [PS] Reading Plotinus' procession-with-reversion (with love as the motive of return), Bergson's two-way movement of the memory cone and the Laozi's differentiation with reversal as one pattern, divergence that is paired with a return, is a GAIA framework reading. It is the hook for the Master Report's unconditional/conditional love theme, but no source states the pattern across traditions, and Chan warns against reading ch. 42 as an event. *Operationalization:* Cross-tradition reading of divergence-and-return. *Sources:* P07;P08;P18. *Limits:* Framework interpretation; cross-tradition parallels are not evidence of a common structure.
- **DVB-PHI-04** [H] A Chinese restaurant process started from a single group produced on average 7.88 branches for n = 1,000 (alpha = 1) with a dominant branch holding 61% on average; the validation check (within 5% of the analytic 7.49) failed at 5.2%, so the run is not a validated result. *Operationalization:* Divergence = one-to-many branching (formal skeleton). *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* V1 Fail is attributed to an under-tolerant check (no Monte Carlo tolerance; 300 replicates give SE about 0.14), supported by an out-of-record diagnostic with 3,000 replicates (7.52); recorded, not re-run; fixed tolerance planned for x3. Formal skeleton only: models no tradition.
