## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVB-NAT-01** [E] In a speciation continuum of hybridizing races and incipient species of Heliconius erato-clade butterflies, genomic divergence increases with ecological and reproductive isolation, both at the red colour-pattern locus and at genomic regions unlinked to colour pattern. *Operationalization:* Divergence = genomic differentiation between populations along a speciation continuum. *Sources:* P01. *Limits:* 2.2 Mb of the H. erato genome only; a handful of population pairs; continuum stages are proxies for speciation stages, not a time series.
- **DVB-NAT-04** [E] Darwin's 1837-1838 notebook states that 'organized beings represent a tree irregularly branched', and the text beside an early sketch concerns genealogical distance between descendants of a common ancestor, where Darwin would later locate his principle of divergence of characteristics. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branching descent from a common ancestor (Darwin's principle of divergence). *Sources:* P02. *Limits:* Reported through a history-of-science article; the notebook itself was not read here.
- **DVB-NAT-05** [E] The epigenetic landscape was a visual metaphor introduced in the mid-twentieth century to illustrate the genetic control of embryonic differentiation; Waddington pictured development as a marble rolling down a rugged hillside whose valleys are held in place by genes. *Operationalization:* Divergence = developmental differentiation of cell fates (landscape metaphor). *Sources:* P10. *Limits:* P10 is a short editorial perspective, not a research paper; it describes the metaphor and does not test it.
- **DVB-NAT-09** [E] In Heliconius erato, unlinked loci show high divergence between the incipient species H. himera and H. e. cyrbia (average Fst 0.519, 95% CI 0.507-0.531), well above any within-species comparison (maximum average 0.191), so divergence is not confined to colour-pattern loci even with gene flow. *Operationalization:* Divergence = genome-wide differentiation during speciation with gene flow. *Sources:* P01. *Limits:* One butterfly system; Fst from the authors' marker set; the paper cautions that peaks of divergence need supporting data.
- **DVB-NAT-10** [E] Around colour-pattern loci the effect of selection is restricted to a small region, with relative divergence decaying between 1 and 2 cM from the causative locus; the authors warn that peaks of divergence should be interpreted with caution. *Operationalization:* Divergence = locally elevated differentiation decaying with map distance. *Sources:* P01. *Limits:* Decay distance from one study design; divergence hitchhiking is the authors' interpretation.
- **DVB-NAT-11** [E] In Darwin's Origin diagram, horizontal typographic spacing encodes the amount of divergence: the wider the spacing, the greater the divergence. *Operationalization:* Divergence = depicted distance between lineages. *Sources:* P02. *Limits:* A historian's reading of the diagram's conventions.
- **DVB-NAT-02** [P] The same study infers that divergence may accumulate early in the speciation process and that limited gene flow cannot account for the high divergence between the incipient species H. erato and H. himera. *Operationalization:* Divergence with gene flow (divergence proceeding despite exchange). *Sources:* P01. *Limits:* Inference from one incipient-species pair; alternative histories (e.g. older isolation) are not excluded by the data shown.
- **DVB-NAT-03** [P] In a neutral two-deme Wright-Fisher model (N = 100, 400 loci), drift alone raised Fst to 0.385 after 200 generations (heterozygosity decay matched theory within 5%), whereas symmetric migration with Nm = 1 held Fst at 0.056: gene flow strongly restrains neutral divergence. *Operationalization:* Divergence = Fst growth between demes. *Sources:* SIM-DV-NAT-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-NAT-0/iter-1#H2. *Limits:* Toy neutral model; the H1 threshold was moved from 0.5 to 0.25 after a pre-spec smoke test (disclosed in the SPEC). Selection, which drives the colour-locus divergence in P01, is absent.
- **DVB-NAT-06** [P] The word 'epigenetics' has itself diverged: P10's author judges that so many studies are now called epigenetic that one hardly knows what is and what is not epigenetic, and that much of the field has veered far from Waddington's landscape concept. *Operationalization:* Divergence = semantic drift of a scientific term. *Sources:* P10. *Limits:* An editor's view supported by a keyword map of 2017-2021 publications; not a systematic conceptual analysis.
- **DVB-NAT-07** [P] In a two-deme Wright-Fisher toy (N = 100, Nm = 1, 200 generations), neutral loci stayed near Fst 0.06 while a locus under divergent selection rose with s: 0.06 (s = 0), 0.10 (0.01), 0.18 (0.02), 0.46 (0.05), 0.67 (0.1). The pre-registered 'island' criterion (selected Fst > 0.5 with neutral Fst < 0.2) was not supported at s = 0.05 (0.45 in iteration 1) and supported at s = 0.1. *Operationalization:* Divergence = locus-specific differentiation under divergent selection with gene flow. *Sources:* SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-2#H2. *Limits:* Unlinked loci and genic selection; no hitchhiking, so this is a migration-selection balance toy, not a genomic-island model of P01's butterflies.
- **DVB-NAT-08** [P] In a one-dimensional pitchfork 'landscape' whose single valley splits into two, 2,000 identical cells with no bias divided 48.9% / 51.1% between the valleys (consistent with 50/50 within 4 standard errors), while a bias of 0.05 applied during the split sent 95.7% into one valley: near a bifurcation a small asymmetry decides most fates. *Operationalization:* Divergence = bifurcation of a state space into separate attractors. *Sources:* SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-3#V2;SIM-DV-NAT-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* A normal-form toy, not a gene-network model; P10 supplies only the metaphor.
- **DVB-NAT-12** [P] In a two-deme linkage toy (N = 200, 400 generations), neutral Fst fell with recombination distance from a divergently selected locus: 0.52, 0.51, 0.34, 0.17, 0.07 for c = 0, 0.001, 0.01, 0.05, 0.5 (s = 0.1, m = 0.01). Weaker selection (s = 0.03) gave a lower island (0.27 to 0.07) and more gene flow (m = 0.05) homogenised the background (0.13 to 0.02). The no-selection control showed no decline (0.05-0.07). The pre-registered 'decay within c = 0.01' criterion was not supported at s = 0.1 (0.34 vs midpoint 0.30) but supported at s = 0.03 and m = 0.05; in the control the island check was not supported, as expected. *Operationalization:* Divergence = island of differentiation around a selected locus. *Sources:* SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-1#H3;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-2#H3;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-3#H3;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-4#H1. *Limits:* Haploid star-linkage toy; c is a per-generation recombination probability, not Heliconius cM; random starting frequencies, no secondary contact.
- **DVB-NAT-13** [P] Doubling deme size (N = 200, Nm = 2) reproduced the DVA dose-response at an unlinked selected locus (Fst 0.03 to 0.68 from s = 0 to 0.1, neutral about 0.03); the island criterion was again met only at s = 0.1 (0.455 at s = 0.05). *Operationalization:* Divergence = migration-selection balance. *Sources:* SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-5#H2. *Limits:* Same unlinked toy as DVA.
- **DVB-NAT-14** [P] At a pitchfork bifurcation, biases of 0.005, 0.01 and 0.02 sent 56%, 62% and 76% of 2,000 cells into one valley (50.2% with no bias); the pre-registered '> 90% at the largest bias' was not supported, so with noise sigma 0.1 a bias of about 0.05 (DVA) rather than 0.02 is needed for a near-unanimous fate split. *Operationalization:* Divergence = bifurcation sensitivity to small asymmetries. *Sources:* SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-6#V2;SIM-DV-NAT-B/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Normal-form toy; P10 provides only the metaphor.
