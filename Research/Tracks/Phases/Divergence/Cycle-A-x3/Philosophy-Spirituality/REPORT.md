# Divergence in the Philosophy Spirituality — Cycle-A-x3 (DVA)

Phase report: Divergence (`../../README.md`). Definitions and competing definitions: `../DEFINITIONS.md`. Labels: **E** Established · **P** Plausible · **PS** Philosophical/Spiritual · **H** Hypothetical. GAIA's Phase axis is a framework category (PS), kept separate from the findings.

## Reading of this cycle

## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVA-PHI-01** [E] Bergson held that if the original vital impulse is common to all life, there must also be a principle of divergence and differentiation explaining evolution, and that the successive bifurcations of life organise into two great opposite tendencies, instinct and intelligence, which remain inseparable tendencies rather than self-contained states. *Operationalization:* Divergence = differentiation of tendencies from a common impulse. *Sources:* P08. *Limits:* Reports a philosophical position, not its truth.
- **DVA-PHI-02** [E] In Plotinus, the 'theory of the two activities' is the cornerstone of the so-called process of 'emanation', by which the principles of reality other than the first come to be constituted. *Operationalization:* Divergence = procession of the many from the One. *Sources:* P07. *Limits:* The SEP entry itself flags 'emanation' as a conventional label ('so-called').
- **DVA-PHI-05** [E] Across 640,110 respondents in 117 countries (World Values Survey and European Values Study), religion is the cultural trait with the largest cross-country divergence by the Cultural Fixation Index: personal emphasis on religion varies 5-10 times more across countries than emphasis on family, politics, or work effort. *Operationalization:* Divergence = between-country share of variance in religious values (CFst). *Sources:* P15. *Limits:* Self-reported values; the surveys may miss traits (e.g. national identity) that diverge even more, as the authors note.
- **DVA-PHI-08** [E] In cultural evolution, phylogenetic trees are one line of evidence among several: horizontal transmission, incomplete lineage sorting and independent invention can make histories non-tree-like, and phylogenetic networks allow hybrid nodes with two parents. *Operationalization:* Limits of the tree image of divergence. *Sources:* P14. *Limits:* Filed under PHI as philosophy of the branching image; it is equally a HUM/SOC methods point (overlap disclosed).
- **DVA-PHI-06** [P] About one-third of religion's cross-country divergence is attributable to affiliation with different traditions and about two-thirds to differences in the intensity of religiosity; the authors suggest secularization now contributes more to religious divergence than differences between faiths. *Operationalization:* Divergence in intensity vs divergence by tradition. *Sources:* P15. *Limits:* Decomposition by residualising on denomination dummies; the secularization reading is the authors' interpretation.
- **DVA-PHI-07** [P] Evans et al. note that the spread of world religions might show tree-like nested schisms, but such trees would record much more recent events than language trees; reconstructing Christianity's history on an Indo-European language tree 'would thus make no sense'. *Operationalization:* Divergence = religious schism as branching. *Sources:* P14. *Limits:* A remark in a methods review, not a phylogeny of religions.
- **DVA-PHI-09** [P] Re-running DV0's 'one -> many' process (alpha = 1, n = 1,000, 300 replicates, new seed) with a tolerance of 4 Monte Carlo standard errors gave 7.543 branches against the analytic 7.485 (SE 0.146): Pass. DV0's V1 Fail is therefore attributed to its fixed 5% band, which ignored sampling error; DV0's Fail stays on record and DV0-PHI-04 keeps its Hypothetical label. *Operationalization:* Formal skeleton of 'one becoming many'. *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Formal process only; models no tradition.
- **DVA-PHI-10** [P] On synthetic data with a known population value of 0.20, the Cultural Fixation Index recovered 0.2016 for a continuous trait, but the same trait coded yes/no gave 0.131 (35% lower): CFst comparisons are only meaningful between items on the same response scale, which is why P15 compares within question sets. *Operationalization:* Measurement of cross-population divergence. *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Normal latent traits and a median split; real items differ in skew and wording.
- **DVA-PHI-11** [P] A constant-rate splitting ('schism') process gave 20.18 groups at time 3 against the expected e^3 = 20.09; when splitting rates were heritable and variable, trees grown to 30 groups were much more lopsided (normalised Colless index 0.319 vs 0.174): uneven branching of traditions is what one would expect if propensity to split is inherited. *Operationalization:* Divergence = nested schism tree shape. *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Pure-birth model with no extinction or mergers; P14 stresses reticulation, which this toy ignores. It models no real tradition.
- **DVA-PHI-03** [PS] Plotinian procession and Bergsonian divergence are both 'one-to-many' accounts but differ in kind: one derives a hierarchy of principles from a first principle, the other derives diverging life tendencies from a common impulse in time; treating both as one 'Divergence phase' is an interpretive mapping, not a shared mechanism. *Operationalization:* Divergence as a cross-tradition philosophical category. *Sources:* P07;P08. *Limits:* Interpretation by this report; other readings (e.g. Bergson's criticism of finalism) are not yet compared.
- **DVA-PHI-12** [PS] Treating religious schism, the cross-country spread in religiosity and the metaphysical 'one into many' as instances of one Divergence phase is a GAIA framework reading: the sources show different structures (branching of traditions in P14, between-country variance in intensity of belief in P15, metaphysical procession in DV0's P07/P08). *Operationalization:* Cross-structure reading of spiritual/religious divergence. *Sources:* P14;P15. *Limits:* Framework interpretation; not evidence that a single phase exists.
- **DVA-PHI-04** [H] A Chinese restaurant process started from a single group produced on average 7.88 branches for n = 1,000 (alpha = 1) with a dominant branch holding 61% on average; the validation check (within 5% of the analytic 7.49) failed at 5.2%, so the run is not a validated result. *Operationalization:* Divergence = one-to-many branching (formal skeleton). *Sources:* SIM-DV-PHI-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-PHI-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* V1 Fail is attributed to an under-tolerant check (no Monte Carlo tolerance; 300 replicates give SE about 0.14), supported by an out-of-record diagnostic with 3,000 replicates (7.52); recorded, not re-run; fixed tolerance planned for x3. Formal skeleton only: models no tradition.
