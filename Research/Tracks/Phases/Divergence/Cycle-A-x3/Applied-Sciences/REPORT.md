## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **DVA-APP-01** [E] Multiregion genome and epigenome profiling of nine colorectal tumours showed clonally shared early alterations followed, in the late phase, by branching of a parental clone into numerous subclones; known driver alterations were frequent among early and rare among late alterations. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branching of subclones (intratumour heterogeneity). *Sources:* P05. *Limits:* Nine tumours, one centre; region sampling limits subclone detection.
- **DVA-APP-02** [P] Whether late subclonal divergence is mostly neutral is contested: P05's simulation suggests extensive intratumour heterogeneity could be generated by neutral evolution, and P04 presents punctuated ('Big Bang') evolution with predominantly neutral expansion as an alternative to gradualist selection-driven models. *Operationalization:* Competing models of tumour divergence: neutral vs selective, gradual vs punctuated. *Sources:* P05;P04. *Limits:* P04 is a narrative review; neutrality tests from frequency spectra are themselves disputed (not yet sourced here).
- **DVA-APP-03** [P] A 2026 review reports evidence that the linear-progression model of tumour evolution is less frequent than expected and that parallel or branched evolution is common, with the clinical consequence that a single primary-tumour biopsy may miss divergent subclones. *Operationalization:* Divergence = branched rather than linear progression. *Sources:* P04. *Limits:* Secondhand report of the cited studies, which were not read here.
- **DVA-APP-04** [P] In one neutral birth-death growth run to 5,000 cells, the cumulative count of subclonal mutations was approximately but not tightly linear in 1/f (R^2 = 0.958); the pre-registered criterion R^2 >= 0.98 was not supported. *Operationalization:* Divergence = neutral clonal branching signature. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-0/iter-1#H1. *Limits:* Single run, cell fractions without sequencing noise; the criterion may be too strict for one stochastic realisation. To be tested with replicates in x3.
- **DVA-APP-05** [P] Across 10 replicates of DV0's neutral clonal-branching toy (5 at N = 5,000 and 5 at N = 20,000 cells), R^2 of the M(f) ~ 1/f fit ranged from 0.916 to 0.985 (medians 0.954 and 0.976); the pre-registered median >= 0.98 was not supported at either size, so DV0's single value of 0.958 was typical rather than an outlier. *Operationalization:* Divergence = neutral branching clonal evolution (1/f signature). *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Cell fractions without sequencing noise; the 0.98 threshold is the agent's, not a published criterion; 4 extinct starts were restarted and are reported.
- **DVA-APP-06** [P] Explicit Euler on y' = -lam y converged for h lam = 0.5 to 1.9 and diverged for h lam = 2.1 and 2.5 (|y| about 2.6e12 and 6.7e52 after 300 steps), exactly as the amplification factor |1 - h lam| predicts: numerical divergence of a solver is a property of the method and step size, not of the system solved. *Operationalization:* Divergence = numerical instability of an iterative method. *Sources:* SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-DV-APP-A/iter-3#V2. *Limits:* Linear test equation only; no source on numerical analysis was read in this track.
