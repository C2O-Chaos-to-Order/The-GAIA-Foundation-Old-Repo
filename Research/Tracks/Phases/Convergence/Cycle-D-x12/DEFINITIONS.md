# Convergence — operationalizations and competing definitions

Status: Proposed working definitions for the GAIA Phase axis (Research/README.md §9.3, §12 Q1, Q4). No repository document defines "Convergence" (Research/README.md only lists it as a phase), so each field's reading below is this track's choice. The word has a precise technical meaning in several fields (a sequence or process approaching a limit), and the readings below use that sense where it exists. Competing definitions are recorded, not resolved.

Owner context (recorded 2026-10-24; does not change methods): the owner maps phases to upgrade levels (Base = Divergence, Meta = Insurgence, Super = Allegiance, Hyper = Convergence, Ultra = Ascendence, Magic = Transcendence). "Convergence corresponds to Hyper / Hyperphysical" is the owner's **Hypothetical** claim; it is not tested here and no claim depends on it.

## Cross-field observations (Baseline)
- **Convergence has conditions**: oscillators lock only below P68's bound on the deviating share; chains converge only if regular (P73); inquirers merge only if priors give weight to the truth and evidence separates hypotheses (PL-49, Hypothetical).
- **Weighted averaging**: DeGroot consensus and P69's centralized estimate share the weighted-mean form (PL-48, Plausible).
- **Convergence claims need checking**: P70's printed convergence errors and savings do not reproduce, while P71's statistics do.

## Cross-field observations (x3)
- **One object, two fields**: P74's null for chance molecular convergence is a two-state Markov chain, the same object as P73's regular chains (PL-51, Established).
- **Who sets the consensus**: a stubborn agent fixes DeGroot consensus, the extreme of P69's centralization (PL-52, Plausible).
- **Agreement is not truth**: Peirce's methods converge on the truth only if every error, including shared ones, shrinks (toy; P75).
- **Convergence vs loyalty**: Peirce contrasts inquiry with settling opinion where 'the idea of loyalty replaced that of truth-seeking' (PL-53, Hypothetical).

## Cross-field observations (x6)
- **How fast, not only whether**: mixing time (P78) turns 'the chain converges' into a number; P74's null needs 186 steps to come within 1/4 of equilibrium (PL-54, Established).
- **Limit formulas hold near their limit**: P76's onset formula and P79's asymptotic error are exact where they apply and wrong far from it (PL-56, Plausible).
- **A part that never updates blocks convergence**: two stubborn agents (FOR) and never-improving methods (PHI toy) (PL-55, Hypothetical).
- **Identical-unit results may not transfer**: P68's two-cluster bound fails under small frequency heterogeneity (CVB-NAT-10).
- Source caution: P78's 'seven shuffles' sentence holds at eps = 1/2, not at the eps = 1/4 it fixes (CVB-FOR-07).

## Cross-field observations (x9)
- **Contact can mimic common origin**: dialects level toward the demographic majority through contact (P82), and in a toy widespread contact between traditions gives similarity without a distance gradient, like a common core; the history of contact decides, not the similarity (PL-57, Hypothetical; P81's 'independent of contact' premise).
- **Finite systems sharpen toward their limit**: the riffle shuffle has a 3-4 shuffle cutoff at every deck size and the Kuramoto incoherent floor falls as N^-1/2 (PL-58, Plausible).
- **Bounds can be valid and uninformative**: P69's lower bound never exceeds the simulated advantage but is near zero in P69's own Fig. 2 contexts (CVC-SOC-08).
- **Periodicity cuts both ways**: a periodic chain never mixes, while periodic integrands make the trapezoidal rule converge spectrally (CVC-FOR-11, CVC-APP-07).
- Source caution: P79's Gaussian '1.8 times' and rough-function comparison were not reproduced under the agent's operationalisations (CVC-APP-08); P82's New Zealand dates carry 'citation needed'.

## Cross-field observations (x12)
- **No detected difference is not sameness**: P83 found no difference in mystical experience across nine chanting traditions, but its ANOVA had 53% power at f 0.15, and in a toy an instrument that measures three of six dimensions misses differences on the other three; quadrature rules likewise 'agree' at 0.1% and differ three-fold at 1e-6 (PL-59, Hypothetical; CVD-PHI-12, CVD-PHI-14, CVD-APP-10).
- **The instrument may presuppose the convergence**: P84 traces the MEQ used by P83 to Pahnke and Stace and argues it imports a perennialist 'common core' (CVD-PHI-13, Plausible).
- **Direction depends on context**: conformity overrides weak prestige in the levelling toy, and P69's centralization advantage reverses with bias and dispersion (PL-60, Hypothetical).
- Source caution: P83's Welch religiosity test (t(327) = 5.395) and 2 x 2 ANOVA df do not recompute from its printed statistics under the 60% split (CVD-PHI-10, CVD-PHI-11).
- Saturation: FOR and APP are saturated on their current sources (10 and 8 of 12 x12 passes added nothing).
