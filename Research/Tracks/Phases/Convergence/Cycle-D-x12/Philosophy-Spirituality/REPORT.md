# Convergence in the Philosophy Spirituality — Cycle-D-x12 (CVD)

Phase report: Convergence (`../../README.md`). Definitions and competing definitions: `../DEFINITIONS.md`. Labels: **E** Established · **P** Plausible · **PS** Philosophical/Spiritual · **H** Hypothetical. GAIA's Phase axis is a framework category (PS), kept separate from the findings.

## Reading of this cycle
x12: first empirical PHI sources (P83 survey, P84 critique); P83's 'no difference across traditions' is low-powered and partly misreported, and a toy shows a common-core instrument can miss real differences.

- **CVD-PHI-12** [E] Power of P83's one-way ANOVA across nine traditions (N 401): 0.23 for a small effect (f 0.10), 0.53 for f 0.15 and 0.97 for f 0.25, so 80% power is reached only from f = 0.196. P83's null result across traditions therefore does not exclude small to moderate differences between traditions. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = sameness across traditions vs absence of detected difference. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-7#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-7#H1. *Limits:* Standard noncentral-F computation with equal group sizes (P83's groups are unequal); says nothing about whether differences exist.
- **CVD-PHI-09** [P] P83 surveyed 464 regular chanters from 33 countries with the MEQ30: 60% met the criterion for a complete mystical experience (over 60% on all four factors), and neither the chi-square test of tradition (chi2(8) = 12.573, p = 0.127) nor a one-way ANOVA across nine traditions (F(8,392) = 1.367, p = 0.209) found a difference by tradition, although subscale profiles differed (interaction F(24,1176) = 1.828, p = 0.009). Taketina, a practice not tied to a belief system, had the highest proportion of complete mystical experiences, which P83 calls surprising; P83 presents the features that may promote mystical experience during chanting as a convergence of factors. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = convergence of mystical experience across traditions. *Sources:* P83. *Limits:* Self-selected, mostly Australian and US, 75% female sample; cross-sectional self-report with an instrument whose perennialist origin P84 criticises; tradition groups were small (5-23%).
- **CVD-PHI-11** [P] Recomputing P83's t-tests from its printed means and SDs with assumed group sizes 278/186: absorption t 10.99 (printed 10.910) and d 1.04 (1.03), altruism t 3.88 (3.889) and d 0.37 (0.37) agree; for religiosity the pooled t is 5.35 and d 0.51 (printed 0.52), but the Welch t is 5.64 with df 454, against P83's printed t(327) = 5.395 after 'degrees of freedom were adjusted from 462 to 327', so the Welch t and df were not supported. Post hoc (agent arithmetic, not a pre-registered run): with the group sizes reversed (186 complete, 278 not) the Welch values are t 5.35 and df 324, close to the printed t(327) = 5.395, so either the sizes, the group labels of the SDs, or the printed values differ from the 60% split; this cannot be resolved without P83's data. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = convergence of mystical experience (reporting audit). *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-2#H3. *Limits:* Group sizes are not printed in P83 and were inferred from the 60% prevalence; other sizes would change the Welch df; the OSF data were not checked.
- **CVD-PHI-13** [P] P84 traces the MEQ to Walter Pahnke's 1963 'Mystical Consciousness Typology', derived from Stace: in Pahnke's Good Friday study 4 out of 10 psilocybin subjects reached a 'complete mystical experience' and no placebo subject did. P84 argues that measures based on Stace import perennialist and Christian assumptions about a 'common core' of religion, and reports that Taves' Inventory of Non-Ordinary Experiences, validated with samples in the United States (English) and India (Hindi), revealed significant differences in how items were interpreted across cultures. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = whether the instrument presupposes the convergence it measures. *Sources:* P84. *Limits:* P84 is an argument from religious studies; Pahnke's and Taves' primary work were not read; the bias claim is P84's interpretation.
- **CVD-PHI-01** [PS] Peirce characterised truth as the opinion that inquiry would converge on: 'the opinion which is fated to be ultimately agreed to by all who investigate' (1878), later the 'ideal limit towards which endless investigation would tend' (1901); P72 reports the long-standing objection that such epistemic accounts cannot handle 'buried secrets', statements true or false that no inquiry could settle, and Misak's point that we are then never in a position to judge whether a belief is true. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = Peircean convergence of inquiry. *Sources:* P72. *Limits:* Interpretive; pragmatic theories differ widely (James, Dewey, Rorty, Price, Misak) and P72 says there is no consensus response to the fundamental objection. Slip: 'monor functions'.
- **CVD-PHI-03** [PS] Peirce's own statement of convergence: investigators of the velocity of light by different methods 'may at first obtain different results, but, as each perfects his method and his processes, the results will move steadily together toward a destined centre'; truth is the opinion 'fated to be ultimately agreed to by all who investigate'; he answers the 'buried secrets' worry by holding that any question with a clear meaning would be settled if investigation went far enough, and contrasts science with methods where 'the idea of loyalty replaced that of truth-seeking'. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = Peirce's convergence of inquiry (primary text). *Sources:* P75. *Limits:* 1878 essay; Peirce's footnote says 'fate' means only what is sure to come true. Transcription slip or period spelling: 'curves of Lissajoux'.
- **CVD-PHI-06** [PS] P81 reports perennialism as the idea that religious traditions converge on one truth: some perennialists emphasise common themes in mystical experience across cultures, others hold that traditions 'share a single metaphysical truth or origin' (Steuco: 'one principle of all things, of which there has always been one and the same knowledge among all peoples'). Perennialists argue that themes which 'developed independent of contact between the cultures concerned' likely point to deeper truths. Critics answer that perennialism is ahistorical, overemphasises similarities and downplays differences, and (Craig Martin) makes unfalsifiable claims resembling the 'no true Scotsman' fallacy. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = convergence of religious traditions on a common core. *Sources:* P81. *Limits:* Wikipedia (tertiary); no primary perennialist or critical text read; the claim records a position, not its truth.
- **CVD-PHI-02** [H] Bayesian inquirers sharing 5000 Bernoulli(0.6) observations converged but not within 0.02: disagreement shrank 0.61, 0.40, 0.20, 0.07 at 10, 100, 1000, 5000 observations (H1 not supported) because a confident wrong prior Beta(200, 800) was still at 0.53; a dogmatic inquirer whose prior excluded the truth settled on its nearest support (0.4); for observationally equivalent hypotheses ('buried secrets') posterior odds stayed at the prior odds, so disagreement never shrank. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = merging of opinions. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H3;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H4. *Limits:* A toy of a philosophical thesis; priors and truth are the agent's choices; convergence of opinion is not Peirce's truth.
- **CVD-PHI-04** [H] In a toy of Peirce's passage, nine investigators whose biases (-3% to +3%) and noise shrink by 0.9 per round converged (spread 0.082 -> 0.000002) on the truth; one never-improving method kept the spread at 0.030 and the mean 0.003 off; and a 1% bias shared by all methods let them converge just as tightly on a value 1% from the truth: agreement alone does not show truth unless shared errors are removed. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = agreement of methods vs truth. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-3#H2. *Limits:* Toy; rates arbitrary; the shared-bias case is by construction.
- **CVD-PHI-05** [H] Peirce toy, x6: convergence held with 5x the noise and with 3x the biases, and with slow improvement (rate 0.98) by round 300 (spread 0.00017); but with improvement rates spread from 0.80 to 0.99 the slowest method dominated, leaving 15% of the initial spread at round 100 (the 1% hypothesis was not supported); two never-improving methods with opposite biases kept the spread at 0.058 and their errors cancelled only approximately (mean 0.0014 off; the within-1e-3 hypothesis was not supported); a shared bias of 0.01 again produced tight agreement 0.01 from the truth. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = agreement of methods vs truth. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-6#H2. *Limits:* Toy; rates arbitrary; the shared-bias case is by construction. Two of ten hypotheses not supported (iter-2 H1, iter-3 H2).
- **CVD-PHI-07** [H] In the agent's toy of P81's inference (traditions on a ring of contacts, binary features), a common core raises similarity evenly (0.625 for fidelity 0.5) with no gradient by contact distance (|corr| <= 0.01), while local diffusion produces a strong gradient (corr -0.51 to -0.84, still -0.76 with 10% long-range contact). But diffusion with contact between any traditions also leaves no gradient (corr 0.009) and even more similarity (0.75), so neither the level of similarity nor its pattern separates a common core from widespread contact: the inference rests on P81's premise that the themes 'developed independent of contact', which needs historical evidence, not similarity data. With only 10 features the core model's similarity varied with sd 0.044, so the pre-registered 'sd > 0.05' was not supported. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = common core vs contact. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-3#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-4#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-5#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Toy; features, fidelity and contact rules are the agent's; neither P81 nor its critics propose this model; it tests the logic of an inference, not any religious claim.
- **CVD-PHI-08** [H] Peirce toy (P75), x9: slower improvement (rho 0.95), ten-fold noise and five-fold biases all still converge below 1% of the initial spread by round 100 or 200 (spreads 2e-6 to 8e-6 relative). *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = agreement of methods. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-9#H1. *Limits:* Replication-extension of CVB-PHI-05 (adds nothing new).
- **CVD-PHI-14** [H] Instrument toy for P84's critique: nine traditions, six experience dimensions, an instrument that measures three. When traditions differ only on the three unmeasured dimensions, the instrument finds a difference in 5% of 300 studies (its false-positive rate) while the unmeasured dimensions differ in 100%; with no differences anywhere the rates are 3% and 6%. A null result on a common-core instrument such as P83's is therefore compatible with traditions that differ on what the instrument does not measure. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = common core vs instrument scope. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-8#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-8#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-8#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-9#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-9#H1. *Limits:* Toy; dimensions, effect size (0.5) and sample sizes are the agent's; it shows a logical possibility, not that traditions differ.
- **CVD-PHI-15** [H] Common-core toy, x12: with 100 features global diffusion again leaves no distance gradient (corr -0.010) and more similarity than a core of fidelity 0.5 (0.75 vs 0.62, core sd 0.013); with half the contacts long-range the diffusion gradient weakens to -0.36 (CVC at 10%: -0.76); local diffusion replicates (-0.84). *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = common core vs contact. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-10#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-10#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-11#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-11#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-12#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-D/iter-12#H1. *Limits:* Replication-extension of CVC-PHI-07 (little new).
