- **CVD-SOC-01** [P] P69 shows that whether a centralized influence network (one agent's estimate weighted by omega) improves the collective estimate over the plain mean depends on the distribution of initial estimates: it helps under overestimation bias or large dispersion and is reversed under low dispersion with underestimation; in a reanalysis of four experiments (2885 participants, 99 groups, 54 tasks, 687 collective estimates) the probability of improvement was explained by its heavy-tail measure (z = 4.16). *Operationalization:* SOC Convergence = estimates converging into a collective estimate. *Sources:* P69. *Limits:* Slips: '678 observations' vs 582 + 105 = 687 collective estimates; a logistic model is written with a Gaussian error term; the distributional parametrisation is in the SI (not read).
