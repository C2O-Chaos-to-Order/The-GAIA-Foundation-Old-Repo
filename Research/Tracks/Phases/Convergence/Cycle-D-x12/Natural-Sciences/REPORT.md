## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **CVD-NAT-01** [E] P68 models collective synchrony with a Sakaguchi-Kuramoto model in which each individual's phase lag is its strategy; in a population of cooperators (lag 0) and cheaters (lag alpha) the two-cluster state with phase gap alpha is stable when the cheater fraction n_ch < 1/(1 - cos alpha), numerical critical fractions follow this curve, and under P68's payoffs persistent populations settle at 50-50 because the minority loses its advantage as it grows. *Operationalization:* NAT Convergence = synchronisation (oscillators converging on a common rhythm). *Sources:* P68. *Limits:* Identical natural frequencies; one coupling strength. Slips: Eq. 9 has a stray bracket and drops K from the second term; 'strays too far form'; the Fig. 2 caption says the system is incoherent 'when alpha_ch >= pi/2', which on the bound applies only above the critical fraction.
- **CVD-NAT-04** [E] Simulating P74's null (9194 loci, two lineages, S0 0.3878, S 0.201) reproduced the analytic chance overlap (S0 + (S - S0) decay)^2 from 0.150 to 0.040 at every time and loss rate, and S^2 = 0.0404 matches P74's '~4%'. P74's counts were partly not supported: 86 species give 3655 pairs, not 3,570 (= C(85, 2)); species-disjoint pairs of pairs number 6,370,665 (86) or 6,074,355 (85), not 4,755,188; but 50 x C(100, 2) = 247,500 and the expected disjoint share gives 236,056 vs P74's 236,070. *Operationalization:* NAT Convergence = chance convergence under a null. *Sources:* SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#H3;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#H4;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#H5;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#H6;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-1#H7. *Limits:* Two of seven hypotheses not supported (both count checks; foreknown from arithmetic, disclosed). P74 may have excluded one species from pairs; the 4,755,188 count may refer to a subset not described.
- **CVD-NAT-05** [E] With 500 oscillators and alpha 2pi/3, 3pi/4, 5pi/6 and 11pi/12, the two-cluster state persisted in exactly the 24 cells below P68's bound 1/(1 - cos alpha) and broke in every cell above it, including cells 0.014-0.017 from the bound (n_ch 0.65 vs 0.667; 0.55 vs 0.536; 0.50 vs 0.509). *Operationalization:* NAT Convergence = synchronisation threshold. *Sources:* SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-2#H1. *Limits:* Identical frequencies; T = 200.
- **CVD-NAT-06** [E] P68's replicator dynamic ended at 0.500 cheaters for alpha pi/3, pi/2, 2pi/3 and 5pi/6 from both 0.2 and 0.8, consistent with P68's 'always 50-50' (payoffs equalise when the cheater share is one half). *Operationalization:* NAT Convergence = evolutionary convergence to a mixed population. *Sources:* SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-CV-NAT-A/iter-3#H1. *Limits:* Uses P68's payoff forms; does not test P68's mutation runs.
- **CVD-NAT-07** [E] P76 states the Kuramoto model of synchronisation: each oscillator has its own natural frequency and is coupled to all others through the sine of the phase difference; for a unimodal symmetric frequency density g there is a critical coupling K_c = 2/(pi g(0)) below which the long-run order parameter r is 0, with r ~ sqrt(16/(pi K_c^3)) sqrt(mu/(-g''(0))) just above it (K = K_c(1 + mu)); for N = 2 the pair locks exactly when K > K_c = 2|omega_1|, N = 3 cannot be chaotic, and chaos first occurs at N = 4. The model assumes weak coupling and identical or nearly identical oscillators. *Operationalization:* NAT Convergence = synchronisation of heterogeneous oscillators. *Sources:* P76. *Limits:* Wikipedia (tertiary; formulas rendered from markup); the primary sources (Kuramoto 1975, 1984; Strogatz 2000) were not read. The Lorentzian closed form r = sqrt(1 - K_c/K) is not stated in P76.
- **CVD-NAT-08** [E] P76's onset results reproduce. Unit Gaussian: K_c = 1.59577 and r was within 0.001 of the self-consistency solution at K = 1.65-2.5. P76's small-mu formula is good only at onset: 0.327 vs 0.316 at mu 0.034 and 0.551 vs 0.503 at mu 0.097, but 0.892 vs 0.715 at K = 2 and 1.33 (an impossible r > 1) at K = 2.5. *Operationalization:* NAT Convergence = synchronisation threshold (heterogeneous). *Sources:* SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-1#H3;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-1#H4;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-2#H3;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-2#H4;SIM-CV-NAT-B/iter-2#H5. *Limits:* All-to-all coupling, RK4 dt 0.01, r averaged over t = 100-200; 1.3% of Lorentzian mass clipped.
