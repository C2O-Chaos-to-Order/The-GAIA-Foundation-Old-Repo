## Claims (ledger: `claims/claims.csv`)
- **CVC-PHI-01** [PS] Peirce characterised truth as the opinion that inquiry would converge on: 'the opinion which is fated to be ultimately agreed to by all who investigate' (1878), later the 'ideal limit towards which endless investigation would tend' (1901); P72 reports the long-standing objection that such epistemic accounts cannot handle 'buried secrets', statements true or false that no inquiry could settle, and Misak's point that we are then never in a position to judge whether a belief is true. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = Peircean convergence of inquiry. *Sources:* P72. *Limits:* Interpretive; pragmatic theories differ widely (James, Dewey, Rorty, Price, Misak) and P72 says there is no consensus response to the fundamental objection. Slip: 'monor functions'.
- **CVC-PHI-03** [PS] Peirce's own statement of convergence: investigators of the velocity of light by different methods 'may at first obtain different results, but, as each perfects his method and his processes, the results will move steadily together toward a destined centre'; truth is the opinion 'fated to be ultimately agreed to by all who investigate'; he answers the 'buried secrets' worry by holding that any question with a clear meaning would be settled if investigation went far enough, and contrasts science with methods where 'the idea of loyalty replaced that of truth-seeking'. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = Peirce's convergence of inquiry (primary text). *Sources:* P75. *Limits:* 1878 essay; Peirce's footnote says 'fate' means only what is sure to come true. Transcription slip or period spelling: 'curves of Lissajoux'.
- **CVC-PHI-06** [PS] P81 reports perennialism as the idea that religious traditions converge on one truth: some perennialists emphasise common themes in mystical experience across cultures, others hold that traditions 'share a single metaphysical truth or origin' (Steuco: 'one principle of all things, of which there has always been one and the same knowledge among all peoples'). Perennialists argue that themes which 'developed independent of contact between the cultures concerned' likely point to deeper truths. Critics answer that perennialism is ahistorical, overemphasises similarities and downplays differences, and (Craig Martin) makes unfalsifiable claims resembling the 'no true Scotsman' fallacy. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = convergence of religious traditions on a common core. *Sources:* P81. *Limits:* Wikipedia (tertiary); no primary perennialist or critical text read; the claim records a position, not its truth.
- **CVC-PHI-02** [H] Bayesian inquirers sharing 5000 Bernoulli(0.6) observations converged but not within 0.02: disagreement shrank 0.61, 0.40, 0.20, 0.07 at 10, 100, 1000, 5000 observations (H1 not supported) because a confident wrong prior Beta(200, 800) was still at 0.53; a dogmatic inquirer whose prior excluded the truth settled on its nearest support (0.4); for observationally equivalent hypotheses ('buried secrets') posterior odds stayed at the prior odds, so disagreement never shrank. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = merging of opinions. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H3;SIM-CV-PHI-0/iter-1#H4. *Limits:* A toy of a philosophical thesis; priors and truth are the agent's choices; convergence of opinion is not Peirce's truth.
- **CVC-PHI-04** [H] In a toy of Peirce's passage, nine investigators whose biases (-3% to +3%) and noise shrink by 0.9 per round converged (spread 0.082 -> 0.000002) on the truth; one never-improving method kept the spread at 0.030 and the mean 0.003 off; and a 1% bias shared by all methods let them converge just as tightly on a value 1% from the truth: agreement alone does not show truth unless shared errors are removed. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = agreement of methods vs truth. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-3#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-3#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-A/iter-3#H2. *Limits:* Toy; rates arbitrary; the shared-bias case is by construction.
- **CVC-PHI-05** [H] Peirce toy, x6: convergence held with 5x the noise and with 3x the biases, and with slow improvement (rate 0.98) by round 300 (spread 0.00017); but with improvement rates spread from 0.80 to 0.99 the slowest method dominated, leaving 15% of the initial spread at round 100 (the 1% hypothesis was not supported); two never-improving methods with opposite biases kept the spread at 0.058 and their errors cancelled only approximately (mean 0.0014 off; the within-1e-3 hypothesis was not supported); a shared bias of 0.01 again produced tight agreement 0.01 from the truth. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = agreement of methods vs truth. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-3#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-3#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-3#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-4#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-4#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-5#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-5#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-6#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-6#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-B/iter-6#H2. *Limits:* Toy; rates arbitrary; the shared-bias case is by construction. Two of ten hypotheses not supported (iter-2 H1, iter-3 H2).
- **CVC-PHI-07** [H] In the agent's toy of P81's inference (traditions on a ring of contacts, binary features), a common core raises similarity evenly (0.625 for fidelity 0.5) with no gradient by contact distance (|corr| <= 0.01), while local diffusion produces a strong gradient (corr -0.51 to -0.84, still -0.76 with 10% long-range contact). But diffusion with contact between any traditions also leaves no gradient (corr 0.009) and even more similarity (0.75), so neither the level of similarity nor its pattern separates a common core from widespread contact: the inference rests on P81's premise that the themes 'developed independent of contact', which needs historical evidence, not similarity data. With only 10 features the core model's similarity varied with sd 0.044, so the pre-registered 'sd > 0.05' was not supported. *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = common core vs contact. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-1#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-1#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-1#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-2#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-2#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-2#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-3#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-3#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-4#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-4#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-4#H2;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-5#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-5#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-6#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-6#H1. *Limits:* Toy; features, fidelity and contact rules are the agent's; neither P81 nor its critics propose this model; it tests the logic of an inference, not any religious claim.
- **CVC-PHI-08** [H] Peirce toy (P75), x9: slower improvement (rho 0.95), ten-fold noise and five-fold biases all still converge below 1% of the initial spread by round 100 or 200 (spreads 2e-6 to 8e-6 relative). *Operationalization:* PHI Convergence = agreement of methods. *Sources:* SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-7#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-7#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-8#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-8#H1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-9#V1;SIM-CV-PHI-C/iter-9#H1. *Limits:* Replication-extension of CVB-PHI-05 (adds nothing new).
