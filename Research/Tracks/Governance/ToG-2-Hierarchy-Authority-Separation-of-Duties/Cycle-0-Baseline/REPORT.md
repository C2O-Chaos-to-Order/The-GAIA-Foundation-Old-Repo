## Roles and separation (§7)
Roles listed: owner, author/producer, independent reviewer, ethics/rights reviewer, security/privacy reviewer, data steward, implementer/operator, auditor, affected-party representative, AI agent/tool.

Minimum separation:
1. Author cannot be sole independent reviewer of consequential work.
2. System under test cannot be sole judge of pass.
3. Agents may record but cannot declare gate passed when independent/ethics/security/owner approval required.
4. Disclose and recuse on conflicts.
5. Emergency containment within playbook; restoration requires approval path.
6. Non-retaliation route for concerns.

Decision record must identify: ID, scope, proposal, authority, contributors/reviewers, evidence, alternatives, affected parties, risk level, controls, conflicts, result, rationale, dissent, timestamp, expiry/review, rollback/remedy. [Established]

Primary domain: GAIA-TOT-002 (Governance and Decision Rights). Secondaries: TOT-013, TOT-035. [Plausible]
