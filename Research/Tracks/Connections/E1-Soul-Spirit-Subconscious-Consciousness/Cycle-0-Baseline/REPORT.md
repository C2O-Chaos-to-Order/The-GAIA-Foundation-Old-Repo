The Work Queue §L wording "none among the four topics" is therefore not exact (C0-9 FAIL, CN0-01 [E]). Item b21 (re-check the track's links) is partly answered by this.

The other five pairs share no evidence source (C0-11 FAIL). Each topic draws on 17–21 distinct sources.

## 2. Shared factors (judged; `factors_c0.csv`)
Six of eleven factors appear in all four topics, five in three:
- **Method, all four:** what is measured is a report or a belief (CN0-03 [E]); cut-offs misclassify (CN0-04 [E], from simulations with illustrative inputs); headline effects are fragile (CN0-05 [P]); association is not cause (F04).
- **Ethics, all four:** responsibility, care and justice (CN0-18 [P]).
- **Tradition, all four:** the self and what persists (F07). Traditions describe this, and it is not evidence.
- **Subject matter, three topics:** death and the end of life (Soul, Spirit, Consciousness; CN0-06 [P]); sleep and dreaming (Soul, Subconscious, Consciousness; CN0-07 [E]); contemplative and nondual awareness, and degrees rather than a binary (Spirit, Subconscious, Consciousness); beliefs shaping moral judgment (Soul, Subconscious, Consciousness).

The factor overlap is near-saturated (every pair shares 7–10 of 11 factors), so at this resolution the factors say more about the common predicament of the four literatures than about any one pair.

## 3. Differences
- **Different empirical objects** (CN0-08 [P]):
  - Soul: beliefs about a soul.
  - Spirit: reported self-transcendent experience.
  - Subconscious: processing inferred without report, in several senses.
  - Consciousness: experience and its correlates, with theories under test.
- **A dependent pair** (CN0-09 [P]): only the Subconscious is defined by contrast with another of the four. Subconscious and Consciousness are a dependent pair (CL-01 [P]). Their shared threshold methods are CL-03 [E].
- **Conceptually connected, evidentially separate.** The near-disjoint source bases (§1) contrast with the many shared factors (§2). The topics meet in method and in subject matter, but no study in the ledgers measures two of them together. The one exception is TD-CONS-29: implicit learning, awareness and a recalled experience of death, all in 36 patients with 9 usable EEGs.

## 4. Unity
- **In the science** (CN0-12 [P]): on present records, unity is methodological. No ledger claim establishes a common substrate, mechanism or causal link.
- **In traditions** (described, not proven):
  - Advaita joins deep sleep, turiya as pure consciousness, and the true self in one teaching (CN0-11 [PS]).
  - Yogacara, Advaita and Leibniz describe the subliminal as a form or degree of consciousness (CN0-10 [E] as description; CL-14).
  - Traditions describe both a reality beyond ordinary experience and pure awareness. Whether those are one is open (CL-05 [PS]).
- **Framing** (CN0-17 [PS]): GAIA as motherthread holds the four together as an organising principle. It is the owner's framework, not a finding.
- **Against README §9.5**, a unity that "should preserve meaningful distinctions": the methodological unity does, and it keeps the four objects distinct. The tradition unities are recorded as teachings.
