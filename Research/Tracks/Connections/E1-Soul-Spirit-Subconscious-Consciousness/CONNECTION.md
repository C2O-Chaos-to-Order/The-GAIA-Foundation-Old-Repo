# The Connection: Soul, Spirit, Subconscious and Consciousness

**Work Queue §E.** Shared factors, differences and unity among the four topics of the Soul-Spirit-Subconscious-Consciousness (SSSC) track.

**Status:** Proposed. Consolidated in x12 from the Baseline, x3, x6 and x9 cycles. Gate INDETERMINATE (internal checks only; no independent review; Indeterminate is never Pass). Nothing here edits the SSSC track, the Phases track, the main reports, `Research/README.md` or `WORK_QUEUE.md`. Proposed edits appear in §11 as options only.

**Labels:** E = Established, P = Plausible, PS = Philosophical/Spiritual, H = Hypothetical.
- No claim or link is labelled above its weakest ranked basis.

## 1. The answer in brief
1. **The four topics are connected, but not as one thing.** Every pair now has Connection links (28 in all). Before this report the track had one topic-to-topic link, TL-08, Subconscious–Consciousness, a Hypothetical sleep-and-dreams mechanism (CN0-01). The Work Queue's "none among the four topics" was therefore close but not exact.
2. **What robustly unites them is method, and with it the limits of method.**
   - Three factors are present in all four topics under every rule that keeps method factors (§3): F01 what is measured is a report or a belief; F02 cut-offs misclassify; F04 association is not cause.
   - Shared methods are also shared error sources (CN3-03 [H]), so this unity is weak evidence of any unity in the objects (CN0-12 [P]).
3. **No substantive unity survives an evidence-only rule.** The one tradition factor found in all four topics, F07 (the self and what persists), counts only when Philosophical/Spiritual bases are allowed. The science on present records establishes no common substrate, mechanism or cause (CN0-12).
4. **The pairs differ sharply in strength.**
   - Subconscious–Consciousness is a genuinely dependent pair. The Subconscious is defined by contrast with consciousness (CN0-09 [P], CN6-08 [P]), and the two share evidence sources.
   - Spirit–Subconscious is the weakest: no shared source, no cross-mention, no main-report link. Only a shared method weakness (CL-12 [E]) and a tradition vocabulary of realisation (CL-13 [PS]) join them.
5. **The traditions join what the science keeps apart.**
   - Soul and spirit share a breath and life vocabulary across traditions (CN6-01 [E, descriptive]).
   - Advaita joins deep sleep, pure consciousness and the true self in one teaching (CN0-11 [PS]).
   - Jain and Advaita teaching identify the self with consciousness, while Indian Buddhist philosophy denies an abiding self (CN6-03 [E, descriptive]).
   - The traditions disagree with each other, so "the traditions" offer no single unity either.

## 2. How the topics were studied (what each can and cannot reach)
Each topic has a different empirical object (CN0-08 [P]):

| Topic | What the evidence is about | What it is not about |
|---|---|---|
| Soul | beliefs about a soul and what continues after death; end-of-life and near-death reports | a soul |
| Spirit | reported self-transcendent and mystical experience | the reality such experience reports |
| Subconscious | processing inferred from behaviour without report, in several senses | an inner place called the subconscious |
| Consciousness | experience, its levels and neural correlates; theories under adversarial test | a settled account of what consciousness is (CN9-01 [PS]) |

The same constraint runs through all four. The evidence is reports or beliefs (CN0-03 [E]). Cut-offs on noisy measures can misclassify (CN0-04 [E], simulation results). Headline effects are fragile (CN0-05 [P]). Constructs bleed into measures (CN6-06 [P]). Common-core instruments can make traditions look the same (CN3-04 [H]).

## 4. Differences
- **Defined against each other.** Only Subconscious and Consciousness form a pair defined by contrast (CN0-09 [P]).
  - Higher-order theories make the difference relational (CN6-08 [P]).
  - Subliminal and implicit findings bear on access, not on phenomenal experience. Whether phenomenal states without access exist is disputed (CN6-10 [P], CL-28 [P]).
- **Soul and spirit.** Traditions draw the boundary differently, and there is no single cross-tradition boundary (CN6-02 [PS], CL-22 [PS]).
- **Deep sleep, framed two ways.** Perturbational complexity falls from 0.44–0.67 awake to 0.18–0.28 in NREM sleep. Advaita describes deep sleep as partial experience of the bliss of turiya. Both are Established as descriptions, and the contrast decides nothing (CN6-07 [E]).
- **The subject of experience.** Soul, brain process or neither? Near-death reports are read either way (CN6-04 [PS], CL-23 [PS] conflict). No decisive test is known.

## 5. Unity, judged by the README §9.5 criterion
Unity "should preserve meaningful distinctions, explain relationships, and improve understanding without turning analogy into unsupported fact".
- **Unity in the science (CN0-12 [P]):** methodological only. It preserves the distinctions, and it explains why the four literatures fail in the same ways. It does not show that the four are aspects of one object.
- **Unity in traditions (CN0-11 [PS], CN6-03 [E, descriptive], CN6-09 [PS]):** described teachings, plural and in part mutually contradictory. They explain how some traditions relate the topics, not how the topics are related.
- **Unity in the owner's framework (CN0-17 [PS], framing):** GAIA as the motherthread holds the four together as aspects of one organising principle. That is a framing, never evidence. The separability test holds: no Established claim or link rests on a PS or H basis or on the framing source (x9 C9-16).
- **What would change this?** A shared mechanism tested across two topics, for example:
  - whether sleep-dependent implicit reactivation (Subconscious) explains end-of-life experiences during sleep (Soul) (CL-11 [H]);
  - whether altered and mystical states share neural signatures once non-stationary input is ruled out (CN6-05 [H], CL-24 [H]).

## 7. Traditions and science, cited fairly
- **Traditions named:**
  - Greek and Stoic, Hebrew Bible, Christian (Aquinas; John 3:8), Islamic (ruh), Chinese (qi) (CN6-01, CN6-09);
  - Advaita Vedanta, Jain, Buddhist (Yogacara; anatman) (CN0-10, CN0-11, CN6-03, CN6-07);

- **How tradition claims are labelled:** every one is PS or a marked Established description (x6 C6-11, x9 C9-11). Every one cites a source other than the owner's framework (C9-14).
- **Balance:** every topic has both tradition and science claims, and science claims outnumber tradition claims in each.
- **Limits:**
  - The African and Indigenous material rests on one author's synthesis and one outsider encyclopaedia entry, and these traditions are plural and self-described differently.
  - The Islamic and Hebrew material is lexical only.
  - Common-core instruments derived from one tradition's template can make traditions look alike (CN3-04 [H]).
- **Contested science stays contested:**
  - adversarial tests challenged both leading consciousness theories (CN0-05);
  - the entropic-brain idea is Hypothetical (CN6-05);
  - the circulatory-arrest evidence is one feasibility study (CL-15 [H]).
