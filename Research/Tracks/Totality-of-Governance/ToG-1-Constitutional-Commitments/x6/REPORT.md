## 6. Negative findings and competing interpretations

1. Applicability can become a waiver channel. The toy blocks unsupportedNA, but a fraudulent “authorized” rationale may still pass if trusted as an input. Actual non-waivable status and applicability authority need owner and qualified review; the toy does not decide them.
2. Provenance does not equal truth. Even a structurally consistent, hashed history can contain false attestations or an invalid derivation claim. The local dependency engine neither verifies semantic entailment nor implements the full PROV specification. [P01 §2.1.2; P02 §1.2]
3. Declared conflicts are incomplete evidence. Different names can share interests; different organizations can still collude. Conversely, not all institutional relationships entail disqualifying conflict. Contextual qualified judgment is needed rather than blindly interpreting any graph path as conflict. [N03 MEASURE1.3; A01 abstract]
4. Scope attenuation is necessary but insufficient. Correct subset and expiry calculations do not authenticate the issuer, establish legal authority or prove a purpose lawful. Empty grants are not positive permission to act.
5. Current-use revalidation is idealized. Network delay, clock disagreement, atomicity, rollback behavior and unavailable policy services could invalidate real guarantees. The test records logic over ordered events, not operational revocation latency.
6. Protection and remedy are not interchangeable. Containment can stop a modeled action without repairing harm. Specialized gross-violations provisions do not authorize a project to punish people or act as a court. Rights of affected people and those accused remain relevant. [H01 §§10–12,18–23,27]
7. Evidence collection itself can create privacy risk. Preserve only authorized, minimized and protected audit content; immutable retention and public disclosure are not automatic goods. A digest does not itself anonymize sensitive content. This is a proposal, not a privacy-law determination. [G01 §§4.7,4.10; H01 §§10,12,22]
8. More cycles do not solve missing independent review. Unavailable prior archives, partial full-text access and uninspected global maps remain genuine blockers; neither larger enumeration nor same-author reproduction closes them.
