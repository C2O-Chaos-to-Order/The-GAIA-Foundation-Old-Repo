The following are proposed baseline commitments. The owner must review and reconcile them with the existing seven Universal Regulation laws and any binding legal obligations before adoption.

## 4.1 Dignity and life

Protect human dignity, life, bodily integrity, autonomy, and freedom from cruelty. Consider the welfare of animals and ecological systems where relevant. A projected collective benefit never automatically erases the rights or dignity of an individual or group.

## 4.2 Integrity before intelligence

Honesty, traceable evidence, and accurate representation take precedence over capability, speed, influence, or persuasive presentation.

## 4.3 Evidence before assertion

Every material claim must have a stable identity, an appropriate claim class, traceable supporting evidence, stated uncertainty, and known limitations. Belief, metaphor, analogy, simulation, model output, and established mechanism are not interchangeable.

## 4.4 Verification before advancement

Criteria must be defined before testing. Internal consistency, independent challenge, reproducibility, ethical review, security review, and authorization are distinct activities; one cannot silently stand in for another.

## 4.5 Safety before capability

Safeguards must scale with potential harm, reach, autonomy, irreversibility, and the consequences of failure. Higher capability does not lower oversight.

## 4.6 Accountability before authority

Every consequential decision must name the human decision authority, the responsible operator or contributor, the permission boundary, the evidence used, and the means of review or challenge.

## 4.7 Consent before access

Access to personal, sensitive, confidential, community-governed, or third-party material requires appropriate authority, consent where required, and protection. Public availability alone does not confer unlimited reuse rights.

## 4.8 Least harmful effective action

Prefer the least intrusive, least harmful, proportionate and reversible response that can reasonably address the legitimate objective. Escalate protection when credible, serious risks require it.

## 4.9 Truthful uncertainty

Unknown, disputed, missing, inaccessible, failed, or contradictory evidence must remain visible. Do not turn uncertainty into certainty by repeating the same process or by increasing cycle counts.

## 4.10 Stewardship and continuity

Preserve provenance, prior versions, audit evidence, institutional memory, ecological concerns, cultural boundaries, and the ability to correct or roll back decisions.

## 4.11 Non-exemption

No claim of intelligence, consciousness, spiritual significance, mathematical elegance, exceptional power, urgency, or greater-good intent exempts a system or actor from evidence, ethics, safety, accountability, law, or authorization.
