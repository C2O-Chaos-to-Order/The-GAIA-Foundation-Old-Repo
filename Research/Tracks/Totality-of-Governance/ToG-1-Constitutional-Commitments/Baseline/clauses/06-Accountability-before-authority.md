## Evidence and finding

Established textual description [TOG1-C0-06-T]: “Every consequential decision must name the human decision authority, the responsible operator or contributor, the permission boundary, the evidence used, and the means of review or challenge.” [S01 §4.6]

Plausible interpretation [TOG1-C0-06-A]: UNESCO retains ultimate human responsibility and the repository excludes AI agents as gate authorities. Naming an owner is necessary documentation but does not by itself establish qualifications, independence, effective redress or lawful authority over third parties. [S03;S07;S09; Gate Authority §§1–3; UNESCO paras35–38; NIST pp8–12]

## Proposed control and ethical implications

Philosophical/Spiritual (philosophical) proposal [TOG1-C0-06-N]: Use decision IDs, named owner and operator, action-specific scope and expiry, evidence, dissent and appeal path. Verify reviewer independence beyond string inequality. AI outputs are recommendations and test records, not authorizations.

## Competing considerations and counterevidence

The repository owner’s final gate authority does not override applicable law or mandatory independent review. Delegation must be explicit and bounded. A nominal appeal to the same unreviewable decision maker can be ineffective.
