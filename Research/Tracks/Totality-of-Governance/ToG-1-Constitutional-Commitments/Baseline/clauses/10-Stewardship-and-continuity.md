## Proposed control and ethical implications

## Competing considerations and counterevidence

Immutable records and lawful deletion can conflict. Publish verifiable references without unnecessary sensitive content; do not promise universal rollback for irreversible effects. Cultural stewardship requires actual community authority rather than only outsider classification.
