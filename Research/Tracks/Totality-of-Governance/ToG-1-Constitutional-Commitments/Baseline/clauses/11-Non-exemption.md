## Evidence and finding

Established textual description [TOG1-C0-11-T]: “No claim of intelligence, consciousness, spiritual significance, mathematical elegance, exceptional power, urgency, or greater-good intent exempts a system or actor from evidence, ethics, safety, accountability, law, or authorization.” [S01 §4.11]

Plausible interpretation [TOG1-C0-11-A]: The repository’s absolute boundary directly anchors this clause. UNESCO and UDHR provide compatible human-rights constraints, but do not independently establish every item in GAIA’s particular exemption list. ## Proposed control and ethical implications

Philosophical/Spiritual (philosophical) proposal [TOG1-C0-11-N]: Treat capability, urgency, metaphysical framing and greater-good scores as non-authorizing metadata. Require the same evidence, consent/authority, review and authorization controls. Emergency exceptions must be previously authorized and narrow, not self-created exemptions.

## Competing considerations and counterevidence

Non-exemption does not preclude lawful, explicitly governed emergency procedures. It precludes a claim of special status replacing authority. The report makes no finding about machine sentience, supernatural truth or a universal moral law.
