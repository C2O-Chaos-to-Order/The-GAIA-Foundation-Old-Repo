## Evidence and finding

Established textual description [TOG1-C0-01-T]: “Protect human dignity, life, bodily integrity, autonomy, and freedom from cruelty. Consider the welfare of animals and ecological systems where relevant. A projected collective benefit never automatically erases the rights or dignity of an individual or group.” [S01 §4.1]

Plausible interpretation [TOG1-C0-01-A]: The external texts give strong textual precedent for a rights-preserving commitment. They do not establish that this project has implemented it. Human rights, animal welfare and ecosystem protection are related but distinct objects; one dignity score would obscure those differences. [S07;S08; UNESCO paras 13–18,25–26,28–29; UDHR arts 1–5,7,30]

## Proposed control and ethical implications

Block advancement where severe unjustified harm is established.
