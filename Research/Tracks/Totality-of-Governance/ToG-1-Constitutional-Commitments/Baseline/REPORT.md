## 1. Purpose, scope and definitions

Question: What does each constitutional commitment require, how does it relate to existing project rules and external normative guidance, and what evidence would be needed before claiming it operates effectively?

This is ToG 1 Baseline only. It neither performs x3/x6/x9/x12 nor checks off the queue’s entire ToG 1 series. The eleven annexes are clause analyses, not eleven new foundational reports. Original seven-report identities remain unchanged. The broader standing requirements from the prior documentation review remain applicable; the original Research README and plan were available in the preceding conversation, but their bytes were not re-fetched or rehashed in this baseline.

Definitions:
- Constitutional commitment: a proposed high-level constraint for this project, not state sovereignty or jurisdiction over third parties.
- Control: an operational requirement proposed to express a commitment; its existence in prose does not show it is implemented.
- Evidence state: a judgment about a specified criterion. PASS/FAIL/INDETERMINATE is not a moral worth score.
- Claim class: empirical, mathematical/computational, historical/textual, interpretive/philosophical, spiritual/theological or hypothesis/speculation. This baseline’s textual observations are historical/textual; no human-subject empirical findings were produced.
- Label: Established, Plausible, Philosophical/Spiritual or Hypothetical. Labels are distinct from class, workflow stage, gate and authorization. The philosophical sense of Philosophical/Spiritual is used for normative proposals; no spiritual mechanism is asserted. [S06 appendix]

Exclusions: jurisdiction-specific legal advice, universal proof of ethics, deployed enforcement, human-subject studies, sensitive/community-restricted data, independent peer review, changing standards, adopting the Totality Suite, complete reconstruction of the knowledge graph, or extending the canonical matrix to 750 cells.

## 3. Findings and competing interpretations

### 3.1 Constitutional alignment is not implementation

The charter’s commitments have recognizable textual counterparts: UNESCO addresses dignity, proportionality, privacy and human oversight; the UDHR articulates rights and a prohibition on interpreting its provisions to destroy those rights; NIST describes contextual risk management and organizational accountability. These are Established descriptions of what those texts contain, not findings that GAIA complies with them. [TOG1-C0-E01–E08; S07 paras11–38; S08 arts1–5,8,12,27,30; S09 pp2–14]

A defensible conclusion is provisional compatibility with explicit limits. External texts do not empirically prove the charter’s effectiveness, establish its adoption, or supply the project lawful authority it does not otherwise possess. The eleven commitments need operational criteria, competent decision makers and challenge/redress mechanisms. Mittelstadt’s abstract identifies institutional weaknesses in translating AI principles into practice; this supports caution, not a quantitative prediction of GAIA failure. [TOG1-C0-L01; S10 abstract]

### 3.2 Rights constraints and contextual deliberation both matter

A purely aggregate benefit score could conceal severe harm to a minority. Conversely, treating every practical tension as settled by a slogan avoids the contextual assessment UNESCO calls for. The proposed position is constrained deliberation: identify protected rights, applicable authority and law, affected parties, evidence, alternatives, proportionality, dissent and remedy. This report does not determine which legal restrictions are lawful in a particular jurisdiction. [TOG1-C0-01-A/N; TOG1-C0-08-A/N; S07 para11,25–26,38; S08 arts29–30]

### 3.3 The principal internal precedence findings

1. Charter §4.2–§4.7 closely track Universal Regulation laws1–6; §4.11 corresponds to the absolute boundary. §4.1,§4.8–§4.10 add explicit dignity, proportionality, uncertainty and continuity emphasis. This is an interpretive crosswalk, not formal supersession. [maps/CLAUSE_CROSSWALK.csv]
2. Authorization before implementation is Universal Regulation law7 and remains separately required even though §4 has no identically titled twelfth commitment. It must not disappear through a one-to-one title mapping. [TOG1-C0-E10; S01 §§2,7–8,12; S02 law7; S03]
3. Charter §9’s R0–R4 descriptions and the existing RL-1–RL-5 ladder differ. The charter explicitly does not replace the existing scale. No score conversion is assumed. [TOG1-C0-E09; S04 §1]
4. Both the Universal Regulation file and gate-authority file retain Proposed labels. A merge cannot by itself establish implementation or gate PASS; no adoption history was reconstructed here. [S02 header; S03 §§1–3]
5. A blanket positive constitutional verdict is not supportable without review and operational evidence. The baseline is research output, not an effective constitutional enforcement system. [TOG1-C0-04-A; TOG1-C0-06-A]

Proposed operationalization (Philosophical/Spiritual, philosophical): Record purpose, data category, lawful/project authority, consent or other basis where applicable, licensing, community authority, minimization, retention, revocation and security. For this baseline use public normative texts, short extracts and synthetic fixtures only. [TOG1-C0-07-N]

Competing considerations: This is not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Individual consent may not resolve collective cultural authority. A withdrawn consent requires review of future processing and obligations, not an unsupported promise that all lawful records must always be deleted.

Revision/failure trigger: Protected material is accessed without the applicable basis, or a public URL is treated as unrestricted reproduction permission.

Current evidence boundary: No Indigenous community text, sensitive data, human participants or third-party licensing audit was used; specialist cultural/legal review remains outside this baseline. Implementation is NOT ASSESSED. Independent-review gate remains INDETERMINATE.

### §4.8 — Least harmful effective action

Charter text: “Prefer the least intrusive, least harmful, proportionate and reversible response that can reasonably address the legitimate objective. Escalate protection when credible, serious risks require it.” [TOG1-C0-08-T; S01 §4.8]

Comparative finding (Plausible): UNESCO supports necessity and proportionality, and acknowledges tensions among privacy, transparency and security. The phrase “effective action” matters: a harmless but ineffective option does not necessarily address a serious threat. [TOG1-C0-08-A; S07;S09: UNESCO paras11,25–27,38; NIST §§1.2.2–1.2.3]

Proposed operationalization (Philosophical/Spiritual, philosophical): Document legitimate objective, no-action alternative, candidate measures, effectiveness evidence, affected parties, invasiveness, reversibility, residual risk, authority and review/expiry. Competing considerations: Reversibility is not always available; preserving it may conflict with urgent protection. Proportionality requires contextual human judgment. Containment within an existing permission boundary does not authorize retaliation or invasive investigation.

Revision/failure trigger: A materially less intrusive option with adequate evidence of effectiveness is rejected without a documented reason, or emergency restrictions have no authorized scope and review date.

Current evidence boundary: No real intervention alternatives were evaluated and no optimal policy is inferred from the toy. Implementation is NOT ASSESSED. Independent-review gate remains INDETERMINATE.

## 6. Computational specification and execution

Question: Can a proposed strict conjunction of eleven evidence-control states avoid advancement when any applicable control is missing or failed?

Formal rule for fixed length11: FAIL if any control is FAIL; otherwise INDETERMINATE if any is INDETERMINATE; otherwise PASS. Missing-length inputs return INDETERMINATE; invalid tokens of valid-length vectors raise an error. Applicability is assumed for all eleven, not determined by the algorithm. A mixed FAIL/unknown vector remains FAIL because a known failure must not be concealed by uncertainty. This precedence is a proposal made explicit for testing, not a silent rewrite of every existing gate.

SPEC.md was written and hashed before the successful model execution, within this response. It was not independently preregistered or pushed to GitHub before execution. The script and oracle were written by the same author. No outcome was surprising; enumeration is an exhaustive consistency test of an assumed finite rule, not predictive science.
