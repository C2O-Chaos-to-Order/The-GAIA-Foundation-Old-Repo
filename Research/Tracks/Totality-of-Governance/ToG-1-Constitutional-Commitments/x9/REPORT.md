## 6. Cross-model limits and alternatives

1. Presence is not authenticity. A nonempty authority reference can name an invented record; verification flags themselves need inspectable grounds. The record checker does not validate signatures or source entailment.
2. Non-applicability is not exemption. Toy slot choices are not constitutional adoption. A real applicability decision can require legal, ethical, cultural or domain input; no software score grants that authority.
3. Unknown and false differ. An outdated source or unavailable approval is not necessarily evidence that its substantive claim is false. The policy may block action while preserving the unresolved epistemic state.
4. Hidden facts remain hidden. The H4 witness demonstrates why an observable attestation cannot establish a latent property merely by being positive. Real independence depends on appropriate investigation, context and judgment.
5. Freshness cannot be conjured from a lease. The H6 witness preserves revocation visibility and clock assumptions. A current-answer oracle and a local atomic primitive do not together constitute an implemented distributed solution.
6. Denial has costs. Blocking offline unknowns can prevent valid actions. Proportionality must assess those burdens and viable alternatives, without permitting an aggregate benefit score to erase rights.
7. Procedure is not outcome. Formal compliance, changed behavior and avoided/remedied harm are different constructs. The historical scholarship supports testing that distinction, not declaring GAIA effective or ineffective from secondary studies. [A01]
8. Transparency and privacy can conflict. Appropriate feedback and remedy need protected access and participation, not unlimited public disclosure of sensitive complainant or audit material. Record retention, erasure and lawful processing bases require qualified context-specific decisions.
9. More cycles do not replace missing people or evidence. Same-author reproduction remains internal. Prior archive recovery, independent content/ethics review, owner decisions and all-work mapping are still missing.
