# Research pass 1 — Rights-holder impacts, proportionality and non-offsetting

Date2026-10-25. Single author Perplexity; one bounded analytical pass, not independent review.

## Question
What would make §4.1,4.5,4.8,4.11 operational without using collective benefit to erase rights?

## Source portions actually examined
E01 GP11–18;E02 paras11,25–26;R01 §§4–5

## Added/deepened
UNGP due diligence explicitly looks beyond risk to the enterprise and treats changing human-rights risks as an ongoing issue. Severity includes scale, scope and irremediability. A charitable or broadly beneficial activity does not offset a failure to respect rights.

## Challenge and alternatives
A private independent research project is not a State. UNGP references help design project controls but do not establish its legal form or a new legal duty by assertion. Proportionality requires contextual assessment, not a single synthetic benefit score.

## Output
Rights-impact record fields and alternatives template; bounded model requires individual controls to remain visible; links to dignity,safety,least-harm and non-exemption.

## Limits
No affected-party consultation or actual impact assessment. GP19–31 were not retrieved in full and are not used.
