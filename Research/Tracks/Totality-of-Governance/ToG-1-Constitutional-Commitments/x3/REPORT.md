## 1. Purpose, scope and definitions

Question: How can the eleven proposed commitments become inspectable control proposals while preserving rights, uncertain evidence, qualified human authority and change-sensitive approval?

The cycle responds to the requested x3 only. It does not run x6, x9 orx12, adopt a charter, merge a PR, approve a deployment, or replace current governance. Eleven clause annexes share this cycle’s three research passes and three iterations; they are not eleven separately executed research series and are not additional foundational reports.

A commitment is a proposed project-level norm. A control is a possible operational expression of that norm. An evidence state is an input judgment about a specified criterion, not a measure of a person’s worth or proof that a right is respected. A model decision describes a fictional scenario. A gate is a scope-specific authorized human decision, separate from model output. Reproduction means re-execution with comparable outputs; independence means appropriate separation from the producer and conflicts, not merely another execution.

Labels remain separate from primary claim class and workflow status. Established textual claims describe inspected wording; Established computational claims describe executed and same-author reproduced outcomes under a specified model. Neither becomes evidence of general ethical truth. Philosophical/Spiritual denotes philosophical normative proposals here, not a spiritual mechanism. Plausible marks single-author interpretation; Hypothetical marks an untested causal proposition. [claims/CLAIMS_X3.csv]

## 6. Competing interpretations and negative findings

1. Principles alone are insufficient,but fields alone are also insufficient. A malicious or mistaken actor can supply apparently valid scope,competence,independence and permission flags. The toy accepts those judgments as inputs;it does not investigate whether they are true. [TOG1-X3-P02,SIM1–SIM3]
2. The NIST Core explicitly is not a mandatory sequential checklist. The three iterations are this research cycle’s organization,not a claim that NIST prescribes exactly three gates or a universal causal phase sequence. [TOG1-X3-E04;E03 introduction]
3. A known failure and an unknown are different. The proposal records known forgery/withdrawal/incident asFAIL in the defined circumstances,but missing/stale evidence asINDETERMINATE. Neither unlocks advancement;this precedence is a model design,not a silent rewrite of all repository gate records. [R01 §8;R02 §2;SPEC.md files]
4. Harming one rights-holder is not remedied by an unrelated benefit to others. Yet lawful,necessary and proportionate restrictions require contextual analysis rather than a slogan. This baseline extension neither adjudicates legal restrictions nor gives the owner public authority. [E01 GP11;E02 para11;E04 p15]
5. An ongoing serious risk may warrant previously authorized bounded containment even while broader advancement is blocked. The simulation does not grant emergency powers or model every incident-response path. No real containment was executed. [R01 §7.2.5,§11;E03 MANAGE2.3–2.4]
6. Preserving hashes/history does not establish lawful retention or anonymization. Protected audit metadata and sensitive payloads require separate access,purpose and retention decisions. This is a proposed minimization approach,not jurisdiction-specific privacy advice. [E02 paras32–34;templates/CHANGE_AND_REAUTHORIZATION_RECORD.json]
7. No outcome is independent validation or evidence of moral completeness. Same-author reproduction supports deterministic repeatability only. Structured records improving real audits remains Hypothetical,with no observed effectiveness comparison. [TOG1-X3-H01]
