# Master Report — Unconditional Love and Conditional Love: one-page plan

**Status:** Proposed plan only (2026-10-23; updated 2026-10-24 for Baseline v2 — framework labels, GAIA motherthread, claim IDs). The Master Report itself has **not** been written; it is scheduled after Cycle 4 (×12) verification. Nothing here is a finding.

## Purpose
Investigate what "unconditional love" and "conditional love" mean, how they differ, and what the evidence and traditions say about each — using the **Love** lens of the four-axis grid (README §9.4) across all five Being domains and Earth, while keeping empirical, philosophical, theological and speculative claims separate.

## Working definitions to be tested (not assumed)
- *Conditional love*: care, regard or attachment whose continuation depends on conditions (behaviour, reciprocity, status).
- *Unconditional love*: care or regard not contingent on such conditions (e.g. unconditional positive regard in psychotherapy; agapē in Christian theology; mettā/karuṇā in Buddhism; bhakti and related concepts in Hindu traditions).
Whether "unconditional" is psychologically achievable, normatively desirable without limits, or compatible with boundaries/justice is an **open question**, not a premise.

## Labels
Every Master Report conclusion will carry one of the framework's labels — Established / Plausible / Philosophical/Spiritual / Hypothetical — as defined in `Cycles/Cycle-0-Baseline/FRAMEWORK.md`. Theological meanings of love (agapē, mettā, bhakti, ḥesed) are Established only as descriptions of texts; their truth is Philosophical/Spiritual.

## Method (to run after Cycle 4)
1. Build a claim ledger seeded from Love-relevant claims in Cycles 0–4 plus a dedicated research pass (primary studies, systematic reviews, primary religious texts in reputable translations).
2. Competing accounts: love as emotion, attitude, virtue, relationship, divine attribute; critiques of "unconditional" (boundaries, abuse risk, justice).
3. Simulation only if a defensible model exists (candidate: game-theoretic contingent vs unconditional cooperation strategies — labelled as a model of *strategies*, not of love). Otherwise "Not Executed — model specification incomplete".
4. Verification with Pass/Fail/Indeterminate; ethics review on dignity, consent and non-harm (e.g. "unconditional" must never be used to justify tolerating abuse).

## Falsification / revision criteria
Reject or revise a claimed relationship if its specific evidence fails replication or if traditions' own texts contradict the attributed meaning.
