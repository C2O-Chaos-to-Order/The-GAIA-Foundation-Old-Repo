# Foundational Seven and Transformative Intelligence — Independent Specialist Review Package

**Prepared:** 2026-10-23  
**Status:** REVIEW PACKAGE PREPARED; NO EXTERNAL REVIEW CLAIMED  
**Purpose:** Enable qualified independent reviewers to evaluate bounded questions without treating internal synthesis as peer review.

## 1. Scope and integrity rules

This package requests critical evaluation of the seven foundational reports and Transformative Intelligence (TI) architecture. It is not a request for endorsement. Reviewers should be free to reject claims, propose alternative explanations, or classify a question as underdetermined.

The project principles are:

**Integrity before Intelligence → Trust → Honesty → Truth → Understanding → Wisdom → Intelligence.**

The review must preserve the distinctions between:
- empirical observation and interpretation;
- model output and world-level evidence;
- conceptual analogy and physical mechanism;
- neural correlate and theological/metaphysical conclusion;
- operational capacities and phenomenal experience;
- ordinary coupling, quantum-like formalism, and physical quantum entanglement;
- test design, execution, independent reproduction, and external review.

## 8. Reviewer report template

For every material finding, record:
- finding ID and file/section/line;
- exact claim or definition under review;
- source and source type;
- reviewer discipline and relevant conflicts of interest;
- assessment: supported / supported with boundary / contradicted / not verifiable / interpretive / out of scope;
- reasoning and strongest alternative explanation;
- recommended wording or model change;
- reproducibility artifacts and test result, if applicable;
- remaining uncertainty and conditions for reassessment.
