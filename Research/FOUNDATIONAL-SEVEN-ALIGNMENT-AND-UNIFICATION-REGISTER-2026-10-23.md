## 1. Purpose and governing rule

This register defines the current shared vocabulary, interfaces, dependency rules, and unresolved tensions for the seven foundational reports and the Transformative Intelligence (TI) five-phase meta-architecture. It is an integration baseline, not a claim that all scientific, historical, philosophical, theological, or computational claims have been validated.

The governing sequence is:

**Integrity before Intelligence → Trust → Honesty → Truth → Understanding → Wisdom → Intelligence.**

The research lifecycle is:

**Define → Research → Model → Simulate → Verify → Falsify → Stress Test → Peer Review → Risk Assess → Mitigate → Document → Repeat.**

The TI five-phase cycle is **Divergence → Insurgence → Allegiance → Convergence → Ascendence**. Treat the phases as recurring governance functions, not an empirically established natural law or a mandatory one-way pipeline. Meta-wisdom is a cross-cutting review/invariant obligation, not an unaccountable sixth phase.

## 4. Mandatory category boundaries

All reports and TI revisions must preserve these distinctions:

1. **Observation ≠ interpretation ≠ speculation.** Label the evidential status at the claim level.
2. **Correlation ≠ causation.** Mechanistic claims require designs and evidence that can distinguish alternatives.
3. **Model result ≠ world-level proof.** State model scope, assumptions, parameter provenance, and external-validity limits.
4. **Simulation design ≠ execution.** Execution requires code/model version, data, parameters, seeds where applicable, outputs, failure logs, and reproduction instructions.
5. **Repeated synthesis of the same source ≠ independent confirmation.**
6. **Neural correlate ≠ theological verdict.**
7. **Metaphor or structural analogy ≠ physical mechanism or ontological identity.**
8. **Quantum-like formalism ≠ physical quantum entanglement.** Physical entanglement claims require appropriate physical-system definitions and evidence.
9. **Operational capacity ≠ established phenomenal consciousness.**
10. **Earth-system feedback ≠ proof of a single planetary controller or phenomenal planetary consciousness.**
11. **A proposed invariant ≠ a mathematically proven invariant.** Define state space, transition rules, invariant property, assumptions, and proof/test obligation.
12. **A source citation ≠ claim verification.** Confirm that the cited source supports the exact scope and strength of the claim.

## 5. TI five-phase governance interface

| Phase | Governance function | Minimum record | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Divergence | Generate alternatives without hiding material perspectives | Claim type, provenance, uncertainty, rival models | Relevant alternatives excluded without reason |
| Insurgence | Challenge claims and architecture under predeclared criteria | Counterevidence, falsifier, adversarial test, dissent | Criteria shift after results to protect a preferred claim |
| Allegiance | Adopt provisional commitments that remain revisable | Owner, scope, confidence, review date, anomaly trigger | Provisional commitment silently becomes unquestionable |
| Convergence | Integrate compatible results while preserving distinctions | Dependency links, contradiction/loss ledger, synthesis rationale | Synthesis implies equivalence while concealing material differences |
| Ascendence | Audit and revise the governance process itself | Meta-review, first-order evidence link, re-entry conditions | Meta-level claims cannot be challenged by evidence or appeal |

**Meta-wisdom control:** check provenance integrity, evidence integrity, cross-domain plural coherence, loss/dissent preservation, and non-destructive revisability. These are governance requirements under development, not proven universal laws.
