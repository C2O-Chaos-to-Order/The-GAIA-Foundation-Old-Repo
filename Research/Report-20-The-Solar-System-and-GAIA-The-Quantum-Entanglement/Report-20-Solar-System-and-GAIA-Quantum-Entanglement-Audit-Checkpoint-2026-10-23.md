### Heliopheric current sheet: NASA primary-source check

NASA's “The Heliospheric Current Sheet” page describes a warped surface where the Sun's magnetic polarity changes, extending through the heliosphere. It gives an approximate current density of (10^{-10},mathrm{A/m^2}) and a thickness of about 10,000 km near Earth's orbit; it does not state that a verified integrated current of three billion amperes has been measured. NASA also describes how Earth's passage through sectors and the orientation of the interplanetary magnetic field affect geomagnetic-storm conditions.

Source: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/heliospheric-current-sheet/  
Related NASA explanation: https://www.nasa.gov/science-research/heliophysics/the-suns-magnetic-field-is-about-to-flip/

**Status: General HCS description supported; “approximately three billion amperes” unresolved.** Do not promote current density, integrated current, and total current carried by an extended structure as interchangeable quantities. The report must supply a derivation or a source that explicitly defines and supports the integrated-current figure.

The HCS is a major large-scale heliospheric current-sheet structure, but calling it the “largest coherent electromagnetic structure in the Solar System” needs a precise definition of “largest” and “coherent” and a source supporting that superlative.

### Human magnetoreception — Caltech/eNeuro primary study

Wang et al., “Transduction of the Geomagnetic Field as Evidenced from Alpha-band Activity in the Human Brain,” *eNeuro* (2019): https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0483-18.2019

**Status: Primary source identity and abstract-level finding confirmed.** The study reports repeatable EEG alpha-band responses to specific rotations of Earth-strength magnetic fields under controlled conditions. It states that ferromagnetism remained a viable transduction mechanism; it does not establish that human magnetite crystals were conclusively demonstrated as the causal receptor, and it does not establish that human physiology or decision-making is broadly controlled by solar activity.

**Scope limitation:** an EEG response to laboratory magnetic-field rotations is evidence relevant to possible human magnetosensory processing. It is not evidence of quantum entanglement between humans, Earth, and the Sun.

## 3. Main technical findings and corrections required

1. **Use “electromagnetic/dynamical coupling” for the established physical relationship.** Solar wind, interplanetary magnetic fields, planetary magnetospheres, and space-weather responses are coupled through known plasma and electromagnetic processes. This does not require quantum entanglement.
2. **Avoid overclaiming reciprocal planetary feedback.** Planetary magnetospheres respond to solar-wind conditions. The assertion that planetary responses feed back materially into the heliospheric current system needs a specific mechanism and evidence; do not infer symmetric two-way coupling merely because the systems interact.
3. **Distinguish forcing from regulation.** Solar activity is an external forcing on the Earth system. This does not make the Solar System a Gaia-like regulator, nor establish that the Sun or heliosphere actively regulates Earth's biosphere in the sense used in the report.
4. **Do not conflate electromagnetic coupling with “non-separability” without qualification.** In a systems description, subsystems may be dynamically coupled while remaining separately modeled. If “non-separability” is used, state whether it means causal coupling, inability to specify a model without boundary conditions, information-theoretic dependence, or quantum entanglement.
5. **Quantum claims require quantum-specific evidence.** A planetary geometry, a sinusoidal correlation, an action-scale parameter, or ordinary electromagnetic interaction does not establish a single entangled quantum state. The S03–S05 proposals remain unverified and speculative pending exact source recovery and technical audit.
6. **The magnetosphere “coherence architecture” is not established physics on current evidence.** Keep it clearly separated from the conventional account of solar wind–magnetosphere interaction and standard magnetohydrodynamics.
7. **The “spirit / subconscious / body” Solar System table is interpretive architecture.** The heliospheric magnetic field, solar-cycle variability, and heliosphere/planets/plasma can be assigned these symbolic roles within the user's framework, but the mappings are not scientific classifications or evidence of a literal Solar System subconscious/spirit.
8. **Preserve the title but qualify its use.** “Quantum Entanglement” is the submitted title. A reviewed edition should make clear that the established content concerns electromagnetic/dynamical coupling, while physical quantum entanglement is a separate speculative claim.

## 5. Regression cases

A future reviewed edition must reject these unsupported equivalences:

- Electromagnetic coupling = quantum entanglement.
- HCS current density = total current without a justified integration.
- HCS's large spatial extent = proof that it is the “largest coherent” structure without defined criteria.
- Solar-wind forcing = Gaia-like planetary regulation.
- A laboratory EEG response to geomagnetic-field rotations = proof that solar activity controls human decisions.
- Correlation between geomagnetic indices and health outcomes = causal effect without adequate design.
- Geometric planet pairings = measured quantum-entangled pairs.
- Cosine correlations = Bell inequality violation.
- An action-valued parameter = an entanglement measure by dimensional analysis alone.
- A speculative “coherence architecture” = established magnetospheric mechanism.
- Symbolic Solar System “spirit/subconscious/body” mappings = physical discoveries.

## 6. Disposition

**Report 20: received; targeted primary-source checkpoint documented; full audit OPEN / INDETERMINATE.** The strongest defensible scientific claim is that the heliosphere and planetary environments are electromagnetically and dynamically coupled, with solar-wind and interplanetary-field conditions influencing planetary magnetospheres and terrestrial space weather. This is distinct from physical quantum entanglement and does not extend the Gaia hypothesis to the Solar System.

The exact sources for S01–S05 and several strong human-health claims remain unresolved. Their descriptions are not accepted as verified evidence until primary records and exact claim support are established.

No simulation was claimed as run; no report was renumbered; no canonical manuscript was overwritten; no implementation or release was performed.
