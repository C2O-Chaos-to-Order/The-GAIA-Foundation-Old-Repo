## 3. Technical and conceptual findings

1. **Category distinction:** “quantum entanglement” has a precise physical meaning in quantum theory. “Formal,” “structural,” “informational,” and “active-inference” non-separability are not synonyms for physical quantum entanglement. The report’s caution is correct and should be maintained throughout the title, definitions, table, and conclusions.
2. **Hypothesis/entity distinction:** the Terra/GAIA distinction is a useful stipulated vocabulary for this framework. It is not a result that makes a hypothesis and a physical system two quantum subsystems.
3. **Active inference:** a proof-of-concept model can demonstrate behavior under its own assumptions. It cannot, without further evidence, establish that the actual planet literally infers, possesses consciousness, or instantiates quantum entanglement.
4. **Markov blanket:** in active-inference models, this is a statistical/conditional-independence boundary in a specified model. Calling it a “decoherence surface” is metaphorical unless a separate quantum model explicitly defines and validates that correspondence.
5. **Predictions:** ice-core correlations, geomagnetic–biological coupling, and globally synchronized transitions need operational definitions, null models, confound controls, preregistered analysis where appropriate, and independent data. Listing them as possible predictions is not evidence that they have been observed or uniquely predicted by the proposed model.
6. **Architecture table:** “endogenous EM field as consciousness,” “humanity as planetary reflexivity,” and soul/spirit mappings are philosophical or speculative framework assignments, not established scientific equivalences. Preserve them as such and do not let the formal non-separability argument upgrade their evidential status.
7. **Title:** retain the submitted title as source identity, but consider a subtitle such as “Formal and Structural Non-Separability; Quantum-Literal Claims Unestablished” for any future reviewed edition. Do not silently rename the source submission.
